What goes into a grade

Ten parts of daily life, each scored from 0 to 100 against similar places. The overall grade weights them like this:

CostJobsSchoolsSafetyErrandsGettingHealth

Cost of living 16%, Jobs and income 12%, Schools 14%, Safety 12%, Errands and things to do 10%, Getting around 10%, Education levels 6%, Health 10%, Home ownership 6%, Internet access 4%.

Where the numbers come from

Census Bureau surveys for cost, income and commute. Texas Education Agency ratings for schools. Crime reported by local police to the FBI. FEMA for flood and storm risk. NOAA for weather. Overture Maps for shops and parks.

Who lives in a place is shown for information only and never changes a grade.
