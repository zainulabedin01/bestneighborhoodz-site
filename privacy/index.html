Privacy policy

We do not ask for accounts and we do not sell personal information.

What we collect

We use analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. This may set cookies and collect your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. If we show ads, our ad partners may also use cookies to show ads and measure them.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.
