Is Monahans a good place to live? Common questions

Is Monahans, TX a good place to live?

We give Monahans a C-. That places it just below the middle of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 7130th of 11,459 nationally and 266th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Monahans?

Renters in Monahans pay a median of $1,110 a month, 21% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $160,800, 43% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $952, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Monahans?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Monahans are Bellaire, Casa Bonita and Park. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Monahans?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 22% lower than the Texas average. About 1.0% of workers work from home.
