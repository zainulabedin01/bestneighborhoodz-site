What it's like to live in Uhland

Uhland sits about 23 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 1,958 people lived here in 2025, up 24.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2007).

The median age is 33.3, 48% of households have children, 92% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $92,238 a year (18% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Uhland

  • Growing fast. The population grew 25% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Uhland

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Uhland

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Uhland is $1,583 a month, 13% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $63,000 a year. Right now 33% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $279,900, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,532 before tax, or $1,874 with property tax. That is $291 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Uhland pays $4,097 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
