    Living in Denison: common questions

    Is Denison, TX a good place to live?

    Denison earns a grade of C- on our index, 120th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on cost of living and getting around, and trails on health and education levels.

    How much does it cost to live in Denison?

    Median rent in Denison is $1,309 a month, 7% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $180,900, 36% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,190, including property tax.

    Is Denison safe?

    In 2025, Denison Police Department reported 226 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 42% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 746 per 100,000, 64% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Denison?

    We found 9 rated public school campuses inside Denison. 22% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 25 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Denison?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Denison are Central Denison, Southeast Denison and South Denison. We score 4 areas in the city.

    Does Denison flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Denison as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Denison?

    Summers in Denison peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 73 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 44 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Denison?

    The average one-way commute is 19 min, 27% lower than the Texas average. About 9.7% of workers work from home. It is about 10 miles from downtown Sherman.
