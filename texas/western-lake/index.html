What it's like to live in Western Lake

Western Lake sits about 62 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. About 1,165 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 43.6, 18% of households have children, 100% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $34,388 a year (56% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Western Lake

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state.
