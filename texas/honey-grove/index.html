Living in Honey Grove: common questions

Is Honey Grove, TX a good place to live?

We give Honey Grove a B-. It ranks 289th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and cost of living, and trails on internet access and health.

How much does it cost to live in Honey Grove?

Renters in Honey Grove pay a median of $581 a month, 59% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $152,000, 46% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $992, including property tax.

Is Honey Grove safe?

In 2025, Honey Grove Police Department reported 110 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 72% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 494 per 100,000, 76% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Honey Grove?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Honey Grove. 67% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 83 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Honey Grove flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Honey Grove as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Honey Grove?

Summers in Honey Grove peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 33°F. Expect around 78 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 46 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Honey Grove?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 7.3% of workers work from home. It is about 16 miles from downtown Bonham.
