What it's like to live in Fairview

Fairview sits about 26 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 10,913 people lived here in 2025, up 5.2% from 2020. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2006).

Local spots people know include Cypress Park, The Meadows Park and The Village Park.

The median age is 49.1, 25% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $109,347 a year (39% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Fairview

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 72 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 81% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $109,347, 39% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Fairview

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,947, 39% higher than the Texas median.
  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 5.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 34 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Fairview

Fairview renters pay a median of $1,947 a month, 39% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $78,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 66% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $597,300, 110% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $3,269 before tax, or $3,966 with property tax. That is $2,019 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Fairview pays $8,355 a year, 97% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
