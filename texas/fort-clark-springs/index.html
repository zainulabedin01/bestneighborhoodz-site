What it's like to live in Fort Clark Springs

Places locals use include Parade Ground, Rendesvouz Park and Mascot Dog Park.

Cost of living in Fort Clark Springs

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $858 for a one-bedroom, $1,078 for a two-bedroom and $1,393 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $67,000, 76% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $367 before tax, or $460 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Fort Clark Springs pays $1,118 a year, 74% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
