What it's like to live in Pineview Terrace

Pineview Terrace is a neighborhood in Katy with about 2,500 residents, homes built around 1990, a mix of owners and renters and 65% of households with children.

It grades D+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 8th of 8 areas in Katy, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in Pineview Terrace

  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Pineview Terrace

  • Higher poverty. 28% of residents live below the poverty line, against 8.1% across Katy.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 37 minutes, against 31 for Katy as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.
