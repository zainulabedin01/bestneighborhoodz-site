    Living in Katy: common questions

    Is Katy, TX a good place to live?

    Katy gets an overall grade of B- and ranks 82nd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on schools and home ownership, and trails on errands and things to do and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Katy?

    Renters in Katy pay a median of $1,602 a month, 14% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $401,700, 42% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,799, including property tax.

    Is Katy safe?

    In 2025, Katy Police Department reported 123 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 68% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,967 per 100,000, 42% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Katy?

    We found 12 rated public school campuses inside Katy. 75% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 72 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Katy?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Katy are Falls at Green Meadows, Pin Oak Village and Heritage Park West. We score 8 areas in the city.

    Does Katy flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Katy as very high, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, wildfires and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Katy?

    Summers in Katy peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 45°F. Expect around 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 50 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Katy?

    The average one-way commute is 31 min, 17% higher than the Texas average. About 18% of workers work from home. It is about 27 miles from downtown Houston.
