How much does it cost to live in Ennis?

Renters in Ennis pay a median of $1,362 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $260,100, 8% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,762, including property tax.
