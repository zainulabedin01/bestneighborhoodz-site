What it's like to live in Monterra

Monterra is a neighborhood in Fate with about 3,800 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2017), mostly owner-occupied (100%) and 65% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 7 areas in Fate, which grades B overall.

Pros of living in Monterra

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $195,300, 47% higher than the Fate median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 2 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.5 miles away.

Cons of living in Monterra
