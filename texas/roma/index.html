What it's like to live in Roma

Roma is a small city in Starr County. 11,543 people lived here in 2025, up 0.2% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Roma Municipal Park, Roma Community Park and Bicentennial Plaza.

The median age is 28.0, 46% of households have children, 55% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $24,579 a year (69% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Roma

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 197 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 49% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $563, 60% lower than the Texas median.
  • Plenty close to home. 5 grocery stores, 56 restaurants and 5 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Roma

  • Higher unemployment. 12% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Higher poverty. 37% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 45% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 195 days a year at 90°F or hotter.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 68% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Roma

Roma renters pay a median of $563 a month, 60% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $23,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 76% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $775 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $94,400, 67% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $517 before tax, or $618 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Roma pays $1,215 a year, 71% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Roma safe?

In 2025, Roma Police Department reported 23 violent crimes and 106 property crimes. That works out to 197 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 49% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 909 property crimes per 100,000, 56% lower than the state rate.

Schools in Roma

Roma has 3 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Roma ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Florence J Scott Elementary and Roel A & Celia R Saenz Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 92 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.
