Is Roma a good place to live? Common questions

Is Roma, TX a good place to live?

Roma earns an F on our index, in the bottom 8% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 3276th of 3,321 nationally and 190th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are getting around and cost of living, and its weakest is internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Roma?

Renters in Roma pay a median of $563 a month, 60% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $94,400, 67% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $618, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Roma?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Roma are Campobello Number 2 Colonia, Ala Blanca Colonia and Mirasoles Colonia. We score 6 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Roma?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 17% of workers work from home.
