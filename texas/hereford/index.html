What it's like to live in Hereford

Hereford is a small city in Deaf Smith County. 14,976 people lived here in 2025, up 0.1% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Veterans Park, Dameron Park and Aquatic Park.

The median age is 29.4, 44% of households have children, 66% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $56,868 a year (28% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Hereford

  • Low violent crime. 67 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 83% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $953, 32% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.9 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 16 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Hereford

  • Many adults uninsured. 35% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.

Cost of living in Hereford

Hereford renters pay a median of $953 a month, 32% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $38,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 38% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $885 for a one-bedroom, $1,112 for a two-bedroom and $1,369 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $109,700, 61% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $600 before tax, or $750 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Hereford pays $1,797 a year, 58% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
