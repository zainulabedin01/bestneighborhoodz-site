What it's like to live in The Colony

The Colony sits about 22 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 46,196 people lived here in 2025, up 3.7% from 2020. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2001).

Places locals use include Hidden Cove Park, Hidden Cove Park and Marina and Bill Allen Memorial Park.

The median age is 37.8, 28% of households have children, 60% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $114,511 a year (46% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in The Colony

  • Low violent crime. 136 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 65% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $114,511, 46% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in The Colony

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,958, 40% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in The Colony

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in The Colony is $1,958 a month, 40% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $78,000 a year. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $383,900, 35% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,101 before tax, or $2,647 with property tax. That is $689 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in The Colony pays $6,551 a year, 55% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is The Colony safe?

In 2025, The Colony Police Department reported 63 violent crimes and 600 property crimes. That works out to 136 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 65% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,299 property crimes per 100,000, 38% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 68% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in The Colony

The Colony has 10 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 30% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Little Elm ISD and Lewisville ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Prestwick Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 77 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
