Is The Colony a good place to live? Common questions

Is The Colony, TX a good place to live?

We give The Colony a B+. That places it in the top 28% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 877th of 3,321 nationally and 31st of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in The Colony?

Median rent in The Colony is $1,958 a month, 40% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $383,900, 35% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,647, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in The Colony?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in The Colony are Legend Crest, Stewart Peninsula and Legend Trails. We score 8 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in The Colony?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home.
