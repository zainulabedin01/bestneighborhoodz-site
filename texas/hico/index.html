What it's like to live in Hico

Hico is a town in Hamilton County. 1,496 people lived here in 2025, up 12.7% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

The median age is 35.6, 28% of households have children, 77% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $67,500 a year (14% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Hico

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $767, 45% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Hico

  • Spotty home internet. Only 74% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Hico

Hico renters pay a median of $767 a month, 45% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $31,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 73% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $775 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $176,700, 38% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $967 before tax, or $1,176 with property tax. That is $409 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Hico pays $2,503 a year, 41% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
