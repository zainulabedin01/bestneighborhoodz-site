What it's like to live in Bellville

Bellville sits about 53 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 4,379 people lived here in 2025, up 3.9% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Clark Park, Margaret Chesley Memorial Park and Josephine Zeiske Memorial Park.

The median age is 40.1, 33% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $59,583 a year (24% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Bellville

  • Low violent crime. 209 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 46% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,134, 19% lower than the Texas median.
  • Plenty close to home. 2 grocery stores, 31 restaurants and 8 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Bellville

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 4.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 72% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Bellville

Bellville renters pay a median of $1,134 a month, 19% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $45,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 44% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $817 for a one-bedroom, $1,069 for a two-bedroom and $1,480 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $284,400, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,557 before tax, or $1,897 with property tax. That is $763 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Bellville pays $4,081 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Bellville safe?

In 2025, Bellville Police Department reported 9 violent crimes and 33 property crimes. That works out to 209 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 46% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 766 property crimes per 100,000, 63% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Daily life in Bellville

Everyday places mapped inside Bellville, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Clark Park, Margaret Chesley Memorial Park, Josephine Zeiske Memorial Park.

The average one-way commute is 24 minutes, 12% shorter than the Texas average. 4% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Houston is about 53 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Bellville work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $59,583, income per person is $30,314, unemployment is 0.5% and 31% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
