What it's like to live in Lamar

Lamar sits about 37 miles from downtown Corpus Christi, in the Corpus Christi metro. About 1,202 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 62.7, 8% of households have children, 96% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $86,851 a year (11% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Lamar

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,073 for a one-bedroom, $1,404 for a two-bedroom and $1,944 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $318,000, 12% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,741 before tax, or $1,976 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Lamar pays $2,825 a year, 33% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
