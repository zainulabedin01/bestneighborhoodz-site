What it's like to live in Childress

Childress is a town in Childress County. 5,650 people lived here in 2025, down 1.4% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1961).

Local spots people know include Fair Park Disc Golf Station, Fair Park Lake Childress Tx and Childress Public Library.

The median age is 35.6, 38% of households have children, 65% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $57,644 a year (27% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Childress

  • Low violent crime. 88 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 77% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $910, 35% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Childress

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Childress

Childress renters pay a median of $910 a month, 35% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $36,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 53% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $862 for a one-bedroom, $1,128 for a two-bedroom and $1,500 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $85,900, 70% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $470 before tax, or $581 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Childress pays $1,330 a year, 69% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
