What it's like to live in Florence

Florence sits about 38 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 1,245 people lived here in 2025, up 5.2% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Florence Community Park, Florence City Park and Florence Public Library.

The median age is 34.7, 33% of households have children, 77% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $78,750 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Florence

  • Low violent crime. 82 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 79% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,018, 27% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Florence

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 37 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Florence

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Florence is $1,018 a month, 27% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $41,000 a year. Right now 65% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $239,100, 16% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,309 before tax, or $1,544 with property tax. That is $526 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Florence pays $2,819 a year, 33% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
