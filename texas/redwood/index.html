Living in Redwood: common questions

Is Redwood, TX a good place to live?

Redwood gets an overall grade of C and ranks 434th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and jobs and income, and trails on education levels and errands and things to do. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Redwood?

Median rent in Redwood is $1,302 a month, 7% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $109,500, 61% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $708, including property tax.

Does Redwood flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Redwood as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Redwood?

Summers in Redwood peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 39°F. Expect around 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 37 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Redwood?

The average one-way commute is 31 min, 16% higher than the Texas average. About 2.5% of workers work from home. It is about 44 miles from downtown San Antonio.
