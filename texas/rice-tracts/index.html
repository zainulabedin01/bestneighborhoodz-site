Living in Rice Tracts: common questions

Is Rice Tracts, TX a good place to live?

Rice Tracts gets an overall grade of A- and ranks 136th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and jobs and income, and trails on errands and things to do and health. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Rice Tracts?

The median home is worth $241,000, 15% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,581, including property tax.

Does Rice Tracts flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Rice Tracts as low, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Rice Tracts?

Summers in Rice Tracts peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 51°F. Expect around 151 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 28 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Rice Tracts?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 7.4% of workers work from home. It is about 11 miles from downtown Brownsville.
