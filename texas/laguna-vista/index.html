Living in Laguna Vista: common questions

Is Laguna Vista, TX a good place to live?

Laguna Vista earns a grade of B+ on our index, 151st of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on safety and schools, and trails on getting around and errands and things to do. It is a strong pick for families and retirees.

How much does it cost to live in Laguna Vista?

The median home is worth $255,900, 10% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,691, including property tax.

Is Laguna Vista safe?

In 2025, Laguna Vista Police Department reported 26 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 93% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 311 per 100,000, 85% lower than the Texas rate.

Does Laguna Vista flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Laguna Vista as moderate, and coastal flooding as very high. Hazards rated high or very high here: coastal flooding, hurricanes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Laguna Vista?

Summers in Laguna Vista peak at about 91°F in July, and January nights fall to about 52°F. Expect around 95 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 29 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Laguna Vista?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 11% lower than the Texas average. About 19% of workers work from home. It is about 13 miles from downtown Brownsville.
