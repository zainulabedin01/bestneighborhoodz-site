Is Barton Creek a good place to live? Common questions

Is Barton Creek, TX a good place to live?

We give Barton Creek an A. That places it in the top 12% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 721st of 11,459 nationally and 14th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are education levels and jobs and income, and its weakest is cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Barton Creek?

Renters in Barton Creek pay a median of $2,268 a month, 62% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $2,000,000+, 605% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 9.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $11,781, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Barton Creek?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 19% lower than the Texas average. About 48% of workers work from home.
