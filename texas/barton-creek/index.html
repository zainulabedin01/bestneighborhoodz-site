What it's like to live in Barton Creek

Barton Creek sits about 7 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 3,440 people live here. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2005).

The median age is 59.0, 20% of households have children, 79% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $203,947 a year (160% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Barton Creek

  • High incomes. The median household earns $203,947, 160% higher than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Healthier population. Only 5.8% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Barton Creek

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $2,268, 62% higher than the Texas median.
  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 9.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 123 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Barton Creek

Barton Creek renters pay a median of $2,268 a month, 62% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $91,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $2,000,000+, 605% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $10,947 before tax, or $11,781 with property tax. That is $9,513 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Barton Creek pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
