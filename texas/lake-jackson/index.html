    Living in Lake Jackson: common questions

    Is Lake Jackson, TX a good place to live?

    We give Lake Jackson a B-. It ranks 75th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on schools and cost of living, and trails on health and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Lake Jackson?

    Renters in Lake Jackson pay a median of $1,478 a month, 5% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $250,100, 12% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,686, including property tax.

    Is Lake Jackson safe?

    In 2025, Lake Jackson Police Department reported 309 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 20% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,413 per 100,000, 32% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Lake Jackson?

    We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Lake Jackson. 80% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 76 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Lake Jackson?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Lake Jackson are North Lake Jackson, Woodshore and Central Lake Jackson. We score 5 areas in the city.

    Does Lake Jackson flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Lake Jackson as moderate, and coastal flooding as very high. Hazards rated high or very high here: coastal flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Lake Jackson?

    Summers in Lake Jackson peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 45°F. Expect around 94 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 46 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Lake Jackson?

    The average one-way commute is 21 min, 22% lower than the Texas average. About 5.6% of workers work from home. It is about 51 miles from downtown Houston.
