What it's like to live in Sundown

Sundown sits about 35 miles from downtown Lubbock, in the Lubbock metro. 1,317 people lived here in 2025, up 2.3% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Park Street Park and Sunset Park.

The median age is 37.0, 40% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $58,594 a year (25% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Sundown

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $675, 52% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.6 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Sundown

  • Higher unemployment. 14% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.

Cost of living in Sundown

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Sundown is $675 a month, 52% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $27,000 a year. Right now 38% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $868 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $96,400, 66% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $528 before tax, or $630 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Sundown pays $1,228 a year, 71% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
