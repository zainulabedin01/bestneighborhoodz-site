What it's like to live in Central Rockwall

Central Rockwall is an area in Rockwall with about 3,000 residents, homes built around 1995, mostly owner-occupied (85%) and 26% of households with children.

Our grade for Central Rockwall is A. That puts it 2nd of 18 areas we grade in Rockwall, a city that gets an A overall.

Pros of living in Central Rockwall

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,450, 26% lower than the Rockwall median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Central Rockwall

  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 38 minutes, against 32 for Rockwall as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Central Rockwall

Is Central Rockwall a good place to live?

Central Rockwall covers part of Rockwall, TX, home to about 3,000 people. It gets an A, in the top 12% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 2nd of 18 areas we score in Rockwall. It does best on jobs and income and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Central Rockwall?

Renters in Central Rockwall pay a median of $1,450 a month, 26% lower than the Rockwall median. The median home is worth $449,000, 9% higher than the Rockwall median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Rockwall.

What schools serve Central Rockwall?

The closest rated public schools are Virginia Reinhardt Elementary (elementary, rated B), Herman E Utley Middle (middle, rated B) and Rockwall High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
