    Living in Rockwall: common questions

    Is Rockwall, TX a good place to live?

    Rockwall earns a grade of A on our index, 12th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on jobs and income and internet access, and trails on getting around and cost of living. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Rockwall?

    Renters in Rockwall pay a median of $1,947 a month, 39% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $411,700, 45% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,756, including property tax.

    Is Rockwall safe?

    In 2025, Rockwall Police Department reported 111 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 71% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,014 per 100,000, 51% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Rockwall?

    We found 13 rated public school campuses inside Rockwall. 77% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 75 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Rockwall?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Rockwall are Hidden Valley Estates, Central Rockwall and Caruth Lakes. We score 18 areas in the city.

    Does Rockwall flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Rockwall as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Rockwall?

    Summers in Rockwall peak at about 92°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 95 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 40 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Rockwall?

    The average one-way commute is 32 min, 19% higher than the Texas average. About 19% of workers work from home. It is about 21 miles from downtown Dallas.
