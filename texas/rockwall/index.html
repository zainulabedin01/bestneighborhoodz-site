Is Rockwall a good place to live? Common questions

Is Rockwall, TX a good place to live?

Rockwall earns a B+ on our index, in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 177th of 876 nationally and 14th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Rockwall?

Renters in Rockwall pay a median of $1,947 a month, 39% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $411,700, 45% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,756, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Rockwall?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Rockwall are Hidden Valley Estates, Northwest Rockwall and East Rockwall. We score 18 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Rockwall?

The average one-way commute is 32 min, 19% higher than the Texas average. About 19% of workers work from home.
