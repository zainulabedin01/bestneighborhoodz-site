What it's like to live in Abernathy

Abernathy is a town in Hale County. 2,826 people lived here in 2025, down 1.6% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include North Abernathy Park and Abernathy Community Park.

The median age is 34.8, 50% of households have children, 77% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $87,531 a year (12% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Abernathy

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Abernathy is $868 a month, 38% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $35,000 a year. Right now 29% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $931 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $176,400, 38% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $966 before tax, or $1,175 with property tax. That is $307 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Abernathy pays $2,515 a year, 41% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
