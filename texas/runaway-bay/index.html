What it's like to live in Runaway Bay

Runaway Bay sits about 69 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 2,212 people lived here in 2025, up 43.4% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Runaway Bay Public Park and Cricket Ridge Park.

The median age is 54.3, 18% of households have children, 85% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $96,875 a year (23% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Runaway Bay

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Runaway Bay is $1,227 a month, 13% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $49,000 a year. Right now 55% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $892 for a one-bedroom, $1,164 for a two-bedroom and $1,612 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $307,600, 8% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,684 before tax, or $2,028 with property tax. That is $801 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Runaway Bay pays $4,135 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
