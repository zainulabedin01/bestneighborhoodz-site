What it's like to live in Mart

Mart sits about 21 miles from downtown Waco, in the Waco metro. 1,847 people lived here in 2025, up 3.7% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1960).

Places locals use include Rucker Park and Nancy Nail Memorial Library.

The median age is 38.1, 37% of households have children, 64% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $62,917 a year (20% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Mart

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $895, 36% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Mart

  • Higher unemployment. 8.4% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Mart

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Mart is $895 a month, 36% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $36,000 a year. Right now 57% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,029 for a one-bedroom, $1,297 for a two-bedroom and $1,693 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $116,100, 59% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $635 before tax, or $799 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Mart pays $1,959 a year, 54% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
