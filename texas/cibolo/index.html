    Living in Cibolo: common questions

    Is Cibolo, TX a good place to live?

    Cibolo earns a grade of B+ on our index, 55th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on safety and home ownership, and trails on getting around and errands and things to do.

    How much does it cost to live in Cibolo?

    Median rent in Cibolo is $2,112 a month, 51% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $351,900, 24% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,411, including property tax.

    Is Cibolo safe?

    In 2025, Cibolo Police Department reported 151 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 61% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 546 per 100,000, 74% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Cibolo?

    We found 8 rated public school campuses inside Cibolo. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 49 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Cibolo?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Cibolo are Northwest Cibolo, North Cibolo and Central Cibolo. We score 4 areas in the city.

    Does Cibolo flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Cibolo as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Cibolo?

    Summers in Cibolo peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 37°F. Expect around 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 34 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Cibolo?

    The average one-way commute is 29 min, 9% higher than the Texas average. About 16% of workers work from home. It is about 20 miles from downtown San Antonio.
