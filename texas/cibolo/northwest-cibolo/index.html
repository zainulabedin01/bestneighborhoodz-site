Questions about Northwest Cibolo

Is Northwest Cibolo a good place to live?

Northwest Cibolo covers part of Cibolo, TX, home to about 18,400 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 1st of 4 areas we score in Cibolo. It does best on internet access and worst on errands and things to do.

How much does it cost to live in Northwest Cibolo?

Renters in Northwest Cibolo pay a median of $2,140 a month, about the same as the Cibolo median. The median home is worth $372,000, 6% higher than the Cibolo median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 2.9x across Cibolo.

What schools serve Northwest Cibolo?

The closest rated public schools are John A Sippel Elementary (elementary, rated B), Elaine S Schlather Intermediate (middle, rated B) and Byron P Steele II High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
