What is the safest city in Texas?

On our ranking, the top three are Fair Oaks Ranch (16 violent per 100k), Trophy Club (29 violent per 100k), Colleyville (35 violent per 100k). Sorted by violent crimes per 100,000 people, then property crime. Only places whose police reported a full year are included.

How is this list made?

Sorted by violent crimes per 100,000 people, then property crime. Only places whose police reported a full year are included. Every number comes from public data: the US Census Bureau, the Texas Education Agency, the FBI, the CDC and others. We do not take payment to include or rank any place.
