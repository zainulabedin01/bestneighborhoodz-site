Living in Lockhart: common questions

Is Lockhart, TX a good place to live?

Lockhart gets an overall grade of C- and ranks 129th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on safety and cost of living, and trails on errands and things to do and getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Lockhart?

Median rent in Lockhart is $1,152 a month, 18% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $251,600, 11% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,712, including property tax.

Is Lockhart safe?

In 2025, Lockhart Police Department reported 67 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 83% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 802 per 100,000, 62% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Lockhart?

We found 7 rated public school campuses inside Lockhart. 29% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 20 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

Does Lockhart flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Lockhart as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Lockhart?

Summers in Lockhart peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 118 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 35 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Lockhart?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home. It is about 29 miles from downtown Austin.
