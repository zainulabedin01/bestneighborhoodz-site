Is Wichita Falls, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Wichita Falls?

Median rent in Wichita Falls is $1,022 a month, 27% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $156,800, 45% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,078, including property tax.
