Is Cuero a good place to live? Common questions

Is Cuero, TX a good place to live?

Cuero earns a D on our index, in the bottom 24% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 9542nd of 11,459 nationally and 505th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are getting around and cost of living, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Cuero?

Median rent in Cuero is $903 a month, 36% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $176,900, 38% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,083, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Cuero?

The average one-way commute is 18 min, 34% lower than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
