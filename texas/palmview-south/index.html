Living in Palmview South: common questions

Is Palmview South, TX a good place to live?

We give Palmview South a B-. It ranks 294th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on getting around and cost of living, and trails on jobs and income and internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Palmview South?

The median home is worth $82,400, 71% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $569, including property tax.

Does Palmview South flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Palmview South as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Palmview South?

Summers in Palmview South peak at about 99°F in July, and January nights fall to about 53°F. Expect around 177 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 22 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Palmview South?

The average one-way commute is 18 min, 34% lower than the Texas average. About 10.0% of workers work from home. It is about 8 miles from downtown McAllen.
