What it's like to live in Horizon Lake

Horizon Lake is a neighborhood in Leander with about 7,400 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2007), a mix of owners and renters and 43% of households with children.

Our grade for Horizon Lake is B-. That puts it 12th of 12 areas we grade in Leander, a city that gets an A overall.

Questions about Horizon Lake

Is Horizon Lake a good place to live?

Horizon Lake is a neighborhood in Leander, TX, home to about 7,400 people. It gets a B-, in the top 44% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 12th of 12 areas we score in Leander. It does best on internet access and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Horizon Lake?

Renters in Horizon Lake pay a median of $2,010 a month, 4% higher than the Leander median. The median home is worth $405,000, 20% lower than the Leander median. A median home costs 4.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Leander.
