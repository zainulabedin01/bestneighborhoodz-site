What it's like to live in Northcreek Ranch

Northcreek Ranch is a neighborhood in Leander with about 2,300 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2004), a mix of owners and renters and 42% of households with children.

It grades B+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 7th of 12 areas in Leander, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in Northcreek Ranch

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.7 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Northcreek Ranch

  • Higher unemployment. 15% of the labor force is out of work, against 3.4% across Leander.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail, wildfires and winter storms as very high in this part of town.
