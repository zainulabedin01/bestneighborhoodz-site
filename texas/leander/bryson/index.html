What it's like to live in Bryson

Bryson is a neighborhood in Leander with about 4,900 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2019), mostly owner-occupied (75%) and 47% of households with children.

Our grade for Bryson is A-. That puts it 4th of 12 areas we grade in Leander, a city that gets an A overall.

Pros of living in Bryson

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,710, 12% lower than the Leander median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in Bryson

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Bryson

Is Bryson a good place to live?

Bryson is a neighborhood in Leander, TX, home to about 4,900 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 12 areas we score in Leander. It does best on internet access and worst on errands and things to do.

How much does it cost to live in Bryson?

Renters in Bryson pay a median of $1,710 a month, 12% lower than the Leander median. The median home is worth $593,000, 17% higher than the Leander median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Leander.

What schools serve Bryson?

The closest rated public schools are Tarvin Elementary (elementary, rated A), Leander Middle (middle, rated B) and Early College High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
