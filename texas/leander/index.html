Is Leander a good place to live? Common questions

Is Leander, TX a good place to live?

Leander gets an A overall, which puts it in the top 12% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 76th of 876 nationally and 3rd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Leander?

Renters in Leander pay a median of $1,939 a month, 38% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $506,200, 78% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,528, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Leander?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Leander are Grand Mesa at Crystal Falls, Travisso and Bryson. We score 12 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Leander?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 10% higher than the Texas average. About 31% of workers work from home.
