What it's like to live in Las Misiones

Las Misiones is a neighborhood in Laredo with about 2,900 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2008), mostly owner-occupied (85%) and 85% of households with children.

It grades B against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 16th of 61 areas in Laredo, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in Las Misiones

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $162,900, 155% higher than the Laredo median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Las Misiones

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,430, 39% higher than the Laredo median.
  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 2.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Las Misiones

Is Las Misiones a good place to live?
