    Living in Laredo: common questions

    Is Laredo, TX a good place to live?

    Laredo gets an overall grade of B- and ranks 31st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on schools and cost of living, and trails on education levels and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Laredo?

    Renters in Laredo pay a median of $1,030 a month, 27% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $193,500, 32% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,353, including property tax.

    Is Laredo safe?

    In 2025, Laredo Police Department reported 316 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 19% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,157 per 100,000, 45% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Laredo?

    We found 76 rated public school campuses inside Laredo. 95% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 87 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Laredo?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Laredo are Lakeside, Alexander and East Laredo. We score 61 areas in the city.

    Does Laredo flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Laredo as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Laredo?

    Summers in Laredo peak at about 100°F in July, and January nights fall to about 47°F. Expect around 184 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 21 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Laredo?

    The average one-way commute is 22 min, 19% lower than the Texas average. About 5.9% of workers work from home.
