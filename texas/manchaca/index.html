Is Manchaca, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Manchaca?

Median rent in Manchaca is $2,591 a month, 85% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $573,800, 102% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,640, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Manchaca?
