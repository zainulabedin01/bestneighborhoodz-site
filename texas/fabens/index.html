Living in Fabens: common questions

Is Fabens, TX a good place to live?

Fabens earns a grade of C- on our index, 503rd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and errands and things to do, and trails on education levels and health. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Fabens?

Renters in Fabens pay a median of $606 a month, 57% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $95,500, 66% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $637, including property tax.

Are the schools good in Fabens?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Fabens. 33% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 37 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Fabens flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Fabens as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: heat waves, hail and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Fabens?

Summers in Fabens peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 29°F. Expect around 110 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 8 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Fabens?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 12% higher than the Texas average. About 2.9% of workers work from home. It is about 28 miles from downtown El Paso.
