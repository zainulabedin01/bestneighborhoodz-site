What it's like to live in Scenic Oaks

Scenic Oaks sits about 19 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. About 10,569 people live here. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2011).

The median age is 38.6, 39% of households have children, 83% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $174,962 a year (123% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Scenic Oaks

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Scenic Oaks is $1,835 a month, 31% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $73,000 a year. Right now 68% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $563,100, 98% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $3,082 before tax, or $3,809 with property tax. That is $1,974 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Scenic Oaks pays $8,723 a year, 106% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
