What it's like to live in Hale Center

Hale Center is a town in Hale County. 1,979 people lived here in 2025, down 3.9% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Memorial Park, Windmill Park and Freeman Plaza.

The median age is 43.5, 32% of households have children, 84% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $57,083 a year (27% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Hale Center

  • Low violent crime. 101 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 74% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $594, 58% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.3 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 18 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Hale Center

  • Higher unemployment. 8.9% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Many adults uninsured. 31% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 77% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Hale Center

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Hale Center is $594 a month, 58% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $24,000 a year. Right now 60% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $931 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $76,200, 73% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $417 before tax, or $492 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Hale Center pays $903 a year, 79% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
