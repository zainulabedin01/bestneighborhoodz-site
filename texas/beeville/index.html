What it's like to live in Beeville

Beeville is a small city in Bee County. 13,086 people lived here in 2025, down 4.4% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Veterans Memorial Park, Carlos Reyes Park and Poesta Park.

The median age is 31.9, 38% of households have children, 59% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $50,784 a year (35% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Beeville

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,061, 24% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.9 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 5 grocery stores, 66 restaurants and 13 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Beeville

  • Higher poverty. 22% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 28% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 123 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Beeville

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Beeville is $1,061 a month, 24% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $42,000 a year. Right now 58% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $943 for a one-bedroom, $1,233 for a two-bedroom and $1,523 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $94,700, 67% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $518 before tax, or $653 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Beeville pays $1,613 a year, 62% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

In 2025, Beeville Police Department reported 54 violent crimes and 216 property crimes. That works out to 414 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 7% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,654 property crimes per 100,000, 21% lower than the state rate.
