What it's like to live in Aledo

Aledo sits about 49 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 7,122 people lived here in 2025, up 46.5% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2010).

Local spots people know include Park East, Aledo Middle School Baseball Field and Aledo Public Library.

The median age is 34.6, 50% of households have children, 88% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $163,231 a year (108% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Aledo

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $163,231, 108% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 47% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.
  • Healthier population. Only 7.9% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Aledo

Aledo renters pay a median of $2,550 a month, 82% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $102,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 11% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $465,100, 64% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,546 before tax, or $3,280 with property tax. That is $730 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Aledo pays $8,816 a year, 108% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Aledo

Everyday places mapped inside Aledo, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Park East, Aledo Middle School Baseball Field, Aledo Public Library.

The average one-way commute is 29 minutes, 9% longer than the Texas average. 17% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Dallas is about 49 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Aledo work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $163,231, income per person is $57,377, unemployment is 1.5% and 57% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
