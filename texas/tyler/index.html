Is Tyler a good place to live? Common questions

Is Tyler, TX a good place to live?

Tyler earns a C- on our index, just below the middle of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 541st of 876 nationally and 41st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Tyler?

Median rent in Tyler is $1,252 a month, 11% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $235,300, 17% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,517, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Tyler?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Tyler are South Tyler, Southeast Tyler and East Tyler. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Tyler?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 25% lower than the Texas average. About 7.9% of workers work from home.
