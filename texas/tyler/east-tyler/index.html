Questions about East Tyler

Is East Tyler a good place to live?

East Tyler covers part of Tyler, TX, home to about 8,900 people. It gets a B-, in the top 44% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 5th of 11 areas we score in Tyler. It does best on getting around and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in East Tyler?

Median rent in East Tyler is $1,420 a month, 13% higher than the Tyler median. The median home is worth $209,000, 11% lower than the Tyler median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.5x across Tyler.
