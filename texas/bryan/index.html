Is Bryan a good place to live? Common questions

Is Bryan, TX a good place to live?

Bryan gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 766th of 876 nationally and 68th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are getting around and cost of living, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Bryan?

Median rent in Bryan is $1,195 a month, 15% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $227,800, 20% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,537, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Bryan?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Bryan are Northeast Bryan, East Bryan and The Gardens Apartments. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Bryan?

The average one-way commute is 19 min, 30% lower than the Texas average. About 7.6% of workers work from home.
