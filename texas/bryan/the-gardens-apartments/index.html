What it's like to live in The Gardens Apartments

The Gardens Apartments is a neighborhood in Bryan with about 700 residents, older homes (median year built 1962), mostly owner-occupied (66%) and 43% of households with children.

It grades A- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 2nd of 11 areas in Bryan, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in The Gardens Apartments

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $116,200, 96% higher than the Bryan median.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about under 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in The Gardens Apartments

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,640, 37% higher than the Bryan median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes as very high in this part of town.
