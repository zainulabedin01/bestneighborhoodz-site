What it's like to live in Northwest side of West Livingston

Northwest side of West Livingston is an area in West Livingston with about 1,400 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2000), mostly owner-occupied (79%) and 49% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 4 areas in West Livingston, which grades F overall.
