What it's like to live in West Livingston

West Livingston is a town in Polk County. About 8,199 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Lake Livingston State Park, Tigerville Park and Lake Livington State Park.

The median age is 38.7, 37% of households have children, 83% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $61,943 a year (21% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in West Livingston

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,212, 14% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in West Livingston

  • Higher unemployment. 9.1% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 35 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Many adults uninsured. 28% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.

Cost of living in West Livingston

West Livingston renters pay a median of $1,212 a month, 14% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $48,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 41% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $938 for a one-bedroom, $1,227 for a two-bedroom and $1,462 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $153,600, 46% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $841 before tax, or $958 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in West Livingston pays $1,409 a year, 67% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
