What it's like to live in Benbrook

Benbrook sits about 41 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 24,279 people lived here in 2025, down 1.1% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include North Holiday Park, Benbrook City Park and Robot Park.

The median age is 39.4, 31% of households have children, 67% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $86,292 a year (10% higher than the Texas median).

Cons of living in Benbrook

Cost of living in Benbrook

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Benbrook is $1,566 a month, 12% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $63,000 a year. Right now 58% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $296,400, 4% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,622 before tax, or $2,036 with property tax. That is $470 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Benbrook pays $4,963 a year, 17% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Benbrook safe?

In 2025, Benbrook Police Department reported 47 violent crimes and 371 property crimes. That works out to 191 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 51% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,511 property crimes per 100,000, 28% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.
