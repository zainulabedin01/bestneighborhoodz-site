Living in Port Aransas: common questions

Is Port Aransas, TX a good place to live?

We give Port Aransas a B. It ranks 255th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on getting around and jobs and income, and trails on cost of living and safety. It is a strong pick for young professionals.

How much does it cost to live in Port Aransas?

Renters in Port Aransas pay a median of $2,294 a month, 64% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $588,700, 107% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 6.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,588, including property tax.

Is Port Aransas safe?

In 2025, Port Aransas Police Department reported 682 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 76% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 4,039 per 100,000, 94% higher than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Port Aransas?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Port Aransas. 67% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 73 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Port Aransas flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Port Aransas as high, and coastal flooding as very high. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, coastal flooding, hurricanes, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Port Aransas?

Summers in Port Aransas peak at about 88°F in July, and January nights fall to about 50°F. Expect around 57 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 36 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Port Aransas?

The average one-way commute is 17 min, 34% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home. It is about 18 miles from downtown Corpus Christi.
