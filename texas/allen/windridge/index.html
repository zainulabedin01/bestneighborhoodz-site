What it's like to live in Windridge

Windridge is a neighborhood in Allen with about 8,500 residents, homes built around 1991, mostly owner-occupied (68%) and 42% of households with children.

Our grade for Windridge is C. That puts it 24th of 24 areas we grade in Allen, a city that gets an A+ overall.

Pros of living in Windridge

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.7 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Windridge

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Windridge

Is Windridge a good place to live?
