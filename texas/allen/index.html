Is Allen a good place to live? Common questions

Is Allen, TX a good place to live?

Allen earns an A- on our index, in the top 20% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 127th of 876 nationally and 9th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Allen?

Median rent in Allen is $1,932 a month, 38% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $464,100, 64% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,194, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Allen?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Allen are The Park At Montgomery Farm, Cypress Meadows and Cumberland Crossing. We score 24 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Allen?

The average one-way commute is 28 min, 5% higher than the Texas average. About 27% of workers work from home.
