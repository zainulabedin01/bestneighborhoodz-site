What it's like to live in Allen

Allen sits about 22 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 113,447 people lived here in 2025, up 8.4% from 2020. It is densely built, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2003).

Places locals use include Allen Station Park, Ford Park West and Spirit Park.

The median age is 38.4, 42% of households have children, 69% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $130,901 a year (67% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Allen

  • Strong public schools. 91% of the 21 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 79 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 80% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $130,901, 67% higher than the Texas median.
  • Plenty close to home. 14 grocery stores, 378 restaurants and 93 parks are mapped in town.
  • Healthier population. Only 8.9% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Allen

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,932, 38% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Allen

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Allen is $1,932 a month, 38% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $77,000 a year. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $464,100, 64% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,540 before tax, or $3,194 with property tax. That is $1,262 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Allen pays $7,844 a year, 85% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Allen safe?

In 2025, Allen Police Department reported 91 violent crimes and 822 property crimes. That works out to 79 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 80% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 709 property crimes per 100,000, 66% lower than the state rate.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.

Schools in Allen

Allen has 21 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 91% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Allen ISD and Plano ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Beverly Cheatham Elementary, Dr E T Boon Elementary and Beverly Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 91 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Living in Allen: common questions

    Is Allen, TX a good place to live?

    Allen earns a grade of A+ on our index, 3rd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on internet access and schools, and trails on getting around. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Allen?

    Median rent in Allen is $1,932 a month, 38% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $464,100, 64% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,194, including property tax.

    Is Allen safe?

    In 2025, Allen Police Department reported 79 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 80% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 709 per 100,000, 66% lower than the Texas rate.

    Does Allen flood?

    What is the weather like in Allen?
