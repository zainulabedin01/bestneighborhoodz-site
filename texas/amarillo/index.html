    Living in Amarillo: common questions

    Is Amarillo, TX a good place to live?

    Amarillo gets an overall grade of C+ and ranks 42nd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and errands and things to do, and trails on safety and health. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Amarillo?

    Renters in Amarillo pay a median of $1,092 a month, 22% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $196,900, 31% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,328, including property tax.

    Is Amarillo safe?

    In 2025, Amarillo Police Department reported 551 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 42% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,421 per 100,000, 16% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Amarillo?

    We found 70 rated public school campuses inside Amarillo. 69% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 55 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Amarillo?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Amarillo are West Amarillo, Northwest Amarillo and Southwest Amarillo. We score 11 areas in the city.

    Does Amarillo flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Amarillo as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Amarillo?

    Summers in Amarillo peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 25°F. Expect around 72 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 20 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Amarillo?

    The average one-way commute is 20 min, 26% lower than the Texas average. About 6.6% of workers work from home.
