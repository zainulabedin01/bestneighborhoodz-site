Living in Elm Creek: common questions

Is Elm Creek, TX a good place to live?

We give Elm Creek an F. It ranks 718th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on home ownership and schools, and trails on internet access and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Elm Creek?

The median home is worth $170,100, 40% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,080, including property tax.

Are the schools good in Elm Creek?

We found 1 rated public school campuses inside Elm Creek. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 54 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Elm Creek flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Elm Creek as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Elm Creek?

Summers in Elm Creek peak at about 98°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 151 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 21 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Elm Creek?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 8% higher than the Texas average. About 16% of workers work from home. It is about 5 miles from downtown Eagle Pass.
