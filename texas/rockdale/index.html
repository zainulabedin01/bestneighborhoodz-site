Living in Rockdale: common questions

Is Rockdale, TX a good place to live?

We give Rockdale a D-. It ranks 676th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and jobs and income, and trails on health and getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Rockdale?

Renters in Rockdale pay a median of $1,115 a month, 21% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $230,900, 19% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,568, including property tax.

Is Rockdale safe?

In 2025, Rockdale Police Department reported 350 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 10% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,155 per 100,000, 45% lower than the Texas rate.
