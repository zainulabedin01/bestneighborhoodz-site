    Living in Farmers Branch: common questions

    Is Farmers Branch, TX a good place to live?

    Farmers Branch earns a grade of A- on our index, 36th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and internet access, and trails on home ownership and health. It is a strong pick for young professionals and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Farmers Branch?

    Renters in Farmers Branch pay a median of $1,864 a month, 33% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $364,600, 28% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,411, including property tax.

    Is Farmers Branch safe?

    In 2025, Farmers Branch Police Department reported 133 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 66% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,031 per 100,000, about the same as the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Farmers Branch?

    We found 7 rated public school campuses inside Farmers Branch. 86% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 75 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Farmers Branch?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Farmers Branch are Northeast Farmers Branch, East Farmers Branch and Central Farmers Branch. We score 3 areas in the city.

    Does Farmers Branch flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Farmers Branch as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Farmers Branch?

    Summers in Farmers Branch peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 38°F. Expect around 108 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 38 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Farmers Branch?

    The average one-way commute is 22 min, 19% lower than the Texas average. About 20% of workers work from home. It is about 11 miles from downtown Dallas.
