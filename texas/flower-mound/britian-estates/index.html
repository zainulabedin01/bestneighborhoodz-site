What it's like to live in Britian Estates

Britian Estates is a neighborhood in Flower Mound with about 9,400 residents, homes built around 1994, mostly owner-occupied (85%) and 36% of households with children.

Our grade for Britian Estates is A-. That puts it 8th of 9 areas we grade in Flower Mound, a city that gets an A+ overall.

Pros of living in Britian Estates

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.7 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Britian Estates

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,620, 17% higher than the Flower Mound median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Britian Estates

Is Britian Estates a good place to live?

Britian Estates is a neighborhood in Flower Mound, TX, home to about 9,400 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 8th of 9 areas we score in Flower Mound. It does best on education levels and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Britian Estates?

Renters in Britian Estates pay a median of $2,620 a month, 17% higher than the Flower Mound median. The median home is worth $449,000, 20% lower than the Flower Mound median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.5x across Flower Mound.

What schools serve Britian Estates?

The closest rated public schools are Marjory Vickery Elementary (elementary, rated B), STEM Academy at Hedrick Middle (middle, rated D) and Lewisville High School (high, rated D). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
