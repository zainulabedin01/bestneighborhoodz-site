What it's like to live in Flower Mound

Flower Mound sits about 26 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 79,540 people lived here in 2025, up 4.7% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Murrell Park, Wilkerson Park and Timber Creek Park.

The median age is 43.5, 39% of households have children, 82% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $161,235 a year (105% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Flower Mound

  • Strong public schools. 96% of the 22 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 42 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 89% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $161,235, 105% higher than the Texas median.
  • Healthier population. Only 7.2% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Flower Mound

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $2,231, 59% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Flower Mound

Flower Mound renters pay a median of $2,231 a month, 59% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $89,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 46% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $560,200, 97% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $3,066 before tax, or $3,758 with property tax. That is $1,527 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Flower Mound pays $8,300 a year, 96% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Flower Mound safe?

In 2025, Flower Mound Police Department reported 34 violent crimes and 462 property crimes. That works out to 42 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 89% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 571 property crimes per 100,000, 73% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 42% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Flower Mound

Flower Mound has 22 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 96% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Lewisville ISD and Argyle ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Bluebonnet Elementary, Old Settlers Elementary and Argyle South Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 91 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
