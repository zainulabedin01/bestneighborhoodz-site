Is Flower Mound a good place to live? Common questions

Is Flower Mound, TX a good place to live?

We give Flower Mound an A+. That places it in the top 5% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 30th of 876 nationally and 1st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Flower Mound?

Renters in Flower Mound pay a median of $2,231 a month, 59% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $560,200, 97% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,758, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Flower Mound?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Flower Mound are Highland Meadows, Central Flower Mound and West Flower Mound. We score 9 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Flower Mound?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 29% of workers work from home.
