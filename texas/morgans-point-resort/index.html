What it's like to live in Morgan's Point Resort

Morgan's Point Resort sits about 17 miles from downtown Killeen, in the Killeen-Temple metro. 4,746 people lived here in 2025, up 2.1% from 2020. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2002).

Places locals use include John Ansay Park and Hidden Canyon Park.

The median age is 38.2, 29% of households have children, 94% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $79,450 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Cost of living in Morgan's Point Resort

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Morgan's Point Resort is $1,819 a month, 30% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $73,000 a year. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $927 for a one-bedroom, $1,138 for a two-bedroom and $1,576 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $245,100, 14% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,342 before tax, or $1,701 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Morgan's Point Resort pays $4,311 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
