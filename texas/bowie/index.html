What it's like to live in Bowie

Bowie is a town in Montague County. 6,113 people lived here in 2025, up 12.3% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Pelham Park, Meyers Park and Bowie Public Library.

The median age is 34.4, 35% of households have children, 69% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $57,488 a year (27% lower than the Texas median).

Cons of living in Bowie

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Bowie

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Bowie is $1,080 a month, 23% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $43,000 a year. Right now 60% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $907 for a one-bedroom, $1,088 for a two-bedroom and $1,507 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $174,300, 39% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $954 before tax, or $1,096 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Bowie pays $1,703 a year, 60% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

In 2025, Bowie Police Department reported 15 violent crimes and 93 property crimes. That works out to 241 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 38% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,496 property crimes per 100,000, 28% lower than the state rate.
