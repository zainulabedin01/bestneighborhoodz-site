Living in Bridge City: common questions

Is Bridge City, TX a good place to live?

Bridge City gets an overall grade of B and ranks 223rd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on internet access and jobs and income, and trails on schools and health. It is a strong pick for young professionals and remote workers.

How much does it cost to live in Bridge City?

Renters in Bridge City pay a median of $1,431 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $202,800, 29% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,381, including property tax.

Is Bridge City safe?

In 2025, Bridge City Police Department reported 193 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 50% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 466 per 100,000, 78% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Bridge City?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Bridge City. 25% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 35 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Bridge City flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Bridge City as high, and coastal flooding as very high. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, coastal flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Bridge City?

Summers in Bridge City peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 44°F. Expect around 93 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 62 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Bridge City?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 9% lower than the Texas average. About 3.0% of workers work from home. It is about 19 miles from downtown Beaumont.
