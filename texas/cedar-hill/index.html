Is Cedar Hill a good place to live? Common questions

Is Cedar Hill, TX a good place to live?

Cedar Hill earns a D+ on our index, in the bottom 32% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2265th of 3,321 nationally and 94th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Cedar Hill?

Renters in Cedar Hill pay a median of $1,993 a month, 42% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $302,700, 7% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,102, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Cedar Hill?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Cedar Hill are Southwest Cedar Hill, Northeast Cedar Hill and North Cedar Hill. We score 8 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Cedar Hill?

The average one-way commute is 31 min, 18% higher than the Texas average. About 17% of workers work from home.
