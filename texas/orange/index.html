How much does it cost to live in Orange?

Median rent in Orange is $1,108 a month, 21% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $164,900, 42% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,082, including property tax.
