What it's like to live in Mission

Mission sits about 5 miles from downtown McAllen, in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metro. 88,992 people lived here in 2025, up 3.7% from 2020. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2000).

Places locals use include Bentsen Palm Park, Bentsen Palm Community Park and Mission Nature Park.

The median age is 34.9, 41% of households have children, 71% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $60,767 a year (23% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Mission

  • Low violent crime. 134 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 65% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $950, 32% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Mission

  • Higher unemployment. 7.8% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Many adults uninsured. 32% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 177 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Mission

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Mission is $950 a month, 32% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $38,000 a year. Right now 45% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $806 for a one-bedroom, $1,017 for a two-bedroom and $1,305 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $175,700, 38% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $962 before tax, or $1,224 with property tax. That is $274 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Mission pays $3,151 a year, 26% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Mission safe?

In 2025, Mission Police Department reported 120 violent crimes and 1,039 property crimes. That works out to 134 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 65% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,164 property crimes per 100,000, 44% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is up about 36% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Mission

Mission has 31 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 77% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Sharyland ISD and Idea Public Schools.

Top-rated campuses include Sharyland Advanced Academic Academy, Idea North Mission College Preparatory and Sharyland High School. The average rating score across all campuses is 84 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
