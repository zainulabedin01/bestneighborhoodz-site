Is Mission a good place to live? Common questions

Is Mission, TX a good place to live?

Mission gets a D+ overall, which puts it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 612th of 876 nationally and 45th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in Mission?

Median rent in Mission is $950 a month, 32% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $175,700, 38% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,224, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Mission?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Mission are Shary Springs Subdivision, Orchards at Shary Road and East Mission. We score 17 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Mission?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 23% lower than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home.
