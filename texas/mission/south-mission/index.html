What it's like to live in South Mission

South Mission is an area in Mission with about 800 residents, homes built around 1992, mostly owner-occupied (81%) and 22% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 12th of 17 areas in Mission, which grades B overall.

Pros of living in South Mission

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in South Mission

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,520, 165% higher than the Mission median.
  • Higher poverty. 34% of residents live below the poverty line, against 19% across Mission.
