Is Weslaco a good place to live? Common questions

Is Weslaco, TX a good place to live?

Weslaco earns a D- on our index, in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2832nd of 3,321 nationally and 136th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Weslaco?

Median rent in Weslaco is $967 a month, 31% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $140,000, 51% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $964, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Weslaco?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Weslaco are Southwest Weslaco, Sun Country Estates and Olivarez #5 Colonia. We score 6 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Weslaco?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 10% lower than the Texas average. About 7.3% of workers work from home.
