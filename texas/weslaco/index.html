What it's like to live in Weslaco

Weslaco sits about 17 miles from downtown McAllen, in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metro. 44,203 people lived here in 2025, up 10.0% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Harlon Block Park, Pablo pea Park In Weslaco and 18th & Westgate Park.

The median age is 31.1, 41% of households have children, 56% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $59,551 a year (24% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Weslaco

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $967, 31% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.4 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Weslaco

  • Higher poverty. 23% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 35% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 160 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Weslaco

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Weslaco is $967 a month, 31% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $39,000 a year. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $806 for a one-bedroom, $1,017 for a two-bedroom and $1,305 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $140,000, 51% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $766 before tax, or $964 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Weslaco pays $2,378 a year, 44% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Weslaco safe?

In 2025, Weslaco Police Department reported 148 violent crimes and 1,196 property crimes. That works out to 331 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 15% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 2,675 property crimes per 100,000, 28% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 27% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Weslaco

Weslaco has 20 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 80% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Weslaco ISD and Idea Public Schools.

Top-rated campuses include Weslaco High School, Central Middle and Idea College Prep Weslaco. The average rating score across all campuses is 84 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Daily life in Weslaco

    Everyday places mapped inside Weslaco, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Harlon Block Park, Pablo pea Park In Weslaco, 18th & Westgate Park.
