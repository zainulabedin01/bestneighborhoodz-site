What it's like to live in Floresville

Floresville sits about 31 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 9,105 people lived here in 2025, up 26.0% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Floresville River City Park, Masonic Youth Park and Floresville Pecan Park.

The median age is 39.9, 37% of households have children, 67% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $65,270 a year (17% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Floresville

  • Low violent crime. 197 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 49% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,076, 23% lower than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 26% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Floresville

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 130 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Floresville

Floresville renters pay a median of $1,076 a month, 23% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $43,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 45% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $241,000, 15% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,319 before tax, or $1,618 with property tax. That is $542 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Floresville pays $3,589 a year, 15% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Floresville

Everyday places mapped inside Floresville, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Floresville River City Park, Masonic Youth Park, Floresville Pecan Park.
