Is Jacksonville a good place to live? Common questions

Is Jacksonville, TX a good place to live?

We give Jacksonville a D-. That places it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 3039th of 3,321 nationally and 166th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Jacksonville?

Renters in Jacksonville pay a median of $908 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $119,800, 58% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $842, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Jacksonville?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Jacksonville are East Jacksonville, Central Jacksonville and West Jacksonville. We score 4 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Jacksonville?

The average one-way commute is 23 min, 12% lower than the Texas average. About 5.9% of workers work from home.
