What it's like to live in West Jacksonville

West Jacksonville is an area in Jacksonville with about 2,700 residents, homes built around 1983, a mix of owners and renters and 77% of households with children.

Our grade for West Jacksonville is D. That puts it 3rd of 4 areas we grade in Jacksonville, a city that gets a D overall.

Pros of living in West Jacksonville

Cons of living in West Jacksonville

Questions about West Jacksonville

Is West Jacksonville a good place to live?

West Jacksonville covers part of Jacksonville, TX, home to about 2,700 people. It gets a D, in the bottom quarter of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 3rd of 4 areas we score in Jacksonville. It does best on getting around and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in West Jacksonville?

Renters in West Jacksonville pay a median of $960 a month, 6% higher than the Jacksonville median. The median home is worth $103,000, 14% lower than the Jacksonville median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 2.3x across Jacksonville.

What schools serve West Jacksonville?

The closest rated public schools are West Side Elementary (elementary, rated B), Nichols Intermediate (middle, rated D) and Jacksonville High School (high, rated C). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
