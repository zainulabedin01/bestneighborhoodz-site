Living in Trophy Club: common questions

Is Trophy Club, TX a good place to live?

We give Trophy Club an A+. It ranks 2nd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on jobs and income and safety, and trails on getting around and cost of living. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

How much does it cost to live in Trophy Club?

Renters in Trophy Club pay a median of $1,992 a month, 42% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $675,100, 138% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $4,529, including property tax.

Is Trophy Club safe?

In 2025, Trophy Club Police Department reported 29 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 92% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 292 per 100,000, 86% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Trophy Club?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Trophy Club. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 90 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

Does Trophy Club flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Trophy Club as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Trophy Club?

Summers in Trophy Club peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 36°F. Expect around 108 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 37 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Trophy Club?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 25% of workers work from home. It is about 29 miles from downtown Dallas.
