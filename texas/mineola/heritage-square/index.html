What it's like to live in Heritage Square

Heritage Square is a neighborhood in Mineola with about 900 residents, homes built around 1981, a mix of owners and renters and 14% of households with children.

Our grade for Heritage Square is B+. That puts it 2nd of 4 areas we grade in Mineola, a city that gets a D+ overall.

Pros of living in Heritage Square

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.
