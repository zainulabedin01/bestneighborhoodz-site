What it's like to live in Mineola

Mineola is a town in Wood County. 5,537 people lived here in 2025, up 15.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Mineola Civic Center and RV Park, Peterson Park and Meredith Memorial Field.

The median age is 41.3, 26% of households have children, 56% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $53,000 a year (32% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Mineola

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 4 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 218 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 44% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $931, 34% lower than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 16% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Mineola

  • Higher unemployment. 11% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves, hail and winter storms as very high here.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 73% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Mineola

Mineola renters pay a median of $931 a month, 34% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $37,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $862 for a one-bedroom, $1,019 for a two-bedroom and $1,294 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $215,700, 24% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,181 before tax, or $1,328 with property tax. That is $397 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Mineola pays $1,766 a year, 58% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Mineola safe?

In 2025, Mineola Police Department reported 12 violent crimes and 96 property crimes. That works out to 218 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 44% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,741 property crimes per 100,000, 17% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.
