What it's like to live in Central Longview

Central Longview is an area in Longview with about 8,000 residents, homes built around 1976, mostly rented (67% of homes) and 29% of households with children.

Our grade for Central Longview is B-. That puts it 4th of 7 areas we grade in Longview, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in Central Longview

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Central Longview

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Central Longview

Is Central Longview a good place to live?

Central Longview covers part of Longview, TX, home to about 8,000 people. It gets a B-, in the top 44% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 7 areas we score in Longview. It does best on errands and things to do and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Central Longview?

Renters in Central Longview pay a median of $1,160 a month, 5% higher than the Longview median. The median home is worth $220,000, 9% higher than the Longview median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.2x across Longview.

What schools serve Central Longview?

The closest rated public schools are J L Everhart Magnet Elementary (elementary, rated A), Forest Park Magnet School (middle, rated B) and Longview High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
