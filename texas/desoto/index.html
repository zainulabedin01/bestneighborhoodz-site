Is DeSoto a good place to live? Common questions

Is DeSoto, TX a good place to live?

We give DeSoto a D+. That places it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 616th of 876 nationally and 47th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are home ownership and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in DeSoto?

Median rent in DeSoto is $1,666 a month, 19% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $319,400, 13% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,191, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in DeSoto?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in DeSoto are The Creeks at Windmill Hill, Southwest DeSoto and West DeSoto. We score 9 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in DeSoto?

The average one-way commute is 31 min, 15% higher than the Texas average. About 17% of workers work from home.
