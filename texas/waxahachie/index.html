What it's like to live in Waxahachie

Waxahachie sits about 27 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 50,090 people lived here in 2025, up 21.9% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2004).

Local spots people know include Getzendaner Park, Brown Singleton Park and Lions Park.

The median age is 33.3, 36% of households have children, 60% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $85,723 a year (9% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Waxahachie

  • Low violent crime. 180 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 54% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 22% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Waxahachie

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,618, 15% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Waxahachie

Waxahachie renters pay a median of $1,618 a month, 15% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $65,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 42% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $336,500, 19% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,842 before tax, or $2,277 with property tax. That is $659 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Waxahachie pays $5,225 a year, 23% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Waxahachie safe?

In 2025, Waxahachie Police Department reported 91 violent crimes and 517 property crimes. That works out to 180 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 54% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,023 property crimes per 100,000, 51% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 34% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Waxahachie
