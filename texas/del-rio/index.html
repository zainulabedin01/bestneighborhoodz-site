Is Del Rio, TX a good place to live?

Del Rio gets a D+ overall, which puts it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2421st of 3,321 nationally and 101st of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Del Rio?

Median rent in Del Rio is $895 a month, 36% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $160,400, 43% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,083, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Del Rio?
