What it's like to live in Southeast Del Rio

Southeast Del Rio is an area in Del Rio with about 3,000 residents, homes built around 1971, mostly owner-occupied (76%) and 39% of households with children.

Our grade for Southeast Del Rio is D+. That puts it 5th of 5 areas we grade in Del Rio, a city that gets a B overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Del Rio

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.2 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Southeast Del Rio

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,420, 59% higher than the Del Rio median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hail and winter storms as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southeast Del Rio

Is Southeast Del Rio a good place to live?

Southeast Del Rio covers part of Del Rio, TX, home to about 3,000 people. It gets a D+, in the bottom third of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 5th of 5 areas we score in Del Rio. It does best on getting around and worst on internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Southeast Del Rio?

Renters in Southeast Del Rio pay a median of $1,420 a month, 59% higher than the Del Rio median. The median home is worth $107,000, 33% lower than the Del Rio median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 2.4x across Del Rio.

What schools serve Southeast Del Rio?

The closest rated public schools are Dr Fermin Calderon Elementary (elementary, rated B), Brackett Junior High (middle, rated C) and Brackett High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
