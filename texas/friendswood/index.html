Is Friendswood a good place to live? Common questions

Is Friendswood, TX a good place to live?

We give Friendswood a B+. That places it in the top 28% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 691st of 3,321 nationally and 21st of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Friendswood?

Median rent in Friendswood is $1,688 a month, 20% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $420,100, 48% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,828, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Friendswood?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Friendswood are The Pines of Wilderness Trails, The Reserve and Southwest Friendswood. We score 9 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Friendswood?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 18% of workers work from home.
