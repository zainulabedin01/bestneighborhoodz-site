What it's like to live in Leon Valley

Leon Valley sits about 6 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 11,563 people lived here in 2025, up 0.1% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Raymond Rimkus Park, Steurenthaler-Silo Park and Huebner-Onion Natural Area Park.

The median age is 39.0, 36% of households have children, 54% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $59,481 a year (24% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Leon Valley

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,187, 15% lower than the Texas median.
  • Plenty close to home. 9 grocery stores, 90 restaurants and 5 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Leon Valley

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 20% of the 5 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 123 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Leon Valley

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Leon Valley is $1,187 a month, 15% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $47,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $253,200, 11% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,386 before tax, or $1,732 with property tax. That is $545 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Leon Valley pays $4,157 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Leon Valley safe?

In 2025, Leon Valley Police Department reported 42 violent crimes and 574 property crimes. That works out to 369 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 5% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 5,038 property crimes per 100,000, 142% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Daily life in Leon Valley

Everyday places mapped inside Leon Valley, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Raymond Rimkus Park, Steurenthaler-Silo Park, Huebner-Onion Natural Area Park.

The average one-way commute is 27 minutes, about the same as the Texas average. 9% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown San Antonio is about 6 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Leon Valley work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $59,481, income per person is $37,023, unemployment is 7.1% and 31% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
