What it's like to live in Lindsay

Lindsay is a town in Cooke County. 1,261 people lived here in 2025, up 20.8% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2001).

Places locals use include J. M. Lindsay Park and Parkview Park.

The median age is 38.9, 46% of households have children, 88% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $115,417 a year (47% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Lindsay

Cost of living in Lindsay

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lindsay is $1,212 a month, 14% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $48,000 a year. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,004 for a one-bedroom, $1,314 for a two-bedroom and $1,623 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $352,300, 24% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,928 before tax, or $2,323 with property tax. That is $1,111 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Lindsay pays $4,739 a year, 12% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Lindsay

Everyday places mapped inside Lindsay, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include J. M. Lindsay Park, Parkview Park.

The average one-way commute is 27 minutes, about the same as the Texas average. 12% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Gainesville is about 4 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Lindsay work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $115,417, income per person is $43,774, unemployment is 0.5% and 34% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
