What it's like to live in Southwest Sandhill

Southwest Sandhill is a town in Ward County. About 1,143 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 44.3, 39% of households have children, 87% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $74,306 a year (5% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Southwest Sandhill

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 17 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Southwest Sandhill

Cost of living in Southwest Sandhill

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,075 for a one-bedroom, $1,406 for a two-bedroom and $1,675 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $109,700, 61% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $600 before tax, or $677 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Southwest Sandhill pays $914 a year, 78% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
