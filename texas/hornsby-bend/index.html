What it's like to live in Hornsby Bend

Hornsby Bend sits about 11 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 12,367 people live here. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2009).

Local spots people know include Austin's Colony Colorado River Park, Austin's Colony Neighborhood Park and Elm Creek Park.

The median age is 34.5, 35% of households have children, 73% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $89,614 a year (14% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Hornsby Bend

Hornsby Bend renters pay a median of $2,147 a month, 53% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $86,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 43% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $314,900, 11% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,724 before tax, or $2,040 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Hornsby Bend pays $3,801 a year, 10% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
