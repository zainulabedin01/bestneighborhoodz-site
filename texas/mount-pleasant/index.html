    Living in Mount Pleasant: common questions

    Is Mount Pleasant, TX a good place to live?

    How much does it cost to live in Mount Pleasant?

    Median rent in Mount Pleasant is $910 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $170,700, 40% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,115, including property tax.

    Is Mount Pleasant safe?
