    Living in Andrews: common questions

    Is Andrews, TX a good place to live?

    Andrews gets an overall grade of B and ranks 68th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and home ownership, and trails on education levels and health. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Andrews?

    Median rent in Andrews is $1,350 a month, 4% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $196,300, 31% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,297, including property tax.

    Is Andrews safe?

    In 2025, Andrews Police Department reported 219 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 44% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 787 per 100,000, 62% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Andrews?

    What are the best neighborhoods in Andrews?

    Does Andrews flood?

    What is the weather like in Andrews?
