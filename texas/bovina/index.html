What it's like to live in Bovina

Bovina is a town in Parmer County. 1,677 people lived here in 2025, down 0.7% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 32.4, 42% of households have children, 68% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $48,500 a year (38% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Bovina

Bovina renters pay a median of $1,033 a month, 26% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $41,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 11% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $928 for a one-bedroom, $1,214 for a two-bedroom and $1,446 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $113,700, 60% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $622 before tax, or $696 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Bovina pays $878 a year, 79% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
