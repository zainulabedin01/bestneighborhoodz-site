What it's like to live in Preston

Preston sits about 17 miles from downtown Sherman, in the Sherman-Denison metro. About 1,747 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Volleyball Court, The Pit and Preston Bend Park.

The median age is 55.2, 11% of households have children, 79% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $65,069 a year (17% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Preston

Preston renters pay a median of $1,289 a month, 8% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $52,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 66% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,126 for a one-bedroom, $1,395 for a two-bedroom and $1,932 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $184,000, 35% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,007 before tax, or $1,090 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Preston pays $1,000 a year, 76% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
