Is Bayou Vista, TX a good place to live?

We give Bayou Vista a B-. That places it in the top 44% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 4157th of 11,459 nationally and 124th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are health and jobs and income, and its weakest is cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Bayou Vista?

Median rent in Bayou Vista is $2,083 a month, 48% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $375,600, 32% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,413, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Bayou Vista?

The average one-way commute is 34 min, 27% higher than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home.
