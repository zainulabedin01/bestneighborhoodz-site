What it's like to live in Magnolia Pond

Magnolia Pond is a neighborhood in The Woodlands with about 700 residents, homes built around 1985, a mix of owners and renters and 8% of households with children.

It grades B- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 53rd of 55 areas in The Woodlands, which grades A+ overall.

Pros of living in Magnolia Pond

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Magnolia Pond

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $3,470, 90% higher than the The Woodlands median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Magnolia Pond

Is Magnolia Pond a good place to live?
