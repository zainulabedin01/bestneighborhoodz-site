What it's like to live in Foxbriar

Foxbriar is a neighborhood in The Woodlands with about 1,100 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2002), a mix of owners and renters and 24% of households with children.

Our grade for Foxbriar is B+. That puts it 45th of 55 areas we grade in The Woodlands, a city that gets an A+ overall.

Pros of living in Foxbriar

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Foxbriar

  • Higher unemployment. 11% of the labor force is out of work, against 4.9% across The Woodlands.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Foxbriar

Is Foxbriar a good place to live?

Foxbriar is a neighborhood in The Woodlands, TX, home to about 1,100 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 45th of 55 areas we score in The Woodlands. It does best on schools and worst on internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Foxbriar?

Renters in Foxbriar pay a median of $1,820 a month, about the same as the The Woodlands median. The median home is worth $290,000, 43% lower than the The Woodlands median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across The Woodlands.

What schools serve Foxbriar?

The closest rated public schools are Bush Elementary (elementary, rated A), Mitchell Intermediate (middle, rated A) and The Woodlands High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
