What it's like to live in Cascade Canyon

Cascade Canyon is a neighborhood in The Woodlands with about 2,300 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2009), mostly owner-occupied (92%) and 66% of households with children.

Our grade for Cascade Canyon is A+. That puts it 15th of 55 areas we grade in The Woodlands, a city that gets an A+ overall.

Pros of living in Cascade Canyon

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $250,000+, 78% higher than the The Woodlands median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in Cascade Canyon

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $3,500+, 92% higher than the The Woodlands median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Cascade Canyon

Is Cascade Canyon a good place to live?

Cascade Canyon is a neighborhood in The Woodlands, TX, home to about 2,300 people. It gets an A+, in the top 5% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 15th of 55 areas we score in The Woodlands. It does best on education levels and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Cascade Canyon?

Renters in Cascade Canyon pay a median of $3,500+ a month, 92% higher than the The Woodlands median. The median home is worth $837,000, 64% higher than the The Woodlands median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across The Woodlands.

What schools serve Cascade Canyon?

The closest rated public schools are Tough Elementary (elementary, rated A), Bear Branch Intermediate (middle, rated B) and The Woodlands High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
