Is The Woodlands a good place to live? Common questions

Is The Woodlands, TX a good place to live?

The Woodlands gets an A overall, which puts it in the top 12% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 65th of 876 nationally and 2nd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are education levels and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in The Woodlands?

Renters in The Woodlands pay a median of $1,822 a month, 30% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $511,700, 80% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,473, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in The Woodlands?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in The Woodlands are Evangeline Oaks, Beckonvale and Linton Ridge. We score 55 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in The Woodlands?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home.
