What it's like to live in Northeast Temple

Northeast Temple is an area in Temple with about 6,400 residents, older homes (median year built 1963), mostly owner-occupied (81%) and 30% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 6th of 11 areas in Temple, which grades D overall.

Pros of living in Northeast Temple

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,060, 14% lower than the Temple median.

Cons of living in Northeast Temple

  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Northeast Temple

Is Northeast Temple a good place to live?

Northeast Temple covers part of Temple, TX, home to about 6,400 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 6th of 11 areas we score in Temple. It does best on cost of living and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Northeast Temple?

Median rent in Northeast Temple is $1,060 a month, 14% lower than the Temple median. The median home is worth $153,000, 36% lower than the Temple median. A median home costs 1.8x the median household income, compared with 3.5x across Temple.
