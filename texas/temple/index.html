What it's like to live in Temple

Temple sits about 20 miles from downtown Killeen, in the Killeen-Temple metro. 98,412 people lived here in 2025, up 19.7% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Ferguson Park, Lions Park and Temple Lake Park.

The median age is 33.8, 33% of households have children, 52% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $68,204 a year (13% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Temple

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,231, 12% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 20% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Temple

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 118 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Temple

Temple renters pay a median of $1,231 a month, 12% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $49,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 50% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $927 for a one-bedroom, $1,138 for a two-bedroom and $1,576 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $239,000, 16% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,308 before tax, or $1,621 with property tax. That is $390 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Temple pays $3,749 a year, 11% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

    Living in Temple: common questions

    Is Temple, TX a good place to live?

    Temple earns a grade of D on our index, 66th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and getting around, and trails on errands and things to do and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Temple?

    Renters in Temple pay a median of $1,231 a month, 12% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $239,000, 16% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,621, including property tax.

    In 2025, Temple Police Department reported 9 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 98% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 62 per 100,000, 97% lower than the Texas rate. This agency reported only part of the year, so the rate is shown but not scored.

    What is the weather like in Temple?
