Is Temple a good place to live? Common questions

Is Temple, TX a good place to live?

Temple earns a D on our index, in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 669th of 876 nationally and 50th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Temple?

Renters in Temple pay a median of $1,231 a month, 12% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $239,000, 16% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,621, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Temple?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Temple are North Temple, Lakewood Ranch and Wyndham Hill. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Temple?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 21% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home.
