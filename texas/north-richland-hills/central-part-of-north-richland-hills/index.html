What it's like to live in Central part of North Richland Hills

Central part of North Richland Hills is an area in North Richland Hills with about 41,300 residents, homes built around 1987, a mix of owners and renters and 30% of households with children.

It grades B against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 5th of 7 areas in North Richland Hills, which grades A- overall.
