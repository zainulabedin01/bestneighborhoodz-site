Is North Richland Hills a good place to live? Common questions

Is North Richland Hills, TX a good place to live?

We give North Richland Hills a B+. That places it in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 213th of 876 nationally and 20th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in North Richland Hills?

Median rent in North Richland Hills is $1,704 a month, 21% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $360,700, 27% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,428, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in North Richland Hills?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in North Richland Hills are Northeast side of North Richland Hills, Northwest side of North Richland Hills and North side of North Richland Hills. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in North Richland Hills?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 16% of workers work from home.
