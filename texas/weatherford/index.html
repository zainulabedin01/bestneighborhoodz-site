Is Weatherford a good place to live? Common questions

Is Weatherford, TX a good place to live?

Weatherford gets a C- overall, which puts it just below the middle of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2113th of 3,321 nationally and 79th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Weatherford?

Renters in Weatherford pay a median of $1,527 a month, 9% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $312,100, 10% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,108, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Weatherford?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Weatherford are Southeast Weatherford, East Weatherford and Southwest Weatherford. We score 6 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Weatherford?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 6% lower than the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home.
