    Living in Weatherford: common questions

    Is Weatherford, TX a good place to live?

    Weatherford gets an overall grade of C- and ranks 125th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on jobs and income and internet access, and trails on schools and cost of living.

    How much does it cost to live in Weatherford?

    Renters in Weatherford pay a median of $1,527 a month, 9% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $312,100, 10% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,108, including property tax.

    Is Weatherford safe?

    In 2025, Weatherford Police Department reported 206 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 47% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,302 per 100,000, 38% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Weatherford?

    We found 10 rated public school campuses inside Weatherford. 40% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 28 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Weatherford?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Weatherford are Southwest Weatherford, Southeast Weatherford and West Weatherford. We score 6 areas in the city.

    Does Weatherford flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Weatherford as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Weatherford?

    Summers in Weatherford peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 32°F. Expect around 90 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 35 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Weatherford?

    The average one-way commute is 25 min, 6% lower than the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home. It is about 59 miles from downtown Dallas.
