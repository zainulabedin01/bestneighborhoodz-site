What it's like to live in Pecan Grove

Pecan Grove sits about 24 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 24,272 people live here. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Pecan Grove Park, Harlem Road Park and Belin Park and Field.

The median age is 38.2, 41% of households have children, 92% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $131,937 a year (68% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Pecan Grove

  • Strong public schools. 83% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $131,937, 68% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Pecan Grove

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 36 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 116 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Pecan Grove

Pecan Grove renters pay a median of $1,507 a month, 7% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $60,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $383,500, 35% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,099 before tax, or $2,649 with property tax. That is $1,142 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Pecan Grove pays $6,603 a year, 56% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
