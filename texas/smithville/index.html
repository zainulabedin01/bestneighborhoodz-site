What it's like to live in Smithville

Smithville sits about 41 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 4,367 people lived here in 2025, up 8.4% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Smithville Train Park, Smithville Texas Veterans Memorial Park and Keilburg Park.

The median age is 43.1, 19% of households have children, 57% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $59,977 a year (24% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Smithville

  • Low violent crime. 182 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 53% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,230, 12% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Smithville

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 25% of the 4 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 124 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Smithville

Smithville renters pay a median of $1,230 a month, 12% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $49,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 40% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $254,400, 10% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,393 before tax, or $1,688 with property tax. That is $458 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Smithville pays $3,543 a year, 16% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Is Smithville, TX a good place to live?
