What it's like to live in Harper

Harper is a town in Gillespie County. About 1,214 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Harper Community Park and Harper Library and Resale Shop.

The median age is 34.6, 55% of households have children and 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them.

Cost of living in Harper

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,216 for a one-bedroom, $1,591 for a two-bedroom and $1,896 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $434,600, 53% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,379 before tax, or $2,575 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Harper pays $2,355 a year, 44% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Harper

Everyday places mapped inside Harper, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Harper Community Park, Harper Library and Resale Shop.

The average one-way commute is 29 minutes, 7% longer than the Texas average. 20% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Fredericksburg is about 22 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Harper work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
