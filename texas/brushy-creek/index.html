What it's like to live in Brushy Creek

Brushy Creek sits about 15 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 19,576 people live here. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Olson Meadows Park, Fern Bluff Park and Cat Hollow Park.

The median age is 39.5, 42% of households have children, 73% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $138,589 a year (77% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Brushy Creek

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $138,589, 77% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Brushy Creek

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $2,127, 52% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Brushy Creek

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Brushy Creek is $2,127 a month, 52% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $85,000 a year. Right now 37% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $499,300, 76% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,733 before tax, or $3,387 with property tax. That is $1,260 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Brushy Creek pays $7,846 a year, 85% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
