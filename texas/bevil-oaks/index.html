What it's like to live in Bevil Oaks

Bevil Oaks sits about 9 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. 1,042 people lived here in 2025, down 4.4% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 48.8, 29% of households have children, 93% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $91,031 a year (16% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Bevil Oaks

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.6 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Bevil Oaks

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 32 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Bevil Oaks

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $232,700, 18% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,274 before tax, or $1,504 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Bevil Oaks pays $2,764 a year, 35% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
