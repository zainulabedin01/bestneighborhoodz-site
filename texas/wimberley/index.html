What it's like to live in Wimberley

Wimberley sits about 30 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 2,909 people lived here in 2025, up 1.9% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Blue hole Swimming Area, Blue Hole Regional Park and Old Baldy.

The median age is 57.1, 16% of households have children, 67% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $80,362 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Cost of living in Wimberley

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Wimberley is $1,293 a month, 8% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $52,000 a year. Right now 36% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $550,800, 94% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $3,015 before tax, or $3,555 with property tax. That is $2,262 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Wimberley pays $6,478 a year, 53% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
