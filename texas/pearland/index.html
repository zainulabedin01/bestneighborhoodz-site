    Living in Pearland: common questions

    Is Pearland, TX a good place to live?

    Pearland earns a grade of A on our index, 9th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on jobs and income and internet access, and trails on getting around and errands and things to do. It is a strong pick for families, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Pearland?

    Renters in Pearland pay a median of $1,860 a month, 33% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $356,300, 26% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,562, including property tax.

    Is Pearland safe?

    In 2025, Pearland Police Department reported 82 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 79% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,135 per 100,000, 46% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Pearland?

    We found 31 rated public school campuses inside Pearland. 94% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 91 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Pearland?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Pearland are Banbury Cross, Stonebridge and Kelsey Pointe. We score 22 areas in the city.

    Does Pearland flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Pearland as high, and coastal flooding as low. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Pearland?

    Summers in Pearland peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 46°F. Expect around 101 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 53 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Pearland?

    The average one-way commute is 33 min, 23% higher than the Texas average. About 14% of workers work from home. It is about 16 miles from downtown Houston.
