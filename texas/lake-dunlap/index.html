Living in Lake Dunlap: common questions

Is Lake Dunlap, TX a good place to live?

We give Lake Dunlap a B-. It ranks 323rd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and internet access, and trails on getting around and education levels. It is a strong pick for remote workers and people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Lake Dunlap?

The median home is worth $226,800, 20% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,413, including property tax.

Does Lake Dunlap flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Lake Dunlap as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Lake Dunlap?

Summers in Lake Dunlap peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 128 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 31 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Lake Dunlap?

The average one-way commute is 31 min, 17% higher than the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home. It is about 30 miles from downtown San Antonio.
