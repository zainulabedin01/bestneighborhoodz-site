What it's like to live in DeCordova

DeCordova is a town in Hood County. 3,296 people lived here in 2025, up 9.5% from 2020. It is suburban.

The median age is 55.4, 15% of households have children, 96% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $106,106 a year (35% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in DeCordova

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,297 for a one-bedroom, $1,463 for a two-bedroom and $1,900 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $340,700, 20% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,865 before tax, or $2,139 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in DeCordova pays $3,292 a year, 22% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
