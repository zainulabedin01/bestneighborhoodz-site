Living in Borger: common questions

Is Borger, TX a good place to live?

Borger earns a grade of B- on our index, 80th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on cost of living and getting around, and trails on education levels and internet access. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Borger?

Renters in Borger pay a median of $910 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $103,400, 64% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $698, including property tax.

Is Borger safe?

In 2025, Borger Police Department reported 486 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 25% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,491 per 100,000, 29% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Borger?

We found 6 rated public school campuses inside Borger. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 51 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

Does Borger flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Borger as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Borger?

Summers in Borger peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 27°F. Expect around 82 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 22 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Borger?

The average one-way commute is 19 min, 27% lower than the Texas average. About 6.0% of workers work from home.
