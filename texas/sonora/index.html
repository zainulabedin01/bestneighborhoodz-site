    Living in Sonora: common questions

    Is Sonora, TX a good place to live?

    Sonora gets an overall grade of B+ and ranks 181st of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and jobs and income, and trails on education levels and home ownership. It is a strong pick for young professionals, retirees and people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Sonora?

    Median rent in Sonora is $772 a month, 45% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $143,200, 50% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $934, including property tax.

    Is Sonora safe?

    In 2025, Sonora Police Department reported 125 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 68% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 166 per 100,000, 92% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Sonora?

    We found 2 rated public school campuses inside Sonora. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 26 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

    What is the weather like in Sonora?

    Summers in Sonora peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 31°F. Expect around 111 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 23 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Sonora?
