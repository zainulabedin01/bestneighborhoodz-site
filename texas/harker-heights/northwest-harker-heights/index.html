What it's like to live in Northwest Harker Heights

Northwest Harker Heights is an area in Harker Heights with about 3,300 residents, homes built around 1982, a mix of owners and renters and 38% of households with children.

Our grade for Northwest Harker Heights is C. That puts it 4th of 5 areas we grade in Harker Heights, a city that gets a B- overall.

Pros of living in Northwest Harker Heights

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,010, 12% lower than the Harker Heights median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Northwest Harker Heights

  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 2 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Northwest Harker Heights

Is Northwest Harker Heights a good place to live?
