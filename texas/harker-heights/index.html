What it's like to live in Harker Heights

Harker Heights sits about 5 miles from downtown Killeen, in the Killeen-Temple metro. 35,195 people lived here in 2025, up 6.4% from 2020. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2001).

Places locals use include Harker Heights Community Park, Carl Levin Park and Purser Family Park.

The median age is 33.0, 45% of households have children, 62% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $81,190 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Harker Heights

  • Low violent crime. 120 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 69% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,146, 18% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Harker Heights

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.

Cost of living in Harker Heights

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Harker Heights is $1,146 a month, 18% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $46,000 a year. Right now 49% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $927 for a one-bedroom, $1,138 for a two-bedroom and $1,576 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $287,800, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,575 before tax, or $1,887 with property tax. That is $741 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Harker Heights pays $3,743 a year, 12% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Harker Heights safe?

In 2025, Harker Heights Police Department reported 43 violent crimes and 447 property crimes. That works out to 120 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 69% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,249 property crimes per 100,000, 40% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 36% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.
