Is Fort Worth a good place to live? Common questions

Is Fort Worth, TX a good place to live?

We give Fort Worth a D. That places it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 667th of 876 nationally and 49th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and jobs and income, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Fort Worth?

Median rent in Fort Worth is $1,509 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $303,000, 7% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,091, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Fort Worth?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Fort Worth are Covey Homes Litsey Creek, Sundance Square Plaza and North Fort Worth. We score 17 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Fort Worth?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 14% of workers work from home.
