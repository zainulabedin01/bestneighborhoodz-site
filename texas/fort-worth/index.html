About Fort Worth

Fort Worth began in 1849 as an army post on a bluff above the Trinity River, named for General William J. Worth. Cattle drives later earned it the nickname Cowtown. It is the seat of Tarrant County and the western half of the Dallas to Fort Worth area, with parts of the city spilling into Denton, Johnson, Parker and Wise counties.

Most people drive I-35W, I-30 or I-20, with Loop 820 circling the city and SH 121 and SH 183 heading east. American Airlines has its headquarters here, and Lockheed Martin, BNSF Railway, Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base Fort Worth and Texas Christian University are other big employers. The Stockyards National Historic District keeps the cattle history alive, Sundance Square is the downtown gathering spot, and the city's museum area draws art lovers from across the region.

What it's like to live in Fort Worth

Fort Worth sits about 34 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 1,028,117 people lived here in 2025, up 11.9% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas.

Places locals use include Trinity Park and Fort Worth Public Library - Summerglen.

The median age is 33.6, 36% of households have children, 57% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $79,507 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Cost of living in Fort Worth

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Fort Worth is $1,509 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $60,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $303,000, 7% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,659 before tax, or $2,091 with property tax. That is $582 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Fort Worth pays $5,191 a year, 23% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Fort Worth safe?

In 2025, Fort Worth Police Department reported 3,859 violent crimes and 22,886 property crimes. That works out to 375 violent crimes per 100,000 people, about the same as the Texas rate of 387, and 2,221 property crimes per 100,000, 7% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 35% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Fort Worth

Fort Worth has 250 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 42% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Harmony Public Schools - North Texas and Fort Worth ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Harmony School of Innovation - Fort Worth, Marine Creek Collegiate High School and Texas Academy of Biomedical. The average rating score across all campuses is 77 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Living in Fort Worth: common questions

    Is Fort Worth, TX a good place to live?

    We give Fort Worth a D-. It ranks 69th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and jobs and income, and trails on health and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Fort Worth?

    Median rent in Fort Worth is $1,509 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $303,000, 7% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,091, including property tax.

    What is the weather like in Fort Worth?
