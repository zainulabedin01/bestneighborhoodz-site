What it's like to live in Nantucket Estates

Nantucket Estates is a neighborhood in Harlingen with about 1,500 residents, homes built around 1996, mostly owner-occupied (74%) and 65% of households with children.

Our grade for Nantucket Estates is C. That puts it 22nd of 24 areas we grade in Harlingen, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in Nantucket Estates

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in Nantucket Estates

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,260, 36% higher than the Harlingen median.
  • Higher poverty. 38% of residents live below the poverty line, against 25% across Harlingen.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes as very high in this part of town.
