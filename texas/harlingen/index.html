Is Harlingen, TX a good place to live?

Harlingen gets a D overall, which puts it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 670th of 876 nationally and 51st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Harlingen?

Median rent in Harlingen is $928 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $140,600, 50% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $941, including property tax.

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Harlingen are Stone Pine, Palm Vista Estates and Country Estates. We score 24 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Harlingen?

The average one-way commute is 18 min, 33% lower than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home.
