    Living in Harlingen: common questions

    Is Harlingen, TX a good place to live?

    Harlingen gets an overall grade of C+ and ranks 41st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on internet access and education levels. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Harlingen?

    Median rent in Harlingen is $928 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $140,600, 50% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $941, including property tax.

    Is Harlingen safe?

    In 2025, Harlingen Police Department reported 231 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 40% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,298 per 100,000, 10% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Harlingen?

    We found 26 rated public school campuses inside Harlingen. 81% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 70 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Harlingen?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Harlingen are Country Estates, Palm Vista Estates and Stone Pine. We score 24 areas in the city.

    Does Harlingen flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Harlingen as low, and coastal flooding as low. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Harlingen?

    Summers in Harlingen peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 51°F. Expect around 151 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 28 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Harlingen?

    The average one-way commute is 18 min, 33% lower than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home. It is about 20 miles from downtown Brownsville.
