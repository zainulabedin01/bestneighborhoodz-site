What it's like to live in Weston Oaks

Weston Oaks is a neighborhood in Harlingen with about 1,800 residents, homes built around 1994, mostly owner-occupied (71%) and 31% of households with children.

Our grade for Weston Oaks is C+. That puts it 16th of 24 areas we grade in Harlingen, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Questions about Weston Oaks

Is Weston Oaks a good place to live?

Weston Oaks is a neighborhood in Harlingen, TX, home to about 1,800 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 16th of 24 areas we score in Harlingen. It does best on schools and worst on jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in Weston Oaks?

Renters in Weston Oaks pay a median of $1,030 a month, 11% higher than the Harlingen median. The median home is worth $190,000, 35% higher than the Harlingen median. A median home costs 4.0x the median household income, compared with 2.6x across Harlingen.

The closest rated public schools are Dr Hesiquio Rodriguez Elementary (elementary, rated B), Gutierrez Middle (middle, rated B) and Harlingen H S - South (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
