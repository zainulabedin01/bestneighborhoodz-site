Is Canyon Lake a good place to live? Common questions

Is Canyon Lake, TX a good place to live?

Canyon Lake earns a C on our index, around the middle of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 1991st of 3,321 nationally and 73rd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Canyon Lake?

Median rent in Canyon Lake is $1,327 a month, 5% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $389,300, 37% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,426, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Canyon Lake?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Canyon Lake are South Canyon Lake, East Canyon Lake and North Canyon Lake. We score 9 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Canyon Lake?

The average one-way commute is 39 min, 46% higher than the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home.
