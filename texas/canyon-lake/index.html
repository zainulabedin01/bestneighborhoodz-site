    Living in Canyon Lake: common questions

    Is Canyon Lake, TX a good place to live?

    Canyon Lake earns a grade of C+ on our index, 100th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on schools and jobs and income, and trails on errands and things to do and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Canyon Lake?

    Median rent in Canyon Lake is $1,327 a month, 5% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $389,300, 37% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,426, including property tax.

    Are the schools good in Canyon Lake?

    We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Canyon Lake. 80% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 78 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Canyon Lake?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Canyon Lake are South Canyon Lake, East Canyon Lake and Northwest Canyon Lake. We score 9 areas in the city.

    Does Canyon Lake flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Canyon Lake as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Canyon Lake?

    Summers in Canyon Lake peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 103 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 38 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Canyon Lake?

    The average one-way commute is 39 min, 46% higher than the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home. It is about 33 miles from downtown San Antonio.
