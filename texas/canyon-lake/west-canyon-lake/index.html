What it's like to live in West Canyon Lake

West Canyon Lake is an area in Canyon Lake with about 2,600 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2004), mostly owner-occupied (86%) and 25% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 7th of 9 areas in Canyon Lake, which grades C+ overall.

Pros of living in West Canyon Lake

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,170, 12% lower than the Canyon Lake median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in West Canyon Lake

  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 3.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about West Canyon Lake
