Questions about Central Victoria

Is Central Victoria a good place to live?

Central Victoria covers part of Victoria, TX, home to about 34,200 people. It gets a D+, in the bottom third of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 3rd of 6 areas we score in Victoria. It does best on getting around and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Central Victoria?

Renters in Central Victoria pay a median of $1,210 a month, 3% higher than the Victoria median. The median home is worth $199,000, 4% lower than the Victoria median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.1x across Victoria.

What schools serve Central Victoria?

The closest rated public schools are Rowland Elementary (elementary, rated B), Howell Middle (middle, rated B) and Victoria East High School (high, rated C). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
