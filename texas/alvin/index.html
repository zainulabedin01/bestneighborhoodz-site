What it's like to live in Alvin

Alvin sits about 30 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 30,115 people lived here in 2025, up 11.7% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Pearson Park, Morgan Park and National Oak Park.

The median age is 33.7, 38% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $71,477 a year (9% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Alvin

  • Low violent crime. 128 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 67% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,215, 13% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Alvin

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.

Cost of living in Alvin

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Alvin is $1,215 a month, 13% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $49,000 a year. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,212 for a one-bedroom, $1,319 for a two-bedroom and $1,790 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $228,700, 19% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,252 before tax, or $1,596 with property tax. That is $381 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Alvin pays $4,130 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Alvin safe?

In 2025, Alvin Police Department reported 39 violent crimes and 367 property crimes. That works out to 128 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 67% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,200 property crimes per 100,000, 42% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 49% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Alvin

Alvin has 11 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 73% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Alvin ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Fairview Junior High. The average rating score across all campuses is 83 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
