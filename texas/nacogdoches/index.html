    Living in Nacogdoches: common questions

    Is Nacogdoches, TX a good place to live?

    Nacogdoches earns a grade of D- on our index, 166th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on getting around and errands and things to do, and trails on jobs and income and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Nacogdoches?

    Median rent in Nacogdoches is $964 a month, 31% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $186,000, 34% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,159, including property tax.

    Is Nacogdoches safe?

    In 2025, Nacogdoches Police Department reported 296 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 24% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,673 per 100,000, 20% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Nacogdoches?

    We found 9 rated public school campuses inside Nacogdoches. 22% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 12 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Nacogdoches?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Nacogdoches are Northeast Nacogdoches, Southeast Nacogdoches and North Nacogdoches. We score 6 areas in the city.

    Does Nacogdoches flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Nacogdoches as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Nacogdoches?

    Summers in Nacogdoches peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 36°F. Expect around 98 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 50 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Nacogdoches?

    The average one-way commute is 16 min, 41% lower than the Texas average. About 6.5% of workers work from home.
