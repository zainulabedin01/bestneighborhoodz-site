What it's like to live in Duncanville

Duncanville sits about 13 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 38,915 people lived here in 2025, down 4.4% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Lions Park, Marcus Harrington Park and Lakeside Park.

The median age is 36.2, 37% of households have children, 66% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $76,457 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Duncanville

  • Plenty close to home. 14 grocery stores, 147 restaurants and 30 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Duncanville

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 14% of the 14 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,630, 16% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Duncanville

Duncanville renters pay a median of $1,630 a month, 16% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $65,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 63% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $255,500, 10% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,399 before tax, or $1,723 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Duncanville pays $3,893 a year, 8% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Duncanville safe?

In 2025, Duncanville Police Department reported 101 violent crimes and 793 property crimes. That works out to 260 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 33% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 2,041 property crimes per 100,000, about the same as the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 40% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.
