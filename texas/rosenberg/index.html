Is Rosenberg a good place to live? Common questions

Is Rosenberg, TX a good place to live?

Rosenberg gets an F overall, which puts it in the bottom 8% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 3165th of 3,321 nationally and 184th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Rosenberg?

Renters in Rosenberg pay a median of $1,379 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $219,100, 23% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,482, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Rosenberg?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Rosenberg are Walnut Creek, Southeast Rosenberg and Del Webb Sweetgrass. We score 8 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Rosenberg?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 13% higher than the Texas average. About 8.7% of workers work from home.
