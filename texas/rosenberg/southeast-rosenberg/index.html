What it's like to live in Southeast Rosenberg

Southeast Rosenberg is an area in Rosenberg with about 5,500 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2014), mostly owner-occupied (95%) and 48% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 4th of 8 areas in Rosenberg, which grades D- overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Rosenberg

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $106,400, 62% higher than the Rosenberg median.

Cons of living in Southeast Rosenberg

  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 2.0 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southeast Rosenberg

Is Southeast Rosenberg a good place to live?

Southeast Rosenberg covers part of Rosenberg, TX, home to about 5,500 people. It gets a C, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 8 areas we score in Rosenberg. It does best on home ownership and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Southeast Rosenberg?

Median rent in Southeast Rosenberg is $1,350 a month, about the same as the Rosenberg median. The median home is worth $247,000, 13% higher than the Rosenberg median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.3x across Rosenberg.
