What it's like to live in Lake Cherokee

Lake Cherokee sits about 13 miles from downtown Longview, in the Longview metro. About 3,093 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Cost of living in Lake Cherokee

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lake Cherokee is $930 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $37,000 a year. Right now 37% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $921 for a one-bedroom, $1,205 for a two-bedroom and $1,448 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $345,000, 22% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,888 before tax, or $2,107 with property tax. That is $1,177 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Lake Cherokee pays $2,624 a year, 38% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
