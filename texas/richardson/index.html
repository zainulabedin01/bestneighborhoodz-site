Is Richardson a good place to live? Common questions

Is Richardson, TX a good place to live?

Richardson gets a B+ overall, which puts it in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 209th of 876 nationally and 19th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are education levels and internet access, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Richardson?

Renters in Richardson pay a median of $1,857 a month, 32% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $431,400, 52% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,902, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Richardson?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Richardson are West Richardson, East Richardson and North Richardson. We score 12 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Richardson?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 12% lower than the Texas average. About 25% of workers work from home.
