What it's like to live in East Richardson

East Richardson is an area in Richardson with about 9,500 residents, homes built around 1994, mostly owner-occupied (66%) and 26% of households with children.

Our grade for East Richardson is A. That puts it 4th of 12 areas we grade in Richardson, a city that gets an A- overall.

Pros of living in East Richardson

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $133,300, 36% higher than the Richardson median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in East Richardson

  • Higher unemployment. 8.1% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.0% across Richardson.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about East Richardson

Is East Richardson a good place to live?

East Richardson covers part of Richardson, TX, home to about 9,500 people. It gets an A, in the top 12% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 12 areas we score in Richardson. It does best on health and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in East Richardson?

Renters in East Richardson pay a median of $2,030 a month, 9% higher than the Richardson median. The median home is worth $534,000, 24% higher than the Richardson median. A median home costs 4.0x the median household income, compared with 4.4x across Richardson.
