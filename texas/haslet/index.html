Is Haslet a good place to live? Common questions

Is Haslet, TX a good place to live?

Haslet earns an A on our index, in the top 12% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 896th of 11,459 nationally and 18th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Haslet?

Renters in Haslet pay a median of $1,156 a month, 18% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $616,000, 117% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $4,111, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Haslet?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 10% higher than the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home.
