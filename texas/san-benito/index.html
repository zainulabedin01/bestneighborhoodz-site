    Living in San Benito: common questions

    Is San Benito, TX a good place to live?

    We give San Benito a C-. It ranks 116th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on internet access and education levels.

    How much does it cost to live in San Benito?

    Median rent in San Benito is $839 a month, 40% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $85,300, 70% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $574, including property tax.

    Is San Benito safe?

    In 2025, San Benito Police Department reported 203 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 48% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,488 per 100,000, 19% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in San Benito?

    We found 18 rated public school campuses inside San Benito. 72% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 71 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in San Benito?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in San Benito are Hunter's Crossing, West San Benito and Northwest San Benito. We score 6 areas in the city.

    Does San Benito flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in San Benito as low, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in San Benito?

    Summers in San Benito peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 51°F. Expect around 151 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 28 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in San Benito?

    The average one-way commute is 18 min, 34% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home. It is about 15 miles from downtown Brownsville.
