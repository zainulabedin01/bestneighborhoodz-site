What it's like to live in Piney Point Village

Piney Point Village sits about 8 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 3,141 people lived here in 2025, up 1.0% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Flag Tree Park and Carol Tree Pocket Park.

The median age is 50.7, 32% of households have children, 99% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $250,000+ a year (219% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Piney Point Village

  • High incomes. The median household earns $250,000+, 219% higher than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Healthier population. Only 5.3% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Piney Point Village

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 8.0 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Higher unemployment. 8.6% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Piney Point Village

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $2,000,000+, 605% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $10,947 before tax, or $11,781 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Piney Point Village pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
