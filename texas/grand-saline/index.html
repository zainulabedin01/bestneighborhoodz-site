Living in Grand Saline: common questions

Is Grand Saline, TX a good place to live?

Grand Saline earns a grade of C- on our index, 480th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and safety, and trails on health and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Grand Saline?

Renters in Grand Saline pay a median of $925 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $119,600, 58% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $813, including property tax.

Is Grand Saline safe?

In 2025, Grand Saline Police Department reported 90 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 77% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 390 per 100,000, 81% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Grand Saline?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Grand Saline. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 39 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Grand Saline flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Grand Saline as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Grand Saline?

Summers in Grand Saline peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 33°F. Expect around 99 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 46 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Grand Saline?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, 4% lower than the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home.
