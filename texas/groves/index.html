What it's like to live in Groves

Groves sits about 17 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. 16,907 people lived here in 2025, down 2.5% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include John Spikes Memorial Park, Hoffpuair Field and Groves Public Library.

The median age is 38.9, 35% of households have children, 78% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $73,710 a year (6% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Groves

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.2 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Groves

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.

Cost of living in Groves

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Groves is $1,286 a month, 8% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $51,000 a year. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $163,600, 42% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $895 before tax, or $1,122 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Groves pays $2,724 a year, 36% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Groves

Everyday places mapped inside Groves, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include John Spikes Memorial Park, Hoffpuair Field, Groves Public Library.

The average one-way commute is 21 minutes, 22% shorter than the Texas average. 3% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Beaumont is about 17 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Groves work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
