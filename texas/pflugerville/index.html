Is Pflugerville a good place to live? Common questions

Is Pflugerville, TX a good place to live?

Pflugerville gets a B+ overall, which puts it in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 207th of 876 nationally and 18th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Pflugerville?

Renters in Pflugerville pay a median of $1,932 a month, 38% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $402,400, 42% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,748, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Pflugerville?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Pflugerville are Bohl's Place, Old Town and Villages of Hidden Lake. We score 14 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Pflugerville?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home.
