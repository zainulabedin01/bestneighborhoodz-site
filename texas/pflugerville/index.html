What it's like to live in Pflugerville

Pflugerville sits about 14 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 68,316 people lived here in 2025, up 4.8% from 2020. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2004).

Local spots people know include Lake Pflugerville, Pflugfun and Pfluger Park.

The median age is 37.3, 41% of households have children, 70% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $117,799 a year (50% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Pflugerville

  • Low violent crime. 186 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 52% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $117,799, 50% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Pflugerville

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,932, 38% higher than the Texas median.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Pflugerville

Pflugerville renters pay a median of $1,932 a month, 38% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $77,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 50% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $402,400, 42% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,203 before tax, or $2,748 with property tax. That is $816 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Pflugerville pays $6,543 a year, 55% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Pflugerville safe?

In 2025, Pflugerville Police Department reported 125 violent crimes and 1,071 property crimes. That works out to 186 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 52% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,595 property crimes per 100,000, 24% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is up about 26% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Pflugerville

Pflugerville has 24 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 63% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Basis Texas and Pflugerville ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Basis Pflugerville, Hendrickson High School and Kelly Lane Middle. The average rating score across all campuses is 82 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Living in Pflugerville: common questions

    Is Pflugerville, TX a good place to live?

    Pflugerville gets an overall grade of B+ and ranks 25th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on internet access and jobs and income, and trails on getting around and errands and things to do.

    How much does it cost to live in Pflugerville?

    Renters in Pflugerville pay a median of $1,932 a month, 38% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $402,400, 42% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,748, including property tax.

    Is Pflugerville safe?

    In 2025, Pflugerville Police Department reported 186 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 52% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,595 per 100,000, 24% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Pflugerville?

    We found 24 rated public school campuses inside Pflugerville. 63% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 47 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Pflugerville?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Pflugerville are Villages of Hidden Lake, Old Town and Bohl's Place. We score 14 areas in the city.

    Does Pflugerville flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Pflugerville as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Pflugerville?

    Summers in Pflugerville peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 38°F. Expect around 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 37 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Pflugerville?

    The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home. It is about 14 miles from downtown Austin.
