What it's like to live in Mountain Creek

Mountain Creek is a neighborhood in Pflugerville with about 3,700 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2003), mostly owner-occupied (67%) and 50% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 10th of 14 areas in Pflugerville, which grades B+ overall.

Questions about Mountain Creek

Is Mountain Creek a good place to live?

Mountain Creek is a neighborhood in Pflugerville, TX, home to about 3,700 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 10th of 14 areas we score in Pflugerville. It does best on internet access and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Mountain Creek?

Median rent in Mountain Creek is $2,140 a month, 11% higher than the Pflugerville median. The median home is worth $323,000, 20% lower than the Pflugerville median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.4x across Pflugerville.

What schools serve Mountain Creek?

The closest rated public schools are Wieland Elementary (elementary, rated B), Pflugerville Middle (middle, rated C) and Pflugerville High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
