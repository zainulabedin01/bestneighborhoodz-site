What it's like to live in Palm Valley

Palm Valley sits about 23 miles from downtown Brownsville, in the Brownsville-Harlingen metro. 1,340 people lived here in 2025, down 4.8% from 2020. It is suburban.

The median age is 39.5, 40% of households have children, 81% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $99,438 a year (27% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Palm Valley

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Palm Valley is $1,340 a month, 4% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $54,000 a year. Right now 17% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $884 for a one-bedroom, $1,042 for a two-bedroom and $1,393 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $275,000, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,505 before tax, or $1,853 with property tax. That is $513 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Palm Valley pays $4,176 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
