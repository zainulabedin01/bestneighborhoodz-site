What it's like to live in Waskom

Waskom sits about 41 miles from downtown Longview, in the Longview metro. 1,980 people lived here in 2025, up 3.8% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Waskom Park and Lions Club Park.

The median age is 36.2, 35% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $55,781 a year (29% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Waskom

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $928, 34% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.6 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Waskom

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 33% of the 3 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 79% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Waskom

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Waskom is $928 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $37,000 a year. Right now 50% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $951 for a one-bedroom, $1,244 for a two-bedroom and $1,482 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $92,000, 68% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $504 before tax, or $572 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Waskom pays $819 a year, 81% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Waskom

Everyday places mapped inside Waskom, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Waskom Park, Lions Club Park.

The average one-way commute is 27 minutes, about the same as the Texas average. 5% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Longview is about 41 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Waskom work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
