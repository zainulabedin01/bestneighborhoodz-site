What it's like to live in Lakeside City

Lakeside City sits about 5 miles from downtown Wichita Falls, in the Wichita Falls metro. 1,307 people lived here in 2025, up 21.5% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

The median age is 36.5, 48% of households have children, 90% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $113,636 a year (45% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Lakeside City

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.4 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $113,636, 45% higher than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 21% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Lakeside City

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $2,144, 53% higher than the Texas median.

Cost of living in Lakeside City

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lakeside City is $2,144 a month, 53% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $86,000 a year. Right now 0% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $949 for a one-bedroom, $1,145 for a two-bedroom and $1,542 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $268,300, 5% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,469 before tax, or $1,798 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Lakeside City pays $3,947 a year, 7% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
