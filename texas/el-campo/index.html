What it's like to live in El Campo

El Campo is a small city in Wharton County. 12,142 people lived here in 2025, down 1.7% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Legacy Park, Friendship Park and Willie Bell Park.

The median age is 33.7, 39% of households have children, 60% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $67,017 a year (15% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in El Campo

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,071, 24% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 4 grocery stores, 75 restaurants and 11 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in El Campo

  • Higher poverty. 21% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 130 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in El Campo

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in El Campo is $1,071 a month, 24% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $43,000 a year. Right now 65% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $915 for a one-bedroom, $1,184 for a two-bedroom and $1,584 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $201,400, 29% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,102 before tax, or $1,406 with property tax. That is $335 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in El Campo pays $3,639 a year, 14% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
