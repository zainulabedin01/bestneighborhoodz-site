Rent in El Campo

HUD's fair market rents for Wharton County, TX run from $824 for a studio to $1,590 for 4 bedrooms. A two-bedroom is $1,184 a month, which takes an income of about $47,000 a year to stay at 30% of pay. HUD figures include utilities and describe a typical, modest unit.

65% of renters in El Campo already spend 30% or more of their income on rent, against 52% across Texas.

Buying a home in El Campo

Buying the median $201,400 home with 20% down ($40,280) and a 30-year loan at 7.28% costs about $1,102 a month for the loan plus $303 a month in property tax, $1,406 in all. Homeowners insurance, HOA dues and upkeep come on top.

That is $335 a month more than the median rent, so renting is cheaper month to month. Buying pays off mainly if you stay long enough for the home to build equity.

Taxes

Texas has no state income tax, which helps take-home pay. The trade-off is high property tax, set by the county, city, school district and other local bodies.

The median homeowner in El Campo pays $3,639 a year in property tax, 14% lower than the Texas median of $4,232 and 17% higher than the US median. Sales tax in most of Texas tops out at 8.25%.

Cost of living in El Campo: common questions

Is El Campo, TX expensive to live in?

El Campo is cheap by Texas standards. Median rent is $1,071 a month, 24% lower than the Texas median of $1,403, and the median home is worth $201,400, 29% lower than the Texas median of $283,800. Prices only tell half the story. The typical household here earns $67,017, so the median home costs 3.0 times a year of income, against 3.6 times across Texas and 4.1 times across the US. So for people who earn local wages, homes here are easier to afford than in most of Texas.

How much do you need to earn to live in El Campo?

To rent a median home at $1,071 a month and keep rent at 30% of income, you need about $43,000 a year. To buy the median home, the monthly cost is about $1,406, which takes around $56,000 a year by the same rule.

What is the average rent in El Campo?

The median rent across all units is $1,071 a month (Census). HUD's fair market rent is $824 for a studio, $915 for 1 bedroom, $1,184 for 2 bedrooms, $1,584 for 3 bedrooms, $1,590 for 4 bedrooms.

How much is property tax in El Campo?

The median homeowner in El Campo pays $3,639 a year in property tax, 14% lower than the Texas median of $4,232 and 17% higher than the US median. Sales tax in most of Texas tops out at 8.25%.
