How much does it cost to live in Hurst?

Median rent in Hurst is $1,358 a month, 3% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $315,200, 11% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,058, including property tax.
