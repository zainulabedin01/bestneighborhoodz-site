What it's like to live in Southeast Texas City

Southeast Texas City is an area in Texas City with about 5,600 residents, homes built around 1974, a mix of owners and renters and 28% of households with children.

It grades C- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 4th of 8 areas in Texas City, which grades D- overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Texas City

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Southeast Texas City

  • Higher poverty. 23% of residents live below the poverty line, against 16% across Texas City.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southeast Texas City

Is Southeast Texas City a good place to live?

Southeast Texas City covers part of Texas City, TX, home to about 5,600 people. It gets a C-, just below the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 8 areas we score in Texas City. It does best on cost of living and worst on education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Southeast Texas City?

Median rent in Southeast Texas City is $1,200 a month, 8% lower than the Texas City median. The median home is worth $164,000, 25% lower than the Texas City median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.2x across Texas City.

What schools serve Southeast Texas City?

The closest rated public schools are Heights Elementary (elementary, rated A), Blocker Middle (middle, rated C) and Texas City High School (high, rated C). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
