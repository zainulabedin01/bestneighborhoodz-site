    Living in Texas City: common questions

    Is Texas City, TX a good place to live?

    Texas City gets an overall grade of D- and ranks 68th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and internet access, and trails on errands and things to do and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Texas City?

    Renters in Texas City pay a median of $1,307 a month, 7% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $217,300, 23% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,417, including property tax.

    Are the schools good in Texas City?

    We found 15 rated public school campuses inside Texas City. 40% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 37 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Texas City?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Texas City are Northwest Texas City, Lago Mar and East Texas City. We score 8 areas in the city.

    Does Texas City flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Texas City as low, and coastal flooding as very high. Hazards rated high or very high here: coastal flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Texas City?

    Summers in Texas City peak at about 92°F in July, and January nights fall to about 46°F. Expect around 76 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 60 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Texas City?

    The average one-way commute is 26 min, 4% lower than the Texas average. About 5.8% of workers work from home. It is about 36 miles from downtown Houston.
