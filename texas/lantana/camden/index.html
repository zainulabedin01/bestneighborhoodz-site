What it's like to live in Camden

Camden is a neighborhood in Lantana with about 1,800 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2006), mostly owner-occupied (95%) and 54% of households with children.

It grades A- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 3rd of 3 areas in Lantana, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in Camden

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.5 miles away.

Cons of living in Camden

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Camden

Is Camden a good place to live?

Camden is a neighborhood in Lantana, TX, home to about 1,700 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 3rd of 3 areas we score in Lantana. It does best on jobs and income and worst on internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Camden?

Median rent in Camden is $3,130 a month, 9% lower than the Lantana median. The median home is worth $586,000, about the same as the Lantana median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 2.9x across Lantana.

What schools serve Camden?

The closest rated public schools are Ep Rayzor Elementary (elementary, rated B), Harpool Middle (middle, rated A) and Guyer High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
