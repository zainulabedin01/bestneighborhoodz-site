Is Little Elm a good place to live? Common questions

Is Little Elm, TX a good place to live?

Little Elm earns a B on our index, in the top 36% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 280th of 876 nationally and 24th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Little Elm?

Renters in Little Elm pay a median of $1,953 a month, 39% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $420,700, 48% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,921, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Little Elm?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Little Elm are West Little Elm, North Little Elm and Northwest Little Elm. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Little Elm?

The average one-way commute is 35 min, 30% higher than the Texas average. About 27% of workers work from home.
