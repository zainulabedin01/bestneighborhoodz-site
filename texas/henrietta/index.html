How much does it cost to live in Henrietta?

Median rent in Henrietta is $790 a month, 44% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $145,600, 49% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $962, including property tax.
