What it's like to live in Schulenburg

Schulenburg is a town in Fayette County. 2,837 people lived here in 2025, up 6.1% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Schonest - Welt Park, Wolters Park and Schulenburg Sports Complex.

The median age is 41.3, 30% of households have children, 59% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $51,294 a year (35% lower than the Texas median).

Cons of living in Schulenburg

Cost of living in Schulenburg

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Schulenburg is $957 a month, 32% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $38,000 a year. Right now 40% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $933 for a one-bedroom, $1,093 for a two-bedroom and $1,473 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $205,900, 27% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,127 before tax, or $1,302 with property tax. That is $345 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Schulenburg pays $2,105 a year, 50% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
