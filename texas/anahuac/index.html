What it's like to live in Anahuac

Anahuac sits about 43 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 2,023 people lived here in 2025, up 2.5% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Fort Anahuac Park and Chambers County Library.

The median age is 31.6, 39% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $77,500 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Cost of living in Anahuac

Anahuac renters pay a median of $1,566 a month, 12% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $63,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 9% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $226,900, 20% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,242 before tax, or $1,444 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Anahuac pays $2,428 a year, 43% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Anahuac

Everyday places mapped inside Anahuac, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Fort Anahuac Park, Chambers County Library.

The average one-way commute is 27 minutes, about the same as the Texas average. 4% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Houston is about 43 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Anahuac work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $77,500, income per person is $28,804, unemployment is 4.5% and 9% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
