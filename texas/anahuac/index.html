Is Anahuac, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Anahuac?

Renters in Anahuac pay a median of $1,566 a month, 12% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $226,900, 20% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,444, including property tax.
