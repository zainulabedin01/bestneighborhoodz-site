What it's like to live in Little Cypress

Little Cypress sits about 24 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. About 1,963 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 41.6, 38% of households have children, 88% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $91,199 a year (16% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Little Cypress

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $867, 38% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.0 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Little Cypress

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding, hurricanes and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Little Cypress

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Little Cypress is $867 a month, 38% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $35,000 a year. Right now 44% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $183,900, 35% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,007 before tax, or $1,190 with property tax. That is $323 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Little Cypress pays $2,202 a year, 48% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
