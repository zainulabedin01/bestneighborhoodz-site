Is Beaumont a good place to live? Common questions

Is Beaumont, TX a good place to live?

Beaumont gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 741st of 876 nationally and 65th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Beaumont?

Renters in Beaumont pay a median of $1,121 a month, 20% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $166,400, 41% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,130, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Beaumont?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Beaumont are Central Beaumont, Calder Terrace and Northwest Beaumont. We score 23 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Beaumont?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 24% lower than the Texas average. About 4.5% of workers work from home.
