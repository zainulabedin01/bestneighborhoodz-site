What it's like to live in West Beaumont

West Beaumont is an area in Beaumont with about 9,700 residents, homes built around 1982, mostly owner-occupied (66%) and 21% of households with children.

Our grade for West Beaumont is C-. That puts it 7th of 23 areas we grade in Beaumont, a city that gets an F overall.

Cons of living in West Beaumont

Questions about West Beaumont

Is West Beaumont a good place to live?

West Beaumont covers part of Beaumont, TX, home to about 9,700 people. It gets a C-, just below the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 7th of 23 areas we score in Beaumont. It does best on internet access and worst on schools.

How much does it cost to live in West Beaumont?

Renters in West Beaumont pay a median of $1,610 a month, 44% higher than the Beaumont median. The median home is worth $203,000, 22% higher than the Beaumont median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 2.9x across Beaumont.

What schools serve West Beaumont?

The closest rated public schools are Amelia Elementary (elementary, rated D), Vincent Middle (middle, rated F) and West Brook Sr High School (high, rated C). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
