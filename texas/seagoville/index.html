What it's like to live in Seagoville

Seagoville sits about 16 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 21,077 people lived here in 2025, up 14.2% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Bruce Central Park, Bearden Park and Heard Park.

The median age is 33.2, 49% of households have children, 78% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $66,743 a year (15% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Seagoville

  • Low violent crime. 93 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 76% lower than the Texas rate.

Cons of living in Seagoville

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 38 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Many adults uninsured. 27% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Seagoville

Seagoville renters pay a median of $1,381 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $55,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 59% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $230,400, 19% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,261 before tax, or $1,544 with property tax. That is $163 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Seagoville pays $3,398 a year, 20% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
