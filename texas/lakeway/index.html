What it's like to live in Lakeway

Lakeway sits about 14 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 19,027 people lived here in 2025, down 0.9% from 2020. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2004).

Places locals use include Lakeway City Park, Dragon Park and Porpoise Park.

The median age is 49.8, 34% of households have children, 86% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $190,060 a year (142% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Lakeway

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 5 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 68 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 82% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $190,060, 142% higher than the Texas median.
  • Healthier population. Only 6.4% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Lakeway

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $2,981, 112% higher than the Texas median.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Lakeway

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lakeway is $2,981 a month, 112% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $119,000 a year. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $841,300, 196% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $4,605 before tax, or $5,438 with property tax. That is $2,457 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Lakeway pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Lakeway safe?

In 2025, Lakeway Police Department reported 13 violent crimes and 159 property crimes. That works out to 68 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 82% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 836 property crimes per 100,000, 60% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 12% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Lakeway

Lakeway has 5 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Lake Travis ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Lake Travis Middle, Rough Hollow Elementary and Serene Hills Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 89 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
