What it's like to live in McDade

McDade sits about 30 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 1,070 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 39.5, 38% of households have children, 93% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $50,489 a year (36% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in McDade

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,208, 14% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in McDade

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 6.2 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 31 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in McDade

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in McDade is $1,208 a month, 14% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $48,000 a year. Right now 32% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $314,300, 11% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,720 before tax, or $2,081 with property tax. That is $873 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in McDade pays $4,326 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
