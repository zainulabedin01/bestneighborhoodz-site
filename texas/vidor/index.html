What it's like to live in Vidor

Vidor sits about 9 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. 9,597 people lived here in 2025, down 1.6% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Vidor Pirates Park, Vidor Public Library and Vidor HS Library.

The median age is 40.4, 30% of households have children, 70% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $62,192 a year (21% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Vidor

  • Low violent crime. 197 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 49% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $820, 42% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.1 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Vidor

  • Higher unemployment. 7.4% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.

Cost of living in Vidor

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Vidor is $820 a month, 42% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $33,000 a year. Right now 55% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $131,400, 54% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $719 before tax, or $879 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Vidor pays $1,918 a year, 55% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

In 2025, Vidor Police Department reported 19 violent crimes and 186 property crimes. That works out to 197 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 49% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,924 property crimes per 100,000, 8% lower than the state rate.
