What it's like to live in Northwest Seguin

Northwest Seguin is an area in Seguin with about 1,100 residents, homes built around 1990, mostly owner-occupied (94%) and 21% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 6 areas in Seguin, which grades D overall.

Pros of living in Northwest Seguin

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $102,700, 58% higher than the Seguin median.

Cons of living in Northwest Seguin

Questions about Northwest Seguin

Is Northwest Seguin a good place to live?
