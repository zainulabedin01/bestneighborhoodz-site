    Living in Seguin: common questions

    Is Seguin, TX a good place to live?

    We give Seguin a D. It ranks 155th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and cost of living, and trails on health and education levels.

    How much does it cost to live in Seguin?

    Renters in Seguin pay a median of $1,187 a month, 15% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $249,400, 12% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,667, including property tax.

    Is Seguin safe?

    In 2025, Seguin Police Department reported 409 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 6% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,533 per 100,000, 26% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Seguin?

    We found 10 rated public school campuses inside Seguin. 40% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 19 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Seguin?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Seguin are Northwest Seguin, South Seguin and Joye Subdivision. We score 6 areas in the city.

    Does Seguin flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Seguin as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Seguin?

    Summers in Seguin peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 128 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 31 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Seguin?

    The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 9.7% of workers work from home. It is about 35 miles from downtown San Antonio.
