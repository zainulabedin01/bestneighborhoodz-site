Is Seguin a good place to live? Common questions

Is Seguin, TX a good place to live?

We give Seguin a D-. That places it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2844th of 3,321 nationally and 138th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Seguin?

Renters in Seguin pay a median of $1,187 a month, 15% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $249,400, 12% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,667, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Seguin?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Seguin are Northwest Seguin, South Seguin and Villa Vista. We score 6 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Seguin?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 9.7% of workers work from home.
