Is San Angelo a good place to live? Common questions

Is San Angelo, TX a good place to live?

San Angelo gets a C+ overall, which puts it around the middle of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 456th of 876 nationally and 35th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is internet access.

How much does it cost to live in San Angelo?

Renters in San Angelo pay a median of $1,153 a month, 18% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $187,600, 34% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,240, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in San Angelo?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in San Angelo are Butler Farms, Southland and Nasworthy. We score 21 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in San Angelo?

The average one-way commute is 19 min, 28% lower than the Texas average. About 9.1% of workers work from home.
