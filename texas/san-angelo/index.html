    Living in San Angelo: common questions

    Is San Angelo, TX a good place to live?

    San Angelo gets an overall grade of C- and ranks 49th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and getting around, and trails on schools and internet access. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in San Angelo?

    Renters in San Angelo pay a median of $1,153 a month, 18% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $187,600, 34% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,240, including property tax.

    Is San Angelo safe?

    In 2025, San Angelo Police Department reported 287 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 26% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,187 per 100,000, 5% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in San Angelo?

    We found 21 rated public school campuses inside San Angelo. 24% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 6 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in San Angelo?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in San Angelo are Butler Farms, Southland and Bonham. We score 21 areas in the city.

    Does San Angelo flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in San Angelo as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in San Angelo?

    Summers in San Angelo peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 119 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 21 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in San Angelo?

    The average one-way commute is 19 min, 28% lower than the Texas average. About 9.1% of workers work from home.
