What it's like to live in Hillsboro

Hillsboro is a town in Hill County. 9,036 people lived here in 2025, up 10.5% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Wallace Park, Hillsboro City Park and Carter Davis Park.

The median age is 35.1, 33% of households have children, 54% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $56,083 a year (29% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Hillsboro

  • Strong public schools. 75% of the 4 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 166 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 57% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $980, 30% lower than the Texas median.
  • Plenty close to home. 2 grocery stores, 67 restaurants and 7 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Hillsboro

  • Higher poverty. 20% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Hillsboro

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Hillsboro is $980 a month, 30% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $39,000 a year. Right now 58% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $858 for a one-bedroom, $1,080 for a two-bedroom and $1,495 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $157,900, 44% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $864 before tax, or $1,073 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Hillsboro pays $2,510 a year, 41% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Hillsboro safe?

In 2025, Hillsboro Police Department reported 15 violent crimes and 155 property crimes. That works out to 166 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 57% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,711 property crimes per 100,000, 18% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is up about 41% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Hillsboro

Hillsboro has 4 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 75% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Hillsboro ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Hillsboro High School and Hillsboro Junior High. The average rating score across all campuses is 89 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
