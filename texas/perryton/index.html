Living in Perryton: common questions

Is Perryton, TX a good place to live?

We give Perryton a B+. It ranks 163rd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and schools, and trails on health and education levels. It is a strong pick for young professionals.

How much does it cost to live in Perryton?

Renters in Perryton pay a median of $1,005 a month, 28% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $126,500, 55% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $851, including property tax.

Is Perryton safe?

In 2025, Perryton Police Department reported 257 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 34% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 135 per 100,000, 94% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Perryton?

We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Perryton. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 70 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Perryton flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Perryton as very low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Perryton?

Summers in Perryton peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 24°F. Expect around 74 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 23 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Perryton?
