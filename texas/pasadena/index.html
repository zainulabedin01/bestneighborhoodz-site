Is Pasadena a good place to live? Common questions

Is Pasadena, TX a good place to live?

We give Pasadena a D-. That places it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 794th of 876 nationally and 71st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and internet access, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Pasadena?

Renters in Pasadena pay a median of $1,213 a month, 14% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $209,600, 26% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,389, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Pasadena?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Pasadena are Southeast Pasadena, East Pasadena and River Oaks. We score 13 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Pasadena?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 7.9% of workers work from home.
