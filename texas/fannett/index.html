What it's like to live in Fannett

Fannett sits about 12 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. About 1,654 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2000).

The median age is 48.5, 24% of households have children, 89% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $90,962 a year (16% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Fannett

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $230,900, 19% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,264 before tax, or $1,608 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Fannett pays $4,124 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
