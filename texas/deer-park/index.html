What it's like to live in Deer Park

Deer Park sits about 18 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 34,282 people lived here in 2025, down 0.7% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Bayou Bend Park, Dow Park Volleyball Sand Court and Park Meadows Park.

The median age is 38.0, 34% of households have children, 78% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $100,382 a year (28% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Deer Park

  • Low violent crime. 99 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 75% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $100,382, 28% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Deer Park

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.

Cost of living in Deer Park

Deer Park renters pay a median of $1,318 a month, 6% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $53,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 62% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $264,600, 7% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,448 before tax, or $1,778 with property tax. That is $460 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Deer Park pays $3,954 a year, 7% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Deer Park safe?

In 2025, Deer Park Police Department reported 34 violent crimes and 355 property crimes. That works out to 99 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 75% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,028 property crimes per 100,000, 51% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 38% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Deer Park

Top-rated campuses include College Park Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 82 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.
