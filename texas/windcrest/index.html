What it's like to live in Windcrest

Windcrest sits about 9 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 5,809 people lived here in 2025, down 0.8% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Windcrest Duck Pond, Brook Falls Park and Takas Park.

The median age is 45.2, 18% of households have children, 87% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $89,844 a year (14% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Windcrest

  • Low violent crime. 208 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 46% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Windcrest

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 33% of the 3 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Windcrest

Windcrest renters pay a median of $1,372 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $55,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 55% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $302,000, 6% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,653 before tax, or $2,039 with property tax. That is $667 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Windcrest pays $4,635 a year, 10% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Windcrest
