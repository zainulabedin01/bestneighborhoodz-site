What it's like to live in Springtown

Springtown sits about 54 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 5,204 people lived here in 2025, up 70.0% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Springtown Park, Springtown Skatepark and Springtown Public Library.

The median age is 29.8, 41% of households have children, 68% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $75,391 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Springtown

  • Low violent crime. 111 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 71% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 70% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Springtown

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 43 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Springtown

Springtown renters pay a median of $1,329 a month, 5% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $53,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 40% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $261,300, 8% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,430 before tax, or $1,794 with property tax. That is $465 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Springtown pays $4,367 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
