Living in Olmos Park: common questions

Is Olmos Park, TX a good place to live?

We give Olmos Park an A. It ranks 51st of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on education levels and internet access, and trails on cost of living and getting around. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

How much does it cost to live in Olmos Park?

Renters in Olmos Park pay a median of $1,171 a month, 17% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $896,300, 216% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 5.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $5,739, including property tax.

Is Olmos Park safe?

In 2025, Olmos Park Police Department reported 47 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 88% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 232 per 100,000, 89% lower than the Texas rate.

Does Olmos Park flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Olmos Park as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Olmos Park?

Summers in Olmos Park peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 34 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Olmos Park?

The average one-way commute is 18 min, 32% lower than the Texas average. About 20% of workers work from home. It is about 2 miles from downtown San Antonio.
