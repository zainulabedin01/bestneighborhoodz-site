What it's like to live in Winnsboro

Winnsboro is a town in Wood County. 3,678 people lived here in 2025, up 6.7% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Winnsboro City Park and Gilbreath Memorial Library.

The median age is 36.8, 42% of households have children, 53% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $54,828 a year (30% lower than the Texas median).

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $877, 37% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Winnsboro

Cost of living in Winnsboro

Winnsboro renters pay a median of $877 a month, 37% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $35,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 47% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $862 for a one-bedroom, $1,019 for a two-bedroom and $1,294 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $172,000, 39% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $941 before tax, or $1,112 with property tax. That is $235 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Winnsboro pays $2,045 a year, 52% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
