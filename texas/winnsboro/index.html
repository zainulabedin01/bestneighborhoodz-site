Is Winnsboro a good place to live? Common questions

Is Winnsboro, TX a good place to live?

Winnsboro gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 9733rd of 11,459 nationally and 530th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and internet access, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Winnsboro?

Renters in Winnsboro pay a median of $877 a month, 37% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $172,000, 39% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,112, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Winnsboro?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 9% lower than the Texas average. About 15% of workers work from home.
