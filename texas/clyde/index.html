Is Clyde, TX a good place to live?

We give Clyde a D+. That places it in the bottom 32% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 8251st of 11,459 nationally and 363rd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and cost of living, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Clyde?

Renters in Clyde pay a median of $1,017 a month, 28% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $151,200, 47% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $960, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Clyde?

The average one-way commute is 35 min, 30% higher than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
