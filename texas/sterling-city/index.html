Cost of living in Sterling City

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Sterling City is $952 a month, 32% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $38,000 a year. Right now 71% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $943 for a one-bedroom, $1,185 for a two-bedroom and $1,412 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $134,800, 53% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $738 before tax, or $906 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Sterling City pays $2,021 a year, 52% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
