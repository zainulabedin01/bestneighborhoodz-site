What it's like to live in Justin

Justin sits about 37 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 7,285 people lived here in 2025, up 65.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2011).

Local spots people know include Bishop Park, Tally Park and Justin Park.

The median age is 35.2, 50% of households have children, 84% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $114,157 a year (45% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Justin

  • Low violent crime. 119 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 69% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $114,157, 45% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 66% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Justin

Cost of living in Justin

Justin renters pay a median of $2,000 a month, 43% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $80,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 43% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $406,800, 43% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,227 before tax, or $2,811 with property tax. That is $811 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Justin pays $7,014 a year, 66% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
