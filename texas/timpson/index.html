Cost of living in Timpson

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Timpson is $619 a month, 56% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $25,000 a year. Right now 35% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $878 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $82,000, 71% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $449 before tax, or $534 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.
