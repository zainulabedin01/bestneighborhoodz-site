What it's like to live in Liberty City

Liberty City sits about 12 miles from downtown Longview, in the Longview metro. About 2,648 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Liberty City Park and Hugh Camp Memorial Park.

The median age is 38.7, 41% of households have children, 79% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $63,092 a year (20% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Liberty City

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,218, 13% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Liberty City

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 25% of the 4 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher unemployment. 10% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Liberty City

Liberty City renters pay a median of $1,218 a month, 13% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $49,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 12% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,041 for a one-bedroom, $1,232 for a two-bedroom and $1,611 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $200,000, 30% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,095 before tax, or $1,233 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Liberty City pays $1,660 a year, 61% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Liberty City

Everyday places mapped inside Liberty City, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Liberty City Park, Hugh Camp Memorial Park.

The average one-way commute is 22 minutes, 17% shorter than the Texas average. 7% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Longview is about 12 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Liberty City work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $63,092, income per person is $30,643, unemployment is 10% and 20% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.

Living in Liberty City: common questions

Is Liberty City, TX a good place to live?

We give Liberty City a D+. It ranks 532nd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and education levels, and trails on internet access and jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in Liberty City?

Renters in Liberty City pay a median of $1,218 a month, 13% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $200,000, 30% lower than the Texas median.
