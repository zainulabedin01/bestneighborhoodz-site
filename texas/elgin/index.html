What it's like to live in Elgin

Elgin sits about 22 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 13,327 people lived here in 2025, up 37.4% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Thomas Memorial Park, Elgin Memorial Park and Veterans' Memorial Park.

The median age is 36.6, 32% of households have children, 71% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $91,114 a year (16% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Elgin

  • Low violent crime. 204 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 47% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Plenty close to home. 6 grocery stores, 75 restaurants and 12 parks are mapped in town.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 37% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Elgin

  • Weaker school ratings. None of the 6 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 32 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Elgin

Elgin renters pay a median of $1,440 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $58,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $326,400, 15% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,787 before tax, or $2,320 with property tax. That is $880 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Elgin pays $6,400 a year, 51% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
