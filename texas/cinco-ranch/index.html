What it's like to live in Cinco Ranch

Cinco Ranch sits about 23 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 18,611 people live here. It is densely built.

Local spots people know include Willow Fork Park, Cinco Ranch South Lake Park and Greenway Village Gazebo Park.

The median age is 40.3, 51% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $161,972 a year (106% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Cinco Ranch

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 5 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $161,972, 106% higher than the Texas median.
  • Plenty close to home. 3 grocery stores, 24 restaurants and 14 parks are mapped in town.
  • Healthier population. Only 8.3% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Cinco Ranch

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,986, 42% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Cinco Ranch

Cinco Ranch renters pay a median of $1,986 a month, 42% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $79,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 35% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $475,600, 68% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,603 before tax, or $3,376 with property tax. That is $1,390 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Cinco Ranch pays $9,267 a year, 119% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
