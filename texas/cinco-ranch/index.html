Is Cinco Ranch a good place to live? Common questions

Is Cinco Ranch, TX a good place to live?

Cinco Ranch earns an A- on our index, in the top 20% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 411th of 3,321 nationally and 15th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Cinco Ranch?

Renters in Cinco Ranch pay a median of $1,986 a month, 42% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $475,600, 68% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,376, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Cinco Ranch?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Cinco Ranch are Fairway Manor, Cottage Grove and Prairie Grove. We score 10 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Cinco Ranch?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 14% higher than the Texas average. About 29% of workers work from home.
