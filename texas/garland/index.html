Is Garland a good place to live? Common questions

Is Garland, TX a good place to live?

We give Garland a D+. That places it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 606th of 876 nationally and 44th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Garland?

Renters in Garland pay a median of $1,641 a month, 17% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $270,800, 5% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,838, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Garland?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Garland are North Garland, SpringPark and Northeast Garland. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Garland?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 11% higher than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
