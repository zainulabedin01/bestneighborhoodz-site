What it's like to live in Morgan's Landing

Morgan's Landing is a neighborhood in La Porte with about 2,200 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2002), mostly owner-occupied (100%) and 30% of households with children.

Our grade for Morgan's Landing is A-. That puts it 2nd of 11 areas we grade in La Porte, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in Morgan's Landing

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $143,600, 78% higher than the La Porte median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about under 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in Morgan's Landing
