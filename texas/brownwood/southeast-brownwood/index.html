What it's like to live in Southeast Brownwood

Southeast Brownwood is an area in Brownwood with about 1,000 residents, older homes (median year built 1960), a mix of owners and renters and 28% of households with children.

It grades D+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 2nd of 4 areas in Brownwood, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Brownwood

  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Southeast Brownwood

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,060, 20% higher than the Brownwood median.
  • Higher unemployment. 16% of the labor force is out of work, against 4.8% across Brownwood.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 22 minutes, against 14 for Brownwood as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail, winter storms and strong wind as very high in this part of town.
