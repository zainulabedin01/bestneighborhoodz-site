What it's like to live in Brownwood

Brownwood is a small city in Brown County. 18,635 people lived here in 2025, down 1.3% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Riverside Park, Allcorn Park and Coggin Park.

The median age is 37.6, 28% of households have children, 55% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $48,981 a year (38% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Brownwood

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $882, 37% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 14 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Brownwood

  • Higher poverty. 21% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail, winter storms and strong wind as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 116 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Brownwood

Brownwood renters pay a median of $882 a month, 37% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $35,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 57% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $869 for a one-bedroom, $1,137 for a two-bedroom and $1,468 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $144,400, 49% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $790 before tax, or $974 with property tax. That is $92 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Brownwood pays $2,208 a year, 48% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Brownwood safe?

In 2025, Brownwood Police Department reported 79 violent crimes and 439 property crimes. That works out to 421 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 9% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 2,340 property crimes per 100,000, 12% higher than the state rate.
