Living in Bay City: common questions

Is Bay City, TX a good place to live?

Bay City gets an overall grade of D- and ranks 167th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on cost of living and getting around, and trails on jobs and income and health.

How much does it cost to live in Bay City?

Median rent in Bay City is $1,025 a month, 27% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $160,800, 43% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,114, including property tax.

Is Bay City safe?

In 2025, Bay City Police Department reported 410 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 6% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,708 per 100,000, 18% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Bay City?

We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Bay City. 20% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 27 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

Does Bay City flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Bay City as high, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Bay City?

Summers in Bay City peak at about 91°F in July, and January nights fall to about 43°F. Expect around 88 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 49 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Bay City?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 23% lower than the Texas average. About 4.8% of workers work from home.
