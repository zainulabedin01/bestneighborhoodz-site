What it's like to live in San Marcos

San Marcos sits about 31 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 77,830 people lived here in 2025, up 14.9% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2001).

Places locals use include City Park, Five Mile Dam and Ramon Lucio Park.

The median age is 25.5, 19% of households have children, 31% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $51,281 a year (35% lower than the Texas median).

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 6.1 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Higher poverty. 27% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in San Marcos

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in San Marcos is $1,322 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $53,000 a year. Right now 67% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $310,400, 9% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,699 before tax, or $2,097 with property tax. That is $775 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in San Marcos pays $4,774 a year, 13% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is San Marcos safe?

In 2025, San Marcos Police Department reported 320 violent crimes and 1,473 property crimes. That works out to 422 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 9% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,940 property crimes per 100,000, 7% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 23% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.
