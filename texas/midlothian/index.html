    Living in Midlothian: common questions

    Is Midlothian, TX a good place to live?

    We give Midlothian a B+. It ranks 47th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on home ownership and jobs and income, and trails on errands and things to do and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Midlothian?

    Renters in Midlothian pay a median of $2,013 a month, 43% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $411,800, 45% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,786, including property tax.

    Is Midlothian safe?

    In 2025, Midlothian Police Department reported 99 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 74% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 868 per 100,000, 58% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Midlothian?

    We found 12 rated public school campuses inside Midlothian. 83% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 70 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Midlothian?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Midlothian are South Midlothian, Kensington Park and Southeast Midlothian. We score 8 areas in the city.

    Does Midlothian flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Midlothian as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Midlothian?

    Summers in Midlothian peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 96 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 41 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Midlothian?

    The average one-way commute is 32 min, 19% higher than the Texas average. About 16% of workers work from home. It is about 26 miles from downtown Dallas.
