Is Galveston a good place to live? Common questions

Is Galveston, TX a good place to live?

Galveston earns a D on our index, in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 712th of 876 nationally and 62nd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are getting around and education levels, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Galveston?

Renters in Galveston pay a median of $1,298 a month, 7% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $326,000, 15% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 5.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,077, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Galveston?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Galveston are Pirates Cove, Southwest Galveston and Sea Isle. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Galveston?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 24% lower than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
