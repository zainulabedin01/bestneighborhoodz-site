What it's like to live in Pirates Cove

Pirates Cove is a neighborhood in Galveston with about 2,100 residents, homes built around 1984, mostly owner-occupied (91%) and 4% of households with children.

It grades B- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 7 areas in Galveston, which grades D overall.

Pros of living in Pirates Cove

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $121,500, 118% higher than the Galveston median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Pirates Cove

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,170, 67% higher than the Galveston median.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 25 minutes, against 20 for Galveston as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, coastal flooding and hurricanes as very high in this part of town.
