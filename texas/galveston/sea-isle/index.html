What it's like to live in Sea Isle

Sea Isle is a neighborhood in Galveston with about 1,400 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2001), mostly owner-occupied (95%) and 2% of households with children.

Our grade for Sea Isle is D+. That puts it 4th of 7 areas we grade in Galveston, a city that gets a D overall.

Pros of living in Sea Isle

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $111,100, 100% higher than the Galveston median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Sea Isle

  • Higher unemployment. 17% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.8% across Galveston.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 26 minutes, against 20 for Galveston as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding, hurricanes and wildfires as very high in this part of town.
