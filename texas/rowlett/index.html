Is Rowlett a good place to live? Common questions

Is Rowlett, TX a good place to live?

Rowlett earns a B+ on our index, in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 182nd of 876 nationally and 15th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Rowlett?

Renters in Rowlett pay a median of $1,990 a month, 42% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $368,800, 30% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,508, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Rowlett?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Rowlett are Northeast Rowlett, Southwest Rowlett and Southeast Rowlett. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Rowlett?

The average one-way commute is 31 min, 17% higher than the Texas average. About 18% of workers work from home.
