Is Paris a good place to live? Common questions

Is Paris, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Paris?

Renters in Paris pay a median of $971 a month, 31% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $150,000, 47% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $953, including property tax.
