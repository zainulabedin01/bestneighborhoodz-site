Cost of living in Centerville

Centerville renters pay a median of $987 a month, 30% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $39,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 55% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $807 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,326 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $200,500, 29% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,097 before tax, or $1,172 with property tax. That is $185 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Centerville pays $898 a year, 79% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
