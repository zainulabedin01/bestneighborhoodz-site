About Lewisville

On the south shore of Lewisville Lake, this Denton County city also dips into Dallas County and sits just north of DFW Airport. The first settlement was called Holford's Prairie. It was later renamed for Basdeal Lewis, who bought land here in the 1850s, and the city incorporated in 1925. Growth picked up after the Garza-Little Elm Dam was finished in 1954 and created the lake, and again after DFW Airport opened in 1974.

Interstate 35E runs straight through town, with State Highway 121, the Sam Rayburn Tollway, close by. Main Street, FM 407 and FM 3040 carry local traffic. The DCTA A-train stops at Old Town, Hebron and Highland Village/Lewisville Lake stations. JPMorgan Chase and Lewisville ISD are among the largest employers. People spend weekends at the lake, the 2,600-acre Lewisville Lake Environmental Learning Area, Railroad Park and the Old Town district, with Vista Ridge Mall for shopping.

What it's like to live in Lewisville

Lewisville sits about 21 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 139,006 people lived here in 2025, up 10.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Lewisville Lake Park, East Hill Park and Lake Park soccer complex.

The median age is 35.0, 32% of households have children, 46% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $89,233 a year (14% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Lewisville

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lewisville is $1,670 a month, 19% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $67,000 a year. Right now 51% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $378,500, 33% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,072 before tax, or $2,555 with property tax. That is $885 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Lewisville pays $5,799 a year, 37% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Lewisville safe?

In 2025, Lewisville Police Department reported 302 violent crimes and 1,880 property crimes. That works out to 218 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 44% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,358 property crimes per 100,000, 35% lower than the state rate.

Schools in Lewisville

Lewisville has 26 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 35% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Lewisville ISD and Texas College Preparatory Academies.

Top-rated campuses include Castle Hills Elementary, Founders Classical Academy - Lewisville and STEM Academy at Memorial Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 74 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.
