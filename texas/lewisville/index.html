Is Lewisville a good place to live? Common questions

Is Lewisville, TX a good place to live?

We give Lewisville a B-. That places it in the top 44% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 323rd of 876 nationally and 29th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Lewisville?

Median rent in Lewisville is $1,670 a month, 19% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $378,500, 33% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,555, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Lewisville?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Lewisville are Southwest Lewisville, East Lewisville and Reserve at Timber Creek. We score 17 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Lewisville?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 8% lower than the Texas average. About 19% of workers work from home.
