Living in Sinton: common questions

Is Sinton, TX a good place to live?

Sinton gets an overall grade of D and ranks 624th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and cost of living, and trails on education levels and health.

How much does it cost to live in Sinton?

Renters in Sinton pay a median of $938 a month, 33% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $142,000, 50% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $951, including property tax.

Is Sinton safe?

In 2025, Sinton Police Department reported 194 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 50% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,303 per 100,000, 38% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Sinton?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Sinton. 75% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 35 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Sinton flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Sinton as very high, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Sinton?

Summers in Sinton peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 45°F. Expect around 113 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 33 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Sinton?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 6.0% of workers work from home. It is about 23 miles from downtown Corpus Christi.
