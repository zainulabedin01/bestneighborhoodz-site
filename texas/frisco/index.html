    Living in Frisco: common questions

    Is Frisco, TX a good place to live?

    Frisco gets an overall grade of A+ and ranks 2nd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on internet access and education levels, and trails on getting around and cost of living. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Frisco?

    Median rent in Frisco is $2,014 a month, 44% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $642,100, 126% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $4,312, including property tax.

    Is Frisco safe?

    In 2025, Frisco Police Department reported 98 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 75% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 790 per 100,000, 62% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Frisco?

    We found 67 rated public school campuses inside Frisco. 91% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 94 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Frisco?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Frisco are The Grove Frisco, West Frisco and Liberty Crossing. We score 15 areas in the city.

    Does Frisco flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Frisco as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Frisco?

    Summers in Frisco peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 36°F. Expect around 93 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 42 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Frisco?

    The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 38% of workers work from home. It is about 25 miles from downtown Dallas.
