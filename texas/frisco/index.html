Is Frisco a good place to live? Common questions

Is Frisco, TX a good place to live?

Frisco gets an A overall, which puts it in the top 12% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 77th of 876 nationally and 4th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Frisco?

Median rent in Frisco is $2,014 a month, 44% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $642,100, 126% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $4,312, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Frisco?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Frisco are The Grove Frisco, West Frisco and The T's. We score 15 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Frisco?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 38% of workers work from home.
