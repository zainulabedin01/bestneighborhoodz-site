Cost of living in Carrizo Hill

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $807 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,310 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $18,200, 94% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $100 before tax, or $141 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Carrizo Hill pays $497 a year, 88% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
