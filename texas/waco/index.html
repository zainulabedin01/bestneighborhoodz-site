About Waco

Waco sits on the Brazos River about halfway between Dallas and Austin, and it is the county seat of McLennan County. The name comes from the Waco people, a Wichita band who lived here before the city was founded in 1856. The Waco Suspension Bridge, a 475-foot brick span finished in 1870, helped turn the town into a center for trade and settlement.

Interstate 35 is the road everyone knows, running north to south through the city. State Highway 6 and US 84 handle travel the other directions. Baylor University is the biggest employer, followed by Ascension Providence hospital, Waco ISD and H-E-B. Baylor football plays at McLane Stadium. Cameron Park covers 416 acres of trails and cliffs along the Brazos. Visitors come for Magnolia Market at the old grain silos, the Dr Pepper Museum and the Waco Mammoth National Monument, which shows the fossils of 24 Columbian mammoths.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org

What it's like to live in Waco

Waco is the anchor city of the Waco metro area. 147,788 people lived here in 2025, up 5.5% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Lake Waco Dam Trail, Woodway Park and Anniversary Park.

The median age is 29.7, 29% of households have children, 49% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $54,365 a year (31% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Waco

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,165, 17% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 19 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Waco

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.

Cost of living in Waco

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Waco is $1,165 a month, 17% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $47,000 a year. Right now 58% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,029 for a one-bedroom, $1,297 for a two-bedroom and $1,693 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $221,700, 22% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,214 before tax, or $1,508 with property tax. That is $343 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Waco pays $3,537 a year, 16% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Waco safe?

In 2025, Waco Police Department reported 614 violent crimes and 2,665 property crimes. That works out to 414 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 7% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,799 property crimes per 100,000, 14% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 43% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.

Schools in Waco

Waco has 30 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 23% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Midway ISD, Rapoport Academy Public School and China Spring ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Midway High School, Paul and Jane Meyer Public High School and Chapel Park Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 71 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
