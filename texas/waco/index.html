Is Waco a good place to live? Common questions

Is Waco, TX a good place to live?

Waco gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 754th of 876 nationally and 66th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are getting around and cost of living, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Waco?

Median rent in Waco is $1,165 a month, 17% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $221,700, 22% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,508, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Waco?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Waco are West Waco, Northwest Waco and Callan Village. We score 10 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Waco?

The average one-way commute is 19 min, 30% lower than the Texas average. About 9.7% of workers work from home.
