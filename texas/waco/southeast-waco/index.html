What it's like to live in Southeast Waco

Southeast Waco is an area in Waco with about 13,100 residents, homes built around 1981, a mix of owners and renters and 31% of households with children.

Our grade for Southeast Waco is D-. That puts it 7th of 10 areas we grade in Waco, a city that gets an F overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Waco

  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Southeast Waco

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,410, 21% higher than the Waco median.
  • Higher poverty. 31% of residents live below the poverty line, against 23% across Waco.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 0 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southeast Waco

Is Southeast Waco a good place to live?

Southeast Waco covers part of Waco, TX, home to about 13,100 people. It gets a D-, in the bottom 16% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 7th of 10 areas we score in Waco. It does best on getting around and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Southeast Waco?

Renters in Southeast Waco pay a median of $1,410 a month, 21% higher than the Waco median. The median home is worth $122,000, 45% lower than the Waco median. A median home costs 2.5x the median household income, compared with 4.1x across Waco.

What schools serve Southeast Waco?

The closest rated public schools are South Waco Elementary (elementary, rated F), Cesar Chavez Middle (middle, rated F) and University High School (high, rated C). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
