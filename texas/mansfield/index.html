Is Mansfield a good place to live? Common questions

Is Mansfield, TX a good place to live?

Mansfield earns a B+ on our index, in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 219th of 876 nationally and 21st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Mansfield?

Renters in Mansfield pay a median of $1,954 a month, 39% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $410,700, 45% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,885, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Mansfield?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Mansfield are Somerset, Harris Crossing and Walnut Landing. We score 16 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Mansfield?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 15% of workers work from home.
