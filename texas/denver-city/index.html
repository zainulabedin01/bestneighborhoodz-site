Living in Denver City: common questions

Is Denver City, TX a good place to live?

Denver City gets an overall grade of A- and ranks 96th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and cost of living, and trails on health and education levels. It is a strong pick for young professionals, retirees, remote workers and people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Denver City?

Median rent in Denver City is $1,030 a month, 27% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $172,800, 39% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,104, including property tax.

Is Denver City safe?

In 2025, Denver City Police Department reported 184 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 52% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 552 per 100,000, 74% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Denver City?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Denver City. 75% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 48 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Denver City flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Denver City as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Denver City?

Summers in Denver City peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 27°F. Expect around 88 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 19 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Denver City?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 24% lower than the Texas average. About 2.3% of workers work from home.
