What it's like to live in Center

Center is a town in Shelby County. 5,246 people lived here in 2025, down 1.7% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Center Baseball Park, Center Mini Park and Portacool Park.

The median age is 33.3, 47% of households have children, 56% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $48,293 a year (38% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Center

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $823, 41% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.7 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 16 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Center

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 50% of the 4 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher violent crime. 653 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 68% higher than the Texas rate.
  • Higher unemployment. 10% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Higher poverty. 26% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.

Cost of living in Center

Center renters pay a median of $823 a month, 41% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $33,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 29% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $878 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $83,300, 71% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $456 before tax, or $579 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Center pays $1,476 a year, 65% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
