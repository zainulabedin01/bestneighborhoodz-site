Living in Hondo: common questions

Is Hondo, TX a good place to live?

Hondo earns a grade of D- on our index, 643rd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on getting around and internet access, and trails on education levels and errands and things to do.

How much does it cost to live in Hondo?

Median rent in Hondo is $1,460 a month, 4% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $228,400, 20% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,476, including property tax.

Is Hondo safe?

In 2025, Hondo Police Department reported 132 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 66% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,108 per 100,000, 47% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Hondo?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Hondo. 25% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 35 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Hondo flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Hondo as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Hondo?

Summers in Hondo peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 39°F. Expect around 128 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 25 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Hondo?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 22% lower than the Texas average. About 5.6% of workers work from home. It is about 39 miles from downtown San Antonio.
