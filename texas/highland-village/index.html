Is Highland Village a good place to live? Common questions

Is Highland Village, TX a good place to live?

Highland Village earns an A on our index, in the top 12% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 389th of 3,321 nationally and 13th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are home ownership and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Highland Village?

Median rent in Highland Village is $2,429 a month, 73% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $550,000, 94% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,710, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Highland Village?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Highland Village are The Overlook Highland Shores, Lakewood Estates and Baird Addition. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Highland Village?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 30% of workers work from home.
