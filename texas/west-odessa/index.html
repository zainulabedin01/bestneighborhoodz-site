Is West Odessa a good place to live? Common questions

Is West Odessa, TX a good place to live?

West Odessa gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2861st of 3,321 nationally and 142nd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in West Odessa?

Renters in West Odessa pay a median of $891 a month, 36% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $126,300, 55% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $802, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in West Odessa?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in West Odessa are Palm Terrace, Briarwood and Frontier West Addition. We score 17 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in West Odessa?

The average one-way commute is 34 min, 27% higher than the Texas average. About 3.2% of workers work from home.
