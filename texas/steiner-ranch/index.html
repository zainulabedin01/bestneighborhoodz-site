What it's like to live in Steiner Ranch

Steiner Ranch sits about 10 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 18,619 people live here. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2004).

Local spots people know include Bella Mar Pool Steiner Ranch, Towne Square Community Center & Park and John Simpson Park.

The median age is 37.2, 52% of households have children, 71% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $190,523 a year (143% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Steiner Ranch

Steiner Ranch renters pay a median of $1,955 a month, 39% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $78,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 65% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $826,500, 191% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $4,524 before tax, or $5,357 with property tax. That is $3,402 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Steiner Ranch pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
