Is Jefferson a good place to live? Common questions

Is Jefferson, TX a good place to live?

We give Jefferson a D-. That places it in the bottom 16% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 10274th of 11,459 nationally and 604th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and education levels, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Jefferson?

Median rent in Jefferson is $653 a month, 53% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $148,600, 48% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $907, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Jefferson?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 3.6% of workers work from home.
