Living in Itasca: common questions

Is Itasca, TX a good place to live?

Itasca earns a grade of B+ on our index, 195th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and jobs and income, and trails on schools and getting around. It is a strong pick for retirees, remote workers and people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Itasca?

Renters in Itasca pay a median of $785 a month, 44% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $114,800, 60% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $787, including property tax.

Is Itasca safe?

In 2025, Itasca Police Department reported 51 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 87% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 406 per 100,000, 81% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Itasca?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Itasca. 33% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 14 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Itasca flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Itasca as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Itasca?

Summers in Itasca peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 105 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 39 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Itasca?

The average one-way commute is 32 min, 19% higher than the Texas average. About 3.4% of workers work from home.
