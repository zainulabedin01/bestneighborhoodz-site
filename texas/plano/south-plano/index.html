What it's like to live in South Plano

South Plano is an area in Plano with about 9,000 residents, homes built around 1986, a mix of owners and renters and 28% of households with children.

It grades B+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 12th of 18 areas in Plano, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in South Plano

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.5 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in South Plano

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about South Plano

Is South Plano a good place to live?
