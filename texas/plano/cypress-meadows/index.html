What it's like to live in Cypress Meadows

Cypress Meadows is a neighborhood in Plano with about 3,600 residents, homes built around 1996, mostly owner-occupied (67%) and 47% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 6th of 18 areas in Plano, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in Cypress Meadows

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Cypress Meadows

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Cypress Meadows

Is Cypress Meadows a good place to live?

Cypress Meadows is a neighborhood in Plano, TX, home to about 3,600 people. It gets an A, in the top 12% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 6th of 18 areas we score in Plano. It does best on schools and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Cypress Meadows?

Median rent in Cypress Meadows is $1,980 a month, 8% higher than the Plano median. The median home is worth $454,000, about the same as the Plano median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 4.2x across Plano.

What schools serve Cypress Meadows?

The closest rated public schools are Anderson Elementary (elementary, rated A), Rice Middle (middle, rated A) and Liberty High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
