What it's like to live in Custer Hill Estates

Custer Hill Estates is a neighborhood in Plano with about 10,900 residents, homes built around 1994, mostly owner-occupied (69%) and 45% of households with children.

It grades A+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 3rd of 18 areas in Plano, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in Custer Hill Estates

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $155,000, 38% higher than the Plano median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 2 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Custer Hill Estates

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,440, 33% higher than the Plano median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.
