Is Azle, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Azle?

Median rent in Azle is $1,333 a month, 5% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $287,100, about the same as the Texas median. A median home costs 4.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,941, including property tax.
