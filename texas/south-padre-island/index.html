Is South Padre Island, TX a good place to live?

South Padre Island earns a C on our index, around the middle of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 6619th of 11,459 nationally and 232nd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are getting around and education levels, and its weakest is cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in South Padre Island?

Median rent in South Padre Island is $1,521 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $516,900, 82% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 7.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,354, including property tax.

How long is the commute in South Padre Island?

The average one-way commute is 13 min, 52% lower than the Texas average. About 31% of workers work from home.
