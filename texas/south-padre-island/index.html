    Living in South Padre Island: common questions

    Is South Padre Island, TX a good place to live?

    South Padre Island earns a grade of C on our index, 404th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on getting around and education levels, and trails on safety and cost of living. It is a strong pick for young professionals.

    How much does it cost to live in South Padre Island?

    Median rent in South Padre Island is $1,521 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $516,900, 82% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 7.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,354, including property tax.

    Is South Padre Island safe?

    In 2025, South Padre Island Police Department reported 1,589 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 310% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 10,881 per 100,000, 422% higher than the Texas rate.

    What is the weather like in South Padre Island?

    Summers in South Padre Island peak at about 91°F in July, and January nights fall to about 52°F. Expect around 95 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 29 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in South Padre Island?

    The average one-way commute is 13 min, 52% lower than the Texas average. About 31% of workers work from home. It is about 20 miles from downtown Brownsville.
