What it's like to live in East Manvel

East Manvel is an area in Manvel with about 3,200 residents, homes built around 1992, mostly owner-occupied (82%) and 52% of households with children.

Our grade for East Manvel is D. That puts it 4th of 4 areas we grade in Manvel, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in East Manvel

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,760, 20% lower than the Manvel median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.5 miles away.

Cons of living in East Manvel

  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 2.2 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, coastal flooding and hurricanes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about East Manvel
