Living in Murillo: common questions

Is Murillo, TX a good place to live?

Murillo earns a grade of D+ on our index, 564th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and internet access, and trails on health and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Murillo?

Median rent in Murillo is $748 a month, 47% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $164,100, 42% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,122, including property tax.

Are the schools good in Murillo?

We found 2 rated public school campuses inside Murillo. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 28 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Murillo flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Murillo as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Murillo?

Summers in Murillo peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 49°F. Expect around 167 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 24 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Murillo?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 21% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home. It is about 8 miles from downtown McAllen.
