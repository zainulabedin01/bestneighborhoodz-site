What it's like to live in Pilot Point

Pilot Point sits about 36 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 8,052 people lived here in 2025, up 83.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Pilot Point Old City Park, Pilot Point Community Library and Allen Groff Park.

The median age is 35.7, 35% of households have children, 75% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $97,933 a year (25% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Pilot Point

  • Low violent crime. 106 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 73% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Plenty close to home. 3 grocery stores, 32 restaurants and 3 parks are mapped in town.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 84% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Pilot Point

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Pilot Point

Pilot Point renters pay a median of $1,430 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $57,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 25% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $292,500, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,601 before tax, or $1,918 with property tax. That is $488 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Pilot Point pays $3,800 a year, 10% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
