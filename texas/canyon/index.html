Is Canyon a good place to live? Common questions

Is Canyon, TX a good place to live?

We give Canyon a C+. That places it around the middle of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 1506th of 3,321 nationally and 48th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are getting around and education levels, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Canyon?

Median rent in Canyon is $984 a month, 30% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $240,300, 15% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,596, including property tax.
