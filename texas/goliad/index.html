What it's like to live in Goliad

Goliad sits about 27 miles from downtown Victoria, in the Victoria metro. 1,708 people lived here in 2025, up 5.2% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Goliad Little League Fields, Goliad State Park Goliad Tx and Fannin Plaza.

The median age is 36.0, 31% of households have children, 70% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $42,266 a year (46% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Goliad

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $673, 52% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Goliad

  • Higher unemployment. 9.6% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Higher poverty. 26% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and wildfires as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 135 days a year at 90°F or hotter.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 70% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Goliad

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Goliad is $673 a month, 52% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $27,000 a year. Right now 61% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,146 for a one-bedroom, $1,428 for a two-bedroom and $1,701 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $184,200, 35% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,008 before tax, or $1,168 with property tax. That is $495 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Goliad pays $1,921 a year, 55% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Goliad

Everyday places mapped inside Goliad, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Goliad Little League Fields, Goliad State Park Goliad Tx, Fannin Plaza.

The average one-way commute is 26 minutes, about the same as the Texas average. 7% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Victoria is about 27 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Goliad work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $42,266, income per person is $24,397, unemployment is 9.6% and 18% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
