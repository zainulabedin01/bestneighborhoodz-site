Questions about South Missouri City

Is South Missouri City a good place to live?

South Missouri City covers part of Missouri City, TX, home to about 4,200 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 2nd of 8 areas we score in Missouri City. It does best on jobs and income and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in South Missouri City?

Renters in South Missouri City pay a median of $1,840 a month, 5% lower than the Missouri City median. The median home is worth $550,000, 77% higher than the Missouri City median. A median home costs 5.9x the median household income, compared with 3.3x across Missouri City.
