What it's like to live in Bay Colony Pointe

Bay Colony Pointe is a neighborhood in League City with about 3,600 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2006), a mix of owners and renters and 42% of households with children.

Our grade for Bay Colony Pointe is B+. That puts it 24th of 27 areas we grade in League City, a city that gets an A overall.

Pros of living in Bay Colony Pointe

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,510, 10% lower than the League City median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Bay Colony Pointe

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding and hurricanes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Bay Colony Pointe

Is Bay Colony Pointe a good place to live?
