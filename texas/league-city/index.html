Is League City a good place to live? Common questions

Is League City, TX a good place to live?

League City earns an A- on our index, in the top 20% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 121st of 876 nationally and 8th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in League City?

Renters in League City pay a median of $1,684 a month, 20% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $360,000, 27% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,459, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in League City?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in League City are Siena, Bayou Lakes and Whispering Lakes Ranch. We score 27 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in League City?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 12% higher than the Texas average. About 15% of workers work from home.
