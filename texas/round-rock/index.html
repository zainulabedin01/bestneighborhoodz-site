    Living in Round Rock: common questions

    Is Round Rock, TX a good place to live?

    Round Rock earns a grade of B+ on our index, 20th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on jobs and income and internet access, and trails on getting around and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Round Rock?

    Median rent in Round Rock is $1,763 a month, 26% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $418,600, 47% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,827, including property tax.

    Is Round Rock safe?

    In 2025, Round Rock Police Department reported 125 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 68% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,446 per 100,000, 31% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Round Rock?

    We found 28 rated public school campuses inside Round Rock. 68% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 65 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Round Rock?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Round Rock are Central Round Rock, The Oaklands and Behrens Ranch. We score 25 areas in the city.

    Does Round Rock flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Round Rock as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hail and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Round Rock?

    Summers in Round Rock peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 38°F. Expect around 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 37 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Round Rock?

    The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 25% of workers work from home. It is about 17 miles from downtown Austin.
