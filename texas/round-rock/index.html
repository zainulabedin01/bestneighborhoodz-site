Is Round Rock, TX a good place to live?

Round Rock earns a B on our index, in the top 36% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 255th of 876 nationally and 23rd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Round Rock?

Median rent in Round Rock is $1,763 a month, 26% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $418,600, 47% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,827, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Round Rock?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Round Rock are Central Round Rock, Behrens Ranch and Preserve at Stone Oak. We score 25 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Round Rock?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 25% of workers work from home.
