What it's like to live in Lake Forest

Lake Forest is a neighborhood in Round Rock with about 12,100 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2004), mostly owner-occupied (84%) and 51% of households with children.

Our grade for Lake Forest is B+. That puts it 10th of 25 areas we grade in Round Rock, a city that gets a B+ overall.

Pros of living in Lake Forest

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $161,600, 63% higher than the Round Rock median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Lake Forest

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,280, 29% higher than the Round Rock median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Lake Forest

Is Lake Forest a good place to live?

Lake Forest is a neighborhood in Round Rock, TX, home to about 12,100 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 10th of 25 areas we score in Round Rock. It does best on education levels and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Lake Forest?

Renters in Lake Forest pay a median of $2,280 a month, 29% higher than the Round Rock median. The median home is worth $568,000, 36% higher than the Round Rock median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 4.2x across Round Rock.

What schools serve Lake Forest?

The closest rated public schools are Forest Creek Elementary (elementary, rated B), Ridgeview Middle (middle, rated A) and Cedar Ridge High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
