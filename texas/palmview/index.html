Is Palmview a good place to live? Common questions

Is Palmview, TX a good place to live?

Palmview earns a D- on our index, in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2984th of 3,321 nationally and 155th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Palmview?

Renters in Palmview pay a median of $678 a month, 52% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $113,600, 60% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $794, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Palmview?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Palmview are Basham Number 15 Colonia, Central Palmview and Ariel Hinojosa Colonia. We score 6 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Palmview?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 24% lower than the Texas average. About 9.7% of workers work from home.
