What it's like to live in Calallen

Calallen is a neighborhood in Corpus Christi with about 11,300 residents, homes built around 1981, a mix of owners and renters and 39% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 7th of 13 areas in Corpus Christi, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in Calallen

  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Calallen

  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 26 minutes, against 21 for Corpus Christi as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Calallen

Is Calallen a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Calallen?

Median rent in Calallen is $1,280 a month, about the same as the Corpus Christi median. The median home is worth $213,000, about the same as the Corpus Christi median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.1x across Corpus Christi.
