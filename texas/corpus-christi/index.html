Is Corpus Christi a good place to live? Common questions

Is Corpus Christi, TX a good place to live?

Corpus Christi gets a D+ overall, which puts it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 624th of 876 nationally and 48th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Corpus Christi?

Renters in Corpus Christi pay a median of $1,292 a month, 8% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $210,400, 26% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,434, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Corpus Christi?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Corpus Christi are Mustang-Padre Island, East Corpus Christi and Southeast Corpus Christi. We score 13 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Corpus Christi?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 20% lower than the Texas average. About 5.3% of workers work from home.
