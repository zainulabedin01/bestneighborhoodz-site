Questions about East Corpus Christi

Is East Corpus Christi a good place to live?

East Corpus Christi covers part of Corpus Christi, TX, home to about 8,800 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 2nd of 13 areas we score in Corpus Christi. It does best on getting around and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in East Corpus Christi?

Renters in East Corpus Christi pay a median of $1,170 a month, 9% lower than the Corpus Christi median. The median home is worth $332,000, 58% higher than the Corpus Christi median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.1x across Corpus Christi.

What schools serve East Corpus Christi?

The closest rated public schools are Calk-Wilson Elementary (elementary, rated C), Hamlin Middle (middle, rated B) and King High School (high, rated C). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
