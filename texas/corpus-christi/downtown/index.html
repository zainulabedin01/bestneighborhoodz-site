What it's like to live in Downtown

Downtown is a neighborhood in Corpus Christi with about 12,900 residents, older homes (median year built 1958), a mix of owners and renters and 28% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 6th of 13 areas in Corpus Christi, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in Downtown

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,120, 13% lower than the Corpus Christi median.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Downtown

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.

Cost of living in Downtown

Median rent in Downtown is $1,120 a month, 13% lower than the Corpus Christi median. A household would need about $45,000 a year to keep that rent at 30% of income.

The median home is worth $218,000, about the same as the Corpus Christi median, which means a monthly payment of about $1,455 with property tax at today's mortgage rates.

Questions about Downtown

Is Downtown a good place to live?

Downtown is a neighborhood in Corpus Christi, TX, home to about 12,900 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 6th of 13 areas we score in Corpus Christi. It does best on getting around and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Downtown?

Median rent in Downtown is $1,120 a month, 13% lower than the Corpus Christi median. The median home is worth $218,000, 4% higher than the Corpus Christi median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.1x across Corpus Christi.

What schools serve Downtown?

The closest rated public schools are Menger Elementary (elementary, rated B), Baker Middle (middle, rated A) and Ray High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
