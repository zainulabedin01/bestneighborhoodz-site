Is Terrell a good place to live? Common questions

Is Terrell, TX a good place to live?

We give Terrell an F. That places it in the bottom 8% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 3163rd of 3,321 nationally and 183rd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Terrell?

Median rent in Terrell is $1,232 a month, 12% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $218,500, 23% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,513, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Terrell?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Terrell are Southwest Terrell, Northeast Terrell and Central Terrell. We score 3 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Terrell?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 4.9% of workers work from home.
