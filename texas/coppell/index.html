    Living in Coppell: common questions

    Is Coppell, TX a good place to live?

    We give Coppell an A+. It ranks 5th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on education levels and jobs and income, and trails on getting around and cost of living. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Coppell?

    Renters in Coppell pay a median of $2,022 a month, 44% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $560,500, 97% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,901, including property tax.

    Is Coppell safe?

    In 2025, Coppell Police Department reported 92 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 76% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 894 per 100,000, 57% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Coppell?

    Does Coppell flood?

    What is the weather like in Coppell?
