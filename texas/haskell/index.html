Living in Haskell: common questions

Is Haskell, TX a good place to live?

We give Haskell an A-. It ranks 117th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on home ownership and internet access. It is a strong pick for families, retirees and people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Haskell?

Median rent in Haskell is $777 a month, 45% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $104,500, 63% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $700, including property tax.

Is Haskell safe?

In 2025, Haskell Police Department reported 33 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 92% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 457 per 100,000, 78% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Haskell?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Haskell. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 81 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Haskell flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Haskell as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Haskell?

Summers in Haskell peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 29°F. Expect around 110 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 27 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Haskell?

The average one-way commute is 15 min, 45% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home.
