What it's like to live in Bedford

Bedford sits about 22 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 47,958 people lived here in 2025, down 4.0% from 2020. It is densely built.

Places locals use include Boys Ranch Pavilion, Bedford Boys Ranch and Meadow Park Athletic Complex.

The median age is 39.5, 26% of households have children, 51% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $83,971 a year (7% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Bedford

  • Strong public schools. 88% of the 8 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 180 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 54% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Plenty close to home. 8 grocery stores, 189 restaurants and 19 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Bedford

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Bedford

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Bedford is $1,502 a month, 7% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $60,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $350,700, 24% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,920 before tax, or $2,369 with property tax. That is $867 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Bedford pays $5,390 a year, 27% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Schools in Bedford

Bedford has 8 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 88% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Bedford Heights Elementary, Bedford Junior High and Harwood Junior High. The average rating score across all campuses is 88 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

Daily life in Bedford

Everyday places mapped inside Bedford, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Boys Ranch Pavilion, Bedford Boys Ranch, Meadow Park Athletic Complex.

The average one-way commute is 24 minutes, 8% shorter than the Texas average. 18% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Dallas is about 22 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Bedford work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $83,971, income per person is $44,439, unemployment is 4.8% and 43% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
