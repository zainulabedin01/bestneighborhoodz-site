Living in Pittsburg: common questions

Is Pittsburg, TX a good place to live?

Pittsburg gets an overall grade of C+ and ranks 378th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and errands and things to do, and trails on health and home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Pittsburg?

Median rent in Pittsburg is $777 a month, 45% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $119,200, 58% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $800, including property tax.

Is Pittsburg safe?

In 2025, Pittsburg Police Department reported 542 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 40% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 672 per 100,000, 68% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Pittsburg?

What is the weather like in Pittsburg?
