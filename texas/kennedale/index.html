What it's like to live in Kennedale

Kennedale sits about 28 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 10,626 people lived here in 2025, up 24.9% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Sonora Park, Kennedale Towne Center Park and Rodgers Farm Park.

The median age is 38.6, 31% of households have children, 71% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $118,794 a year (51% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Kennedale

  • Low violent crime. 166 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 57% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $118,794, 51% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 25% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Kennedale

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 25% of the 4 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 32 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Kennedale

Kennedale renters pay a median of $1,554 a month, 11% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $62,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $380,100, 34% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,081 before tax, or $2,589 with property tax. That is $1,035 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Kennedale pays $6,098 a year, 44% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Kennedale safe?

In 2025, Kennedale Police Department reported 19 violent crimes and 100 property crimes. That works out to 166 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 57% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 875 property crimes per 100,000, 58% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Daily life in Kennedale

Everyday places mapped inside Kennedale, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Sonora Park, Kennedale Towne Center Park, Rodgers Farm Park.
