What it's like to live in Aubrey

Aubrey sits about 33 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 9,066 people lived here in 2025, up 84.5% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2013).

Places locals use include Matthew's Park, Leslie Park and Aubrey Area Library.

The median age is 33.3, 35% of households have children, 76% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $101,574 a year (29% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Aubrey

  • High incomes. The median household earns $101,574, 29% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 84% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Aubrey

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,882, 34% higher than the Texas median.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 34 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Aubrey

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Aubrey is $1,882 a month, 34% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $75,000 a year. Right now 50% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $345,000, 22% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,888 before tax, or $2,426 with property tax. That is $544 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Aubrey pays $6,446 a year, 52% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
