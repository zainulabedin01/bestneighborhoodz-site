Is Sugar Land a good place to live? Common questions

Is Sugar Land, TX a good place to live?

Sugar Land earns an A on our index, in the top 12% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 80th of 876 nationally and 5th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Sugar Land?

Median rent in Sugar Land is $1,957 a month, 39% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $430,200, 52% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,965, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Sugar Land?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Sugar Land are Colony Park, Central Sugar Land and West Sugar Land. We score 14 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Sugar Land?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 8% higher than the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home.
