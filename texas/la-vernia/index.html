    Is La Vernia, TX a good place to live?

    La Vernia gets an overall grade of C and ranks 427th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and jobs and income, and trails on getting around and internet access.

    How much does it cost to live in La Vernia?

    Renters in La Vernia pay a median of $993 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $347,400, 22% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,213, including property tax.

    What is the weather like in La Vernia?

    How long is the commute in La Vernia?
