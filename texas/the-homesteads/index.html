What it's like to live in The Homesteads

The Homesteads sits about 32 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. About 3,053 people live here. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2002).

The median age is 34.3, 33% of households have children, 76% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $76,994 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in The Homesteads

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.7 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in The Homesteads

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 42 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Many adults uninsured. 33% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in The Homesteads

The Homesteads renters pay a median of $1,708 a month, 22% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $68,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 45% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $133,300, 53% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $730 before tax, or $871 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in The Homesteads pays $1,694 a year, 60% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
