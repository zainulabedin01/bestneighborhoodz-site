Is Corinth a good place to live? Common questions

Is Corinth, TX a good place to live?

Corinth gets a B+ overall, which puts it in the top 28% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 700th of 3,321 nationally and 23rd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Corinth?

Renters in Corinth pay a median of $1,891 a month, 35% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $375,600, 32% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,582, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Corinth?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Corinth are Provence, Terrace Oaks Phase 1 and Lake Sharon Estates Phase 3. We score 4 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Corinth?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 17% of workers work from home.
