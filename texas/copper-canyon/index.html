What it's like to live in Copper Canyon

Copper Canyon sits about 28 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 2,322 people lived here in 2025, up 54.5% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2002).

The median age is 53.5, 32% of households have children, 98% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $155,625 a year (98% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Copper Canyon

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Copper Canyon is $1,950 a month, 39% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $78,000 a year. Right now 47% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $744,300, 162% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $4,074 before tax, or $4,907 with property tax. That is $2,957 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Copper Canyon pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
