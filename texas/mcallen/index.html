Is McAllen a good place to live? Common questions

Is McAllen, TX a good place to live?

We give McAllen a D+. That places it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 615th of 876 nationally and 46th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and internet access, and its weakest is jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in McAllen?

Median rent in McAllen is $1,049 a month, 25% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $190,400, 33% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,316, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in McAllen?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in McAllen are Royal Oak Estates, Northwest McAllen and The Gables. We score 21 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in McAllen?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 18% lower than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
