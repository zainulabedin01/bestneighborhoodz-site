What it's like to live in Del Sol

Del Sol is a neighborhood in McAllen with about 16,800 residents, homes built around 1990, a mix of owners and renters and 43% of households with children.

It grades B+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 11th of 21 areas in McAllen, which grades B+ overall.

Pros of living in Del Sol

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Del Sol

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Del Sol

Is Del Sol a good place to live?

Del Sol is a neighborhood in McAllen, TX, home to about 16,800 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 11th of 21 areas we score in McAllen. It does best on schools and worst on jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in Del Sol?

Median rent in Del Sol is $1,040 a month, about the same as the McAllen median. The median home is worth $202,000, 6% higher than the McAllen median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.1x across McAllen.

What schools serve Del Sol?

The closest rated public schools are Milam Elementary (elementary, rated A), De Leon Middle (middle, rated B) and McAllen High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
