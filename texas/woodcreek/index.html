What it's like to live in Woodcreek

Woodcreek sits about 28 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 1,876 people lived here in 2025, up 5.0% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Augusta Park and Veteran's Memorial Plaza.

The median age is 50.2, 17% of households have children, 54% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $74,107 a year (6% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Woodcreek

Woodcreek renters pay a median of $1,809 a month, 29% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $72,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 68% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $425,600, 50% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,330 before tax, or $2,772 with property tax. That is $963 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Woodcreek pays $5,308 a year, 25% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
