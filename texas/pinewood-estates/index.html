What it's like to live in Pinewood Estates

Pinewood Estates sits about 12 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. About 1,516 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Moore Park and Pinewood Park.

The median age is 37.9, 53% of households have children, 99% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $157,553 a year (101% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Pinewood Estates

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.2 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $157,553, 101% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Pinewood Estates

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Pinewood Estates

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $340,900, 20% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,866 before tax, or $2,264 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Pinewood Estates pays $4,782 a year, 13% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
