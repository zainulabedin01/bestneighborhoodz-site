What it's like to live in Edgewood

Edgewood is a town in Van Zandt County. 1,763 people lived here in 2025, up 10.3% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Edgewood City Park and Edgewood Heritage Park.

The median age is 43.6, 32% of households have children, 62% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $73,533 a year (6% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Edgewood

  • Low violent crime. 56 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 86% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $568, 60% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Edgewood

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Edgewood

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Edgewood is $568 a month, 60% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $23,000 a year. Right now 53% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $905 for a one-bedroom, $1,184 for a two-bedroom and $1,534 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $225,000, 21% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,232 before tax, or $1,481 with property tax. That is $913 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Edgewood pays $2,989 a year, 29% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
