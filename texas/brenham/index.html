What it's like to live in Brenham

Brenham is a small city in Washington County. 19,957 people lived here in 2025, up 15.0% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Hohlt Park, Jackson Street Park and Fireman's Park.

The median age is 33.8, 27% of households have children, 51% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $65,112 a year (17% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Brenham

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,157, 18% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 18 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 15% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Brenham

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 29% of the 7 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Brenham

Brenham renters pay a median of $1,157 a month, 18% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $46,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 46% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,070 for a one-bedroom, $1,400 for a two-bedroom and $1,685 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $241,100, 15% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,320 before tax, or $1,578 with property tax. That is $421 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Brenham pays $3,097 a year, 27% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

In 2025, Brenham Police Department reported 65 violent crimes and 356 property crimes. That works out to 319 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 18% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,748 property crimes per 100,000, 16% lower than the state rate.
