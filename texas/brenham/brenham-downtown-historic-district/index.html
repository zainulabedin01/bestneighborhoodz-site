What it's like to live in Brenham Downtown Historic District

Brenham Downtown Historic District is a neighborhood in Brenham with about 10,000 residents, homes built around 1970, a mix of owners and renters and 26% of households with children.

Our grade for Brenham Downtown Historic District is C-. That puts it 3rd of 4 areas we grade in Brenham, a city that gets a C- overall.
