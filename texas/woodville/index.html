How much does it cost to live in Woodville?

Renters in Woodville pay a median of $1,075 a month, 23% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $214,900, 24% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,412, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Woodville?
