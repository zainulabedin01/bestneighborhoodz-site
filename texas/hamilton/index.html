What it's like to live in Hamilton

Hamilton is a town in Hamilton County. 3,016 people lived here in 2025, up 4.3% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1959).

Places locals use include Hamilton Park, Shrunk Park and Hamilton Public Library.

The median age is 40.5, 24% of households have children, 69% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $43,780 a year (44% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Hamilton

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $888, 37% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.3 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 10 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Hamilton

  • Higher unemployment. 10% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Hamilton

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Hamilton is $888 a month, 37% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $36,000 a year. Right now 63% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $775 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $102,100, 64% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $559 before tax, or $670 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Hamilton pays $1,333 a year, 69% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Living in Hamilton: common questions

Is Hamilton, TX a good place to live?

We give Hamilton a C. It ranks 439th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and getting around, and trails on jobs and income and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Hamilton?

Median rent in Hamilton is $888 a month, 37% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $102,100, 64% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $670, including property tax.

Is Hamilton safe?

In 2024, Hamilton Police Department reported 99 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 74% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 957 per 100,000, 54% lower than the Texas rate. This agency reported only part of the year, so the rate is shown but not scored.

Are the schools good in Hamilton?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Hamilton. 67% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 58 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Hamilton flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Hamilton as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Hamilton?

Summers in Hamilton peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 89 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 32 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Hamilton?
