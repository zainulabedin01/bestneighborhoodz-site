What it's like to live in Rancho Viejo

Rancho Viejo sits about 7 miles from downtown Brownsville, in the Brownsville-Harlingen metro. 2,851 people lived here in 2025, up 0.7% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 41.1, 31% of households have children, 66% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $90,714 a year (16% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Rancho Viejo

Rancho Viejo renters pay a median of $1,392 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $56,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 29% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $884 for a one-bedroom, $1,042 for a two-bedroom and $1,393 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $264,500, 7% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,448 before tax, or $1,819 with property tax. That is $427 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Rancho Viejo pays $4,455 a year, 5% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Rancho Viejo

Everyday places mapped inside Rancho Viejo, from Overture Maps.

The average one-way commute is 26 minutes, about the same as the Texas average. 6% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Brownsville is about 7 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Rancho Viejo work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $90,714, income per person is $54,849, unemployment is 3.7% and 60% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
