Is Big Spring a good place to live? Common questions

Is Big Spring, TX a good place to live?

Big Spring earns a D- on our index, in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2895th of 3,321 nationally and 146th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Big Spring?

Median rent in Big Spring is $1,050 a month, 25% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $140,200, 51% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $886, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Big Spring?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Big Spring are Northeast Big Spring, East Big Spring and Indian Hills. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Big Spring?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 21% lower than the Texas average. About 3.1% of workers work from home.
