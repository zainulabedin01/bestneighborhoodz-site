What it's like to live in Raymondville

Raymondville is a small city in Willacy County. 10,082 people lived here in 2025, down 1.3% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Smiley Face Park and Reber Memorial Library.

The median age is 32.6, 43% of households have children, 70% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $51,442 a year (34% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Raymondville

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 4 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $881, 37% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.0 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 16 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 5 grocery stores, 41 restaurants and 4 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Raymondville

  • Higher unemployment. 9.1% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Higher poverty. 31% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 42% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 161 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Raymondville

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Raymondville is $881 a month, 37% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $35,000 a year. Right now 49% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $775 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,398 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $52,200, 82% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $286 before tax, or $382 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Raymondville pays $1,152 a year, 73% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Schools in Raymondville

Raymondville has 4 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Raymondville ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Pittman Elementary, Myra Green Middle and Smith Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 91 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
