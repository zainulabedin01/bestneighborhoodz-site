Is Universal City, TX a good place to live?

Universal City gets a C+ overall, which puts it around the middle of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 1521st of 3,321 nationally and 51st of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and cost of living, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Universal City?

Renters in Universal City pay a median of $1,315 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $269,600, 5% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,905, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Universal City?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 9.9% of workers work from home.
