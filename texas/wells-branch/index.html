What it's like to live in Wells Branch

Wells Branch sits about 11 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 13,523 people live here. It is densely built.

Places locals use include Mills Pond Recreation Area, Katherine Fleischer Park and Vista Park.

The median age is 33.9, 24% of households have children, 31% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $66,656 a year (15% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Wells Branch

  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Wells Branch

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 6.0 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Wells Branch

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Wells Branch is $1,544 a month, 10% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $62,000 a year. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $401,100, 41% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,196 before tax, or $2,689 with property tax. That is $1,145 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Wells Branch pays $5,916 a year, 40% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
