Is Chico a good place to live? Common questions

Is Chico, TX a good place to live?

Chico earns a D on our index, in the bottom 24% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 9014th of 11,459 nationally and 447th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Chico?

Median rent in Chico is $1,317 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $170,300, 40% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,092, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Chico?

The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 8.0% of workers work from home.
