What it's like to live in East Kyle

East Kyle is an area in Kyle with about 2,000 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2009), mostly owner-occupied (88%) and 42% of households with children.

Our grade for East Kyle is C-. That puts it 4th of 5 areas we grade in Kyle, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in East Kyle

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $148,500, 64% higher than the Kyle median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about under 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in East Kyle

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,200, 26% higher than the Kyle median.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 0 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
