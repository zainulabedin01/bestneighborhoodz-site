How much does it cost to live in Kyle?

Renters in Kyle pay a median of $1,752 a month, 25% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $334,600, 18% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,346, including property tax.
