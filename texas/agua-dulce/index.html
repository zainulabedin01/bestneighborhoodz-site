Living in Agua Dulce: common questions

Is Agua Dulce, TX a good place to live?

Agua Dulce gets an overall grade of D+ and ranks 514th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on home ownership and schools, and trails on education levels and health.

How much does it cost to live in Agua Dulce?

Median rent in Agua Dulce is $1,084 a month, 23% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $120,600, 58% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $845, including property tax.

Does Agua Dulce flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Agua Dulce as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Agua Dulce?

Summers in Agua Dulce peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 32°F. Expect around 78 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 10 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Agua Dulce?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 13% higher than the Texas average. About 19% of workers work from home. It is about 22 miles from downtown El Paso.
