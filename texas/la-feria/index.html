Living in La Feria: common questions

Is La Feria, TX a good place to live?

La Feria gets an overall grade of C- and ranks 475th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and errands and things to do, and trails on internet access and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in La Feria?

Renters in La Feria pay a median of $1,037 a month, 26% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $106,900, 62% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $686, including property tax.

Is La Feria safe?

In 2025, La Feria Police Department reported 367 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 5% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,554 per 100,000, 25% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in La Feria?

We found 6 rated public school campuses inside La Feria. 83% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 77 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does La Feria flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in La Feria as very low, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in La Feria?

Summers in La Feria peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 49°F. Expect around 166 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 25 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in La Feria?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 24% lower than the Texas average. About 4.5% of workers work from home. It is about 25 miles from downtown Brownsville.
