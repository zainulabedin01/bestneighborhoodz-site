What it's like to live in Four Corners

Four Corners sits about 18 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 11,692 people live here. It is densely built, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2004).

The median age is 35.5, 46% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $76,452 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Cost of living in Four Corners

Four Corners renters pay a median of $1,519 a month, 8% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $61,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 96% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $277,000, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,516 before tax, or $1,892 with property tax. That is $373 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Four Corners pays $4,506 a year, 6% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Four Corners

Everyday places mapped inside Four Corners, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Sugar Grove Community Park.

Downtown Houston is about 18 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Four Corners work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
