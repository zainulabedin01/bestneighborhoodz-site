What it's like to live in Marfa

Marfa is a town in Presidio County. 1,574 people lived here in 2025, down 12.0% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1949).

Local spots people know include Coffield Park and Marfa Public Library.

The median age is 47.7, 13% of households have children, 75% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $44,306 a year (44% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Marfa

  • Low violent crime. 62 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 84% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $474, 66% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 6 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 4 grocery stores, 24 restaurants and 6 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Marfa

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of wildfires and winter storms as very high here.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 78% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Marfa

Marfa renters pay a median of $474 a month, 66% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $19,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 72% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $858 for a one-bedroom, $1,078 for a two-bedroom and $1,393 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $251,400, 11% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,376 before tax, or $1,531 with property tax. That is $1,057 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Marfa pays $1,863 a year, 56% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
