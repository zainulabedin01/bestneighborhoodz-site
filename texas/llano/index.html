What it's like to live in Llano

Llano is a town in Llano County. 3,587 people lived here in 2025, up 6.8% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Robinson Llano County Park, Llano River Park and Badu Park.

The median age is 41.6, 30% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $48,309 a year (38% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Llano

  • Low violent crime. 137 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 65% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,078, 23% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Llano

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 4.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 120 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Llano

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Llano is $1,078 a month, 23% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $43,000 a year. Right now 67% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $878 for a one-bedroom, $1,102 for a two-bedroom and $1,526 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $234,100, 18% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,281 before tax, or $1,429 with property tax. That is $351 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Llano pays $1,771 a year, 58% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
