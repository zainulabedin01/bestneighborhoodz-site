Is Ben Wheeler a good place to live? Common questions

Is Ben Wheeler, TX a good place to live?

Ben Wheeler earns an A- on our index, in the top 20% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 2248th of 11,459 nationally and 65th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Ben Wheeler?

The median home is worth $268,400, 5% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,673, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Ben Wheeler?

The average one-way commute is 61 min, 129% higher than the Texas average. About 8.1% of workers work from home.
