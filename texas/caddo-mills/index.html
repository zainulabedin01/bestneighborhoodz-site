What it's like to live in Caddo Mills

Caddo Mills sits about 35 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 5,715 people lived here in 2025, up 284.3% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Trailstone Green Space and Hanchey Park.

The median age is 33.5, 42% of households have children, 71% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $107,067 a year (36% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Caddo Mills

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 5 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 125 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 68% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,135, 19% lower than the Texas median.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $107,067, 36% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 284% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Caddo Mills

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Caddo Mills

Caddo Mills renters pay a median of $1,135 a month, 19% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $45,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 41% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $299,700, 6% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,640 before tax, or $2,099 with property tax. That is $964 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Caddo Mills pays $5,497 a year, 30% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Caddo Mills

Downtown Dallas is about 35 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Caddo Mills work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
