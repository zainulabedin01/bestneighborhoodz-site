What it's like to live in Jones Creek

Jones Creek sits about 56 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 1,952 people lived here in 2025, down 0.4% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 33.5, 43% of households have children, 92% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $72,847 a year (7% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Jones Creek

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Jones Creek is $1,086 a month, 23% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $43,000 a year. Right now 23% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,212 for a one-bedroom, $1,319 for a two-bedroom and $1,790 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $182,300, 36% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $998 before tax, or $1,202 with property tax. That is $116 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Jones Creek pays $2,450 a year, 42% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
