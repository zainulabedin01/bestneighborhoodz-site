What it's like to live in Whitley Place

Whitley Place is a neighborhood in Prosper with about 6,100 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2013), mostly owner-occupied (95%) and 57% of households with children.

It grades B+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 6th of 7 areas in Prosper, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in Whitley Place

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in Whitley Place

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $3,500+, 61% higher than the Prosper median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Whitley Place

Is Whitley Place a good place to live?

Whitley Place is a neighborhood in Prosper, TX, home to about 6,100 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 6th of 7 areas we score in Prosper. It does best on education levels and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Whitley Place?

Median rent in Whitley Place is $3,500+ a month, 61% higher than the Prosper median. The median home is worth $946,000, 15% higher than the Prosper median. A median home costs 4.3x the median household income, compared with 4.2x across Prosper.

What schools serve Whitley Place?

The closest rated public schools are Cynthia A Cockrell Elementary (elementary, rated A), Lorene Rogers Middle (middle, rated A) and Walnut Grove High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
