Is Prosper a good place to live? Common questions

Is Prosper, TX a good place to live?

We give Prosper an A-. That places it in the top 20% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 654th of 3,321 nationally and 19th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Prosper?

Renters in Prosper pay a median of $2,176 a month, 55% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $823,700, 190% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $5,342, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Prosper?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Prosper are Southwest Prosper, Central Prosper and Crestview at Prosper. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Prosper?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 10% higher than the Texas average. About 36% of workers work from home.
