Living in Sweetwater: common questions

Is Sweetwater, TX a good place to live?

We give Sweetwater a D+. It ranks 138th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on cost of living and getting around, and trails on schools and health. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Sweetwater?

Renters in Sweetwater pay a median of $907 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $80,800, 72% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $540, including property tax.

Is Sweetwater safe?

In 2025, Sweetwater Police Department reported 485 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 25% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,278 per 100,000, 39% lower than the Texas rate.
