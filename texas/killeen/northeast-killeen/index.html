Questions about Northeast Killeen

Is Northeast Killeen a good place to live?

Northeast Killeen covers part of Killeen, TX, home to about 25,700 people. It gets a D, in the bottom quarter of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 7 areas we score in Killeen. It does best on getting around and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Northeast Killeen?

Renters in Northeast Killeen pay a median of $1,230 a month, about the same as the Killeen median. The median home is worth $181,000, 16% lower than the Killeen median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.5x across Killeen.
