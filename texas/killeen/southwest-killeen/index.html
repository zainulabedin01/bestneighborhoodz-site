What it's like to live in Southwest Killeen

Southwest Killeen is an area in Killeen with about 2,800 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2012), mostly owner-occupied (93%) and 19% of households with children.

Our grade for Southwest Killeen is D. That puts it 3rd of 7 areas we grade in Killeen, a city that gets an F overall.

Pros of living in Southwest Killeen

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $93,900, 54% higher than the Killeen median.

Cons of living in Southwest Killeen

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,130, 76% higher than the Killeen median.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 3.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southwest Killeen

Is Southwest Killeen a good place to live?

Southwest Killeen covers part of Killeen, TX, home to about 2,800 people. It gets a D, in the bottom quarter of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 3rd of 7 areas we score in Killeen. It does best on home ownership and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Southwest Killeen?

Renters in Southwest Killeen pay a median of $2,130 a month, 76% higher than the Killeen median. The median home is worth $267,000, 24% higher than the Killeen median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.5x across Killeen.

What schools serve Southwest Killeen?

The closest rated public schools are Alice W Douse Elementary (elementary, rated D), Charles E Patterson Middle (middle, rated C) and Chaparral High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
