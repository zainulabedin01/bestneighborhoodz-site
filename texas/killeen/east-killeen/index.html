What it's like to live in East Killeen

East Killeen is an area in Killeen with about 7,900 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2012), mostly owner-occupied (85%) and 55% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 7 areas in Killeen, which grades F overall.

Pros of living in East Killeen

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $113,000, 85% higher than the Killeen median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in East Killeen

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,250, 86% higher than the Killeen median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about East Killeen

Is East Killeen a good place to live?

East Killeen covers part of Killeen, TX, home to about 7,900 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 1st of 7 areas we score in Killeen. It does best on home ownership and worst on errands and things to do.

How much does it cost to live in East Killeen?

Median rent in East Killeen is $2,250 a month, 86% higher than the Killeen median. The median home is worth $346,000, 61% higher than the Killeen median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.5x across Killeen.

The closest rated public schools are Maude Moore Wood Elementary (elementary, rated B), Union Grove Middle (middle, rated C) and Chaparral High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
