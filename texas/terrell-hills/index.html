What it's like to live in Terrell Hills

Terrell Hills sits about 5 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 5,215 people lived here in 2025, up 3.4% from 2020. It is suburban, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1957).

The median age is 42.7, 36% of households have children, 81% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $186,400 a year (138% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Terrell Hills

  • Low violent crime. 58 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 85% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,078, 23% lower than the Texas median.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $186,400, 138% higher than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Healthier population. Only 7.1% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Terrell Hills

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Terrell Hills

Terrell Hills renters pay a median of $1,078 a month, 23% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $43,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 75% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $838,400, 195% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $4,589 before tax, or $5,423 with property tax. That is $4,345 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Terrell Hills pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
