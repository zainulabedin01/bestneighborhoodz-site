What it's like to live in Winnie

Winnie sits about 60 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 3,290 people live here. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Safari Park, Winnie Stowell County Park and Winnie-Stowell County Softball Baseball Complex.

The median age is 27.1, 36% of households have children, 51% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $80,913 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Winnie

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.1 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Winnie

  • Higher unemployment. 13% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 73% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Winnie

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Winnie is $1,417 a month, about the same as the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $57,000 a year. Right now 7% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $166,500, 41% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $911 before tax, or $1,091 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Winnie pays $2,161 a year, 49% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
