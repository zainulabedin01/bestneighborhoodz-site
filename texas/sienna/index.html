What it's like to live in Sienna

Sienna sits about 22 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 24,992 people live here. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2010).

Places locals use include Sienna Plantation - Steep Bank Village West Park and Brushy Lake Playground.

The median age is 35.9, 59% of households have children, 96% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $177,837 a year (127% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Sienna

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $177,837, 127% higher than the Texas median.
  • Healthier population. Only 8.1% of working-age adults lack health insurance.

Cons of living in Sienna

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $2,837, 102% higher than the Texas median.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 36 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 122 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Sienna

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Sienna is $2,837 a month, 102% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $113,000 a year. Right now 8% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $470,100, 66% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,573 before tax, or $3,407 with property tax. That is $570 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Sienna pays $10,000+ a year, 136% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Sienna

Everyday places mapped inside Sienna, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Sienna Plantation - Steep Bank Village West Park, Brushy Lake Playground.

The average one-way commute is 36 minutes, 35% longer than the Texas average. 22% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Houston is about 22 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Sienna work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
