Is Breckenridge, TX a good place to live?

Breckenridge earns a D+ on our index, in the bottom 32% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 7951st of 11,459 nationally and 334th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Breckenridge?

Renters in Breckenridge pay a median of $839 a month, 40% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $75,900, 73% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $542, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Breckenridge?

The average one-way commute is 23 min, 14% lower than the Texas average. About 2.8% of workers work from home.
