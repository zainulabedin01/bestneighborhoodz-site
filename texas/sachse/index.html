What it's like to live in Sachse

Sachse sits about 16 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 32,834 people lived here in 2025, up 21.1% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2005).

Local spots people know include Heritage Park, Stone Park and Salmon Park.

The median age is 39.9, 40% of households have children, 85% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $131,808 a year (68% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Sachse

  • Strong public schools. 83% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 69 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 82% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $131,808, 68% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 21% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Sachse

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,798, 28% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Sachse

Sachse renters pay a median of $1,798 a month, 28% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $72,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $406,200, 43% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,223 before tax, or $2,796 with property tax. That is $998 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Sachse pays $6,873 a year, 62% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Schools in Sachse

Sachse has 6 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 83% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Wylie ISD and Garland ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Don Whitt Elementary, Cheri Cox Elementary and Sachse High School. The average rating score across all campuses is 90 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
