Living in Bonham: common questions

Is Bonham, TX a good place to live?

Bonham earns a grade of D on our index, 153rd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on safety and getting around, and trails on internet access and health.

How much does it cost to live in Bonham?

Renters in Bonham pay a median of $1,073 a month, 24% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $201,800, 29% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,287, including property tax.

Is Bonham safe?

In 2025, Bonham Police Department reported 97 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 75% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 813 per 100,000, 61% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Bonham?

We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Bonham. 20% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 6 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

Does Bonham flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Bonham as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Bonham?

Summers in Bonham peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 33°F. Expect around 78 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 46 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Bonham?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 18% lower than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home.
