Is Arlington a good place to live? Common questions

Is Arlington, TX a good place to live?

Arlington gets a D overall, which puts it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 704th of 876 nationally and 60th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and cost of living, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Arlington?

Median rent in Arlington is $1,470 a month, 5% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $304,700, 7% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,061, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Arlington?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Arlington are Webb, Sierra Elite Estates and Western Trails. We score 21 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Arlington?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home.
