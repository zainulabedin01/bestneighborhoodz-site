What it's like to live in Fox Run

Fox Run is a neighborhood in Arlington with about 4,200 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2004), mostly owner-occupied (87%) and 37% of households with children.

Our grade for Fox Run is B-. That puts it 7th of 21 areas we grade in Arlington, a city that gets a D overall.

Pros of living in Fox Run

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $128,200, 71% higher than the Arlington median.

Cons of living in Fox Run

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,820, 24% higher than the Arlington median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Fox Run

Is Fox Run a good place to live?

Fox Run is a neighborhood in Arlington, TX, home to about 4,200 people. It gets a B-, in the top 44% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 7th of 21 areas we score in Arlington. It does best on home ownership and worst on errands and things to do.

How much does it cost to live in Fox Run?

Renters in Fox Run pay a median of $1,820 a month, 24% higher than the Arlington median. The median home is worth $441,000, 45% higher than the Arlington median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 4.1x across Arlington.
