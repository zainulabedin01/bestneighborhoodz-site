Living in The Hills: common questions

Is The Hills, TX a good place to live?

The Hills earns a grade of A+ on our index, 10th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on education levels and internet access, and trails on cost of living and errands and things to do. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

How much does it cost to live in The Hills?

Median rent in The Hills is $3,500+ a month, 150% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $846,000, 198% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 5.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $5,450, including property tax.

Does The Hills flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in The Hills as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in The Hills?

Summers in The Hills peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 39°F. Expect around 106 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 36 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in The Hills?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 11% lower than the Texas average. About 41% of workers work from home. It is about 14 miles from downtown Austin.
