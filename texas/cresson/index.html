What it's like to live in Cresson

Cresson is a town in Johnson County. 2,126 people lived here in 2025, up 57.4% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2002).

The median age is 34.2, 35% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $91,518 a year (17% higher than the Texas median).

Cons of living in Cresson

Cost of living in Cresson

Cresson renters pay a median of $1,680 a month, 20% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $67,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 37% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,297 for a one-bedroom, $1,463 for a two-bedroom and $1,900 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $293,200, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,605 before tax, or $1,796 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Cresson pays $2,296 a year, 46% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
