What it's like to live in Irving

Irving sits about 13 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 257,076 people lived here in 2025, up 0.1% from 2020.

Local spots people know include Fritz Park, Sam Houston Trail Park and T.W. Richardson Grove.

The median age is 32.8, 36% of households have children, 38% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $81,830 a year (4% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Irving

  • Plenty close to home. 60 grocery stores, 1,108 restaurants and 124 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Irving

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 40% of the 53 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,619, 15% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Irving

Irving renters pay a median of $1,619 a month, 15% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $65,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 45% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $315,600, 11% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,727 before tax, or $2,123 with property tax. That is $504 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Irving pays $4,752 a year, 12% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Irving safe?

In 2025, Irving Police Department reported 691 violent crimes and 4,532 property crimes. That works out to 268 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 31% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,755 property crimes per 100,000, 16% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 21% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Irving

Irving has 53 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 40% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Uplift Education, Coppell ISD and Dallas ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Uplift North Hills Prep High School, Valley Ranch Elementary and Canyon Ranch Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 78 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
