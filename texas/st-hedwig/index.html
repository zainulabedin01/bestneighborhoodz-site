Is St. Hedwig a good place to live? Common questions

Is St. Hedwig, TX a good place to live?

St. Hedwig earns an A on our index, in the top 12% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 654th of 11,459 nationally and 11th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are home ownership and cost of living, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in St. Hedwig?

The median home is worth $282,100, about the same as the Texas median. A median home costs 1.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,884, including property tax.

How long is the commute in St. Hedwig?

The average one-way commute is 31 min, 18% higher than the Texas average. About 6.6% of workers work from home.
