What it's like to live in Shallowater

Shallowater sits about 11 miles from downtown Lubbock, in the Lubbock metro. 2,957 people lived here in 2025, down 0.3% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 37.8, 46% of households have children, 77% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $89,107 a year (14% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Shallowater

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 4 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,202, 14% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.4 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Shallowater

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail, wildfires and winter storms as very high here.

Cost of living in Shallowater

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Shallowater is $1,202 a month, 14% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $48,000 a year. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,050 for a one-bedroom, $1,241 for a two-bedroom and $1,718 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $210,000, 26% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,149 before tax, or $1,472 with property tax. That is $270 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Shallowater pays $3,876 a year, 8% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
