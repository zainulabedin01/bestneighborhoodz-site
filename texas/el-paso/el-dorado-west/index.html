What it's like to live in El Dorado West

El Dorado West is a neighborhood in El Paso with about 2,700 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2006), a mix of owners and renters and 44% of households with children.

Our grade for El Dorado West is B+. That puts it 26th of 74 areas we grade in El Paso, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in El Dorado West

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.7 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in El Dorado West

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,330, 24% higher than the El Paso median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail and winter storms as very high in this part of town.

Questions about El Dorado West

Is El Dorado West a good place to live?

El Dorado West is a neighborhood in El Paso, TX, home to about 2,700 people. It gets a B+, in the top 28% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 26th of 74 areas we score in El Paso. It does best on schools and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in El Dorado West?

Renters in El Dorado West pay a median of $1,330 a month, 24% higher than the El Paso median. The median home is worth $170,000, 8% lower than the El Paso median. A median home costs 2.5x the median household income, compared with 3.1x across El Paso.

What schools serve El Dorado West?

The closest rated public schools are Jane A Hambric School (elementary, rated B), Jane A Hambric School (middle, rated B) and El Dorado High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
