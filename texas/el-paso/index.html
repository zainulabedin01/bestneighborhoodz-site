    Living in El Paso: common questions

    Is El Paso, TX a good place to live?

    El Paso earns a grade of C+ on our index, 38th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on health and jobs and income.

    How much does it cost to live in El Paso?

    Renters in El Paso pay a median of $1,073 a month, 24% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $184,500, 35% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,323, including property tax.

    Is El Paso safe?

    In 2025, El Paso Police Department reported 289 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 26% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,341 per 100,000, 36% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in El Paso?

    We found 166 rated public school campuses inside El Paso. 74% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 67 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in El Paso?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in El Paso are Vista del Sol East, Pebble Hills Park and Northwest El Paso. We score 74 areas in the city.

    Does El Paso flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in El Paso as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: heat waves, hail and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in El Paso?

    Summers in El Paso peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 35°F. Expect around 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 9 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in El Paso?

    The average one-way commute is 23 min, 12% lower than the Texas average. About 8.9% of workers work from home.
