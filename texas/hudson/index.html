Living in Hudson: common questions

Is Hudson, TX a good place to live?

We give Hudson a C+. It ranks 377th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and internet access, and trails on health and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Hudson?

Renters in Hudson pay a median of $1,114 a month, 21% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $171,700, 39% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,141, including property tax.

Are the schools good in Hudson?

We found 4 rated public school campuses inside Hudson. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 88 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Hudson flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Hudson as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Hudson?

Summers in Hudson peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 38°F. Expect around 104 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 50 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Hudson?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 21% lower than the Texas average. About 7.6% of workers work from home. It is about 3 miles from downtown Lufkin.
