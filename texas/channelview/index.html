What it's like to live in Channelview

Channelview sits about 16 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 43,605 people live here. It is suburban.

Places locals use include River Terrace Park, Meadowbrook Park and Carpenter's Hiking Biking Trail.

The median age is 30.9, 50% of households have children, 72% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $64,422 a year (18% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Channelview

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Channelview is $1,279 a month, 9% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $51,000 a year. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $195,900, 31% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,072 before tax, or $1,365 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Channelview pays $3,518 a year, 17% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
