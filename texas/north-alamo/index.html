What it's like to live in North Alamo

North Alamo sits about 8 miles from downtown McAllen, in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metro. About 4,819 people live here. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2003).

The median age is 28.3, 67% of households have children, 72% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $55,408 a year (29% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in North Alamo

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $697, 50% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in North Alamo

  • Higher poverty. 23% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 41% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 167 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in North Alamo

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in North Alamo is $697 a month, 50% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $28,000 a year. Right now 23% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $806 for a one-bedroom, $1,017 for a two-bedroom and $1,305 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $82,900, 71% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $454 before tax, or $564 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in North Alamo pays $1,320 a year, 69% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
