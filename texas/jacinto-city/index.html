    Living in Jacinto City: common questions

    Is Jacinto City, TX a good place to live?

    Jacinto City earns a grade of C- on our index, 484th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and internet access, and trails on education levels and jobs and income.

    How much does it cost to live in Jacinto City?

    Median rent in Jacinto City is $1,242 a month, 11% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $156,200, 45% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,050, including property tax.

    Is Jacinto City safe?

    In 2025, Jacinto City Police Department reported 117 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 70% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,639 per 100,000, 27% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Jacinto City?

    We found 2 rated public school campuses inside Jacinto City. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 65 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Jacinto City?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Jacinto City are Mangum Place, Jacinto Oaks and Songwood. We score 4 areas in the city.

    Does Jacinto City flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Jacinto City as high, and coastal flooding as high. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, coastal flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Jacinto City?

    Summers in Jacinto City peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 44°F. Expect around 109 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 54 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Jacinto City?
