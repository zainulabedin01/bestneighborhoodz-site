What it's like to live in Rosharon

Rosharon sits about 30 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 1,737 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2005).

The median age is 51.5, 22% of households have children, 66% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $102,678 a year (31% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Rosharon

  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.0 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $102,678, 31% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Rosharon

  • Higher unemployment. 40% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 36 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Rosharon

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,212 for a one-bedroom, $1,319 for a two-bedroom and $1,790 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $205,000, 28% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,122 before tax, or $1,539 with property tax.

Daily life in Rosharon

The average one-way commute is 36 minutes, 34% longer than the Texas average. 5% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Houston is about 30 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Rosharon work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
