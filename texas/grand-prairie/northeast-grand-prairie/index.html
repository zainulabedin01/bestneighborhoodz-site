What it's like to live in Northeast Grand Prairie

Northeast Grand Prairie is an area in Grand Prairie with about 21,800 residents, homes built around 1977, mostly owner-occupied (73%) and 42% of households with children.

It grades F against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 10th of 10 areas in Grand Prairie, which grades D overall.

Pros of living in Northeast Grand Prairie

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Northeast Grand Prairie

  • Higher unemployment. 8.6% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.2% across Grand Prairie.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Northeast Grand Prairie

Is Northeast Grand Prairie a good place to live?

Northeast Grand Prairie covers part of Grand Prairie, TX, home to about 21,800 people. It gets an F, in the bottom 8% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 10th of 10 areas we score in Grand Prairie. It does best on home ownership and worst on education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Northeast Grand Prairie?

Median rent in Northeast Grand Prairie is $1,640 a month, about the same as the Grand Prairie median. The median home is worth $221,000, 25% lower than the Grand Prairie median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Grand Prairie.

What schools serve Northeast Grand Prairie?

The closest rated public schools are Hector P Garcia Elementary (elementary, rated F), James Fannin Middle (middle, rated D) and Nimitz High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
