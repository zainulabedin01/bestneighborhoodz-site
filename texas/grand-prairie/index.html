Is Grand Prairie a good place to live? Common questions

Is Grand Prairie, TX a good place to live?

We give Grand Prairie a D. That places it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 671st of 876 nationally and 52nd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are home ownership and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Grand Prairie?

Renters in Grand Prairie pay a median of $1,629 a month, 16% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $295,500, 4% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,030, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Grand Prairie?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Grand Prairie are South Grand Prairie, West Grand Prairie and North Westchester Meadows. We score 10 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Grand Prairie?

The average one-way commute is 28 min, 5% higher than the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home.
