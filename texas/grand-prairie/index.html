    Living in Grand Prairie: common questions

    Is Grand Prairie, TX a good place to live?

    We give Grand Prairie a D. It ranks 65th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on home ownership and safety, and trails on getting around and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Grand Prairie?

    Renters in Grand Prairie pay a median of $1,629 a month, 16% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $295,500, 4% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,030, including property tax.

    Is Grand Prairie safe?

    In 2025, Grand Prairie Police Department reported 202 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 48% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,331 per 100,000, 36% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Grand Prairie?

    We found 57 rated public school campuses inside Grand Prairie. 40% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 22 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Grand Prairie?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Grand Prairie are West Grand Prairie, South Grand Prairie and Lake Port Village. We score 10 areas in the city.

    Does Grand Prairie flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Grand Prairie as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Grand Prairie?

    Summers in Grand Prairie peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 33°F. Expect around 101 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 41 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Grand Prairie?

    The average one-way commute is 28 min, 5% higher than the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home. It is about 16 miles from downtown Dallas.
