What it's like to live in North Westchester Meadows

North Westchester Meadows is a neighborhood in Grand Prairie with about 19,300 residents, homes built around 1995, mostly owner-occupied (76%) and 32% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 5th of 10 areas in Grand Prairie, which grades D overall.

Questions about North Westchester Meadows

Is North Westchester Meadows a good place to live?
