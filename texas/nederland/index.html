What it's like to live in Nederland

Nederland sits about 12 miles from downtown Beaumont, in the Beaumont-Port Arthur metro. 18,108 people lived here in 2025, down 3.9% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Doornbos Heritage Park, "Tex" Ritter Historical Park and Nederland Veterans Park.

The median age is 36.2, 33% of households have children, 73% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $77,570 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Nederland

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 7 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 19 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Nederland

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.

Cost of living in Nederland

Nederland renters pay a median of $1,400 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $56,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 51% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $986 for a one-bedroom, $1,218 for a two-bedroom and $1,588 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $208,300, 27% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,140 before tax, or $1,408 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Nederland pays $3,216 a year, 24% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Nederland safe?

In 2025, Nederland Police Department reported 43 violent crimes and 198 property crimes. That works out to 239 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 38% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,099 property crimes per 100,000, 47% lower than the state rate.

Schools in Nederland

Nederland has 7 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Nederland ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Hillcrest Elementary, Highland Park Elementary and Langham Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 89 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

Daily life in Nederland

Everyday places mapped inside Nederland, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Doornbos Heritage Park, "Tex" Ritter Historical Park, Nederland Veterans Park.

The average one-way commute is 19 minutes, 29% shorter than the Texas average. 3% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Beaumont is about 12 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Nederland work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $77,570, income per person is $36,703, unemployment is 4.7% and 24% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
