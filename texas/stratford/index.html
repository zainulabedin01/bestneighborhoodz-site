What it's like to live in Stratford

Stratford is a town in Sherman County. 1,978 people lived here in 2025, up 2.2% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1961).

Local spots people know include Stratford City Park and Sherman County Library.

The median age is 39.9, 28% of households have children, 79% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $49,286 a year (37% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Stratford

  • Strong public schools. 67% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 17 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Stratford

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Stratford

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $848 for a one-bedroom, $1,065 for a two-bedroom and $1,376 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $129,900, 54% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $711 before tax, or $964 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Stratford pays $3,033 a year, 28% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
