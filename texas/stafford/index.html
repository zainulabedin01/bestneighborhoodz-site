    Living in Stafford: common questions

    Is Stafford, TX a good place to live?

    Stafford gets an overall grade of B and ranks 62nd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and cost of living, and trails on safety and home ownership. It is a strong pick for young professionals, remote workers and people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Stafford?

    Renters in Stafford pay a median of $1,519 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $257,700, 9% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,686, including property tax.

    Is Stafford safe?

    In 2025, Stafford Police Department reported 451 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 16% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,606 per 100,000, 25% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Stafford?

    We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Stafford. 40% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 39 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Stafford?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Stafford are Northwest Stafford, Crestmont Place and Central Stafford. We score 3 areas in the city.

    Does Stafford flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Stafford as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Stafford?

    Summers in Stafford peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 45°F. Expect around 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 50 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Stafford?

    The average one-way commute is 26 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home. It is about 15 miles from downtown Houston.
