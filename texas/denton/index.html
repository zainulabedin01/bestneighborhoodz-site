Is Denton a good place to live? Common questions

Is Denton, TX a good place to live?

We give Denton a C-. That places it just below the middle of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 535th of 876 nationally and 40th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are getting around and education levels, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Denton?

Median rent in Denton is $1,420 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $348,200, 23% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,364, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Denton?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Denton are East Denton, Lake Sharon Estates Phase 3 and South Denton. We score 8 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Denton?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 11% lower than the Texas average. About 14% of workers work from home.
