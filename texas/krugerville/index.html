Living in Krugerville: common questions

Is Krugerville, TX a good place to live?

We give Krugerville a B+. It ranks 147th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on home ownership and jobs and income, and trails on getting around and cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Krugerville?

The median home is worth $426,000, 50% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,858, including property tax.

Does Krugerville flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Krugerville as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Krugerville?

Summers in Krugerville peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 95 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 38 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Krugerville?

The average one-way commute is 32 min, 20% higher than the Texas average. About 18% of workers work from home. It is about 36 miles from downtown Dallas.
