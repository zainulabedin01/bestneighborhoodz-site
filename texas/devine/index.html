Living in Devine: common questions

Is Devine, TX a good place to live?

Devine earns a grade of D- on our index, 638th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on home ownership and errands and things to do, and trails on schools and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Devine?

Renters in Devine pay a median of $725 a month, 48% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $167,900, 41% lower than the Texas median.
