What it's like to live in Cleburne

Cleburne sits about 48 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 39,942 people lived here in 2025, up 27.7% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Buddy Stewart Park, Cleburne City Park and Winston Patrick McGregor Park.

The median age is 35.0, 37% of households have children, 60% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $70,656 a year (10% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Cleburne

  • Low violent crime. 172 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 55% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 28% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Cleburne

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.

Cost of living in Cleburne

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Cleburne is $1,322 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $53,000 a year. Right now 53% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,481 for a one-bedroom, $1,745 for a two-bedroom and $2,284 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $221,100, 22% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,210 before tax, or $1,474 with property tax. That is $152 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Cleburne pays $3,162 a year, 25% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Cleburne safe?

In 2025, Cleburne Police Department reported 69 violent crimes and 351 property crimes. That works out to 172 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 55% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 877 property crimes per 100,000, 58% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 64% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

    Living in Cleburne: common questions

    Median rent in Cleburne is $1,322 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $221,100, 22% lower than the Texas median.
