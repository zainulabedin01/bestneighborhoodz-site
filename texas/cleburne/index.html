Is Cleburne a good place to live? Common questions

Is Cleburne, TX a good place to live?

Cleburne earns a D on our index, in the bottom 24% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2541st of 3,321 nationally and 107th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and cost of living, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Cleburne?

Median rent in Cleburne is $1,322 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $221,100, 22% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,474, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Cleburne?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Cleburne are Bellevue Place, Cross Creek Estates and Westhill Terrace. We score 15 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Cleburne?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 5% lower than the Texas average. About 3.6% of workers work from home.
