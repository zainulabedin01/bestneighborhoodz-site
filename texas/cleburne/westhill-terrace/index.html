What it's like to live in Westhill Terrace

Westhill Terrace is a neighborhood in Cleburne with about 2,400 residents, homes built around 1982, mostly owner-occupied (75%) and 40% of households with children.

Our grade for Westhill Terrace is B+. That puts it 3rd of 15 areas we grade in Cleburne, a city that gets a C overall.

Pros of living in Westhill Terrace

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $99,800, 41% higher than the Cleburne median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Westhill Terrace

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,700, 29% higher than the Cleburne median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and wildfires as very high in this part of town.
