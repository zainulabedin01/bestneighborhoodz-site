What it's like to live in South Socorro

South Socorro is an area in Socorro with about 1,300 residents, homes built around 1993, a mix of owners and renters and 32% of households with children.

Our grade for South Socorro is D-. That puts it 5th of 6 areas we grade in Socorro, a city that gets a C- overall.

Pros of living in South Socorro
