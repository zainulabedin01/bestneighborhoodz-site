What it's like to live in Serenada

Serenada sits about 28 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. About 1,444 people live here. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 54.4, 25% of households have children, 98% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $154,653 a year (97% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Serenada

  • High incomes. The median household earns $154,653, 97% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Serenada

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 31 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail, wildfires and winter storms as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Serenada

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $480,100, 69% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,628 before tax, or $3,110 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Serenada pays $5,783 a year, 37% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
