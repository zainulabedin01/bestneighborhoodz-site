Living in Forest Hill: common questions

Is Forest Hill, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Forest Hill?

Renters in Forest Hill pay a median of $1,763 a month, 26% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $187,400, 34% lower than the Texas median.
