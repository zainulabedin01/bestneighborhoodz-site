What it's like to live in Pearsall

Pearsall is a town in Frio County. 9,170 people lived here in 2025, up 4.5% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Frio Regional Park, Polo Patino Park and Firemans Park.

The median age is 31.4, 38% of households have children, 55% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $49,198 a year (37% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Pearsall

  • Low violent crime. 113 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 71% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $948, 32% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.2 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 19 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Pearsall

  • Weaker school ratings. None of the 4 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher poverty. 28% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 37% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves, hail and wildfires as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 140 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Pearsall

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Pearsall is $948 a month, 32% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $38,000 a year. Right now 47% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $977 for a one-bedroom, $1,227 for a two-bedroom and $1,586 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $106,700, 62% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $584 before tax, or $734 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Pearsall pays $1,802 a year, 57% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
