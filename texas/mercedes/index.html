What it's like to live in Mercedes

Mercedes sits about 21 miles from downtown McAllen, in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metro. 17,915 people lived here in 2025, up 10.2% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Places locals use include H.E.B. Park, Kennedy-Collier Park and Mercedes City Park.

The median age is 33.2, 40% of households have children, 63% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $46,904 a year (40% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Mercedes

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $812, 42% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.4 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 19 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Mercedes

  • Higher poverty. 29% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Many adults uninsured. 38% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, heat waves and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 160 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Mercedes

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Mercedes is $812 a month, 42% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $32,000 a year. Right now 63% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $806 for a one-bedroom, $1,017 for a two-bedroom and $1,305 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $111,800, 61% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $612 before tax, or $772 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Mercedes pays $1,920 a year, 55% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Mercedes safe?

In 2025, Mercedes Police Department reported 61 violent crimes and 325 property crimes. That works out to 341 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 12% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,819 property crimes per 100,000, 13% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 15% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Mercedes

Mercedes has 12 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 50% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by South Texas ISD and Mercedes ISD.

Top-rated campuses include South Texas ISD Science Academy, Mercedes Early College Academy and South Texas ISD Health Professions. The average rating score across all campuses is 81 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
