What it's like to live in Bulverde

Bulverde sits about 22 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 7,337 people lived here in 2025, up 29.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Mammen Family Public Library and Bulverde Spring Branch Library.

The median age is 50.2, 26% of households have children, 92% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $112,188 a year (43% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Bulverde

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 145 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 63% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $112,188, 43% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 30% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Bulverde

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,794, 28% higher than the Texas median.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 33 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 122 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Bulverde

Bulverde renters pay a median of $1,794 a month, 28% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $72,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 15% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $420,200, 48% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,300 before tax, or $2,713 with property tax. That is $919 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Bulverde pays $4,953 a year, 17% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Bulverde

Everyday places mapped inside Bulverde, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Mammen Family Public Library, Bulverde Spring Branch Library.

Downtown San Antonio is about 22 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Bulverde work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
