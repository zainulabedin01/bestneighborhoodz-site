What it's like to live in Brookside Village

Cost of living in Brookside Village

Brookside Village renters pay a median of $1,488 a month, 6% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $60,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,212 for a one-bedroom, $1,319 for a two-bedroom and $1,790 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $352,300, 24% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,928 before tax, or $2,325 with property tax. That is $837 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Brookside Village pays $4,763 a year, 13% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
