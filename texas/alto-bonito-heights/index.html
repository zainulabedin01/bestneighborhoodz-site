What it's like to live in Alto Bonito Heights

Alto Bonito Heights is a town in Starr County. About 1,121 people live here. It is densely built.

The median age is 22.5, 27% of households have children, 98% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $35,625 a year (55% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Alto Bonito Heights

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $775 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Alto Bonito Heights pays $634 a year, 85% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
