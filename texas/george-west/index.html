Living in George West: common questions

Is George West, TX a good place to live?

George West earns a grade of B+ on our index, 199th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and errands and things to do, and trails on health and education levels. It is a strong pick for families and young professionals.

How much does it cost to live in George West?

Renters in George West pay a median of $848 a month, 40% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $151,700, 47% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $972, including property tax.
