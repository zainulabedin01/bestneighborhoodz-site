Living in Balcones Heights: common questions

Is Balcones Heights, TX a good place to live?

Balcones Heights earns a grade of F on our index, 705th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on errands and things to do and getting around, and trails on safety and health.

How much does it cost to live in Balcones Heights?

Median rent in Balcones Heights is $952 a month, 32% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $199,500, 30% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 6.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,403, including property tax.

Is Balcones Heights safe?

In 2025, Balcones Heights Police Department reported 1,853 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 378% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 11,712 per 100,000, 462% higher than the Texas rate.

Does Balcones Heights flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Balcones Heights as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Balcones Heights?

Summers in Balcones Heights peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 34 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Balcones Heights?

The average one-way commute is 25 min, 7% lower than the Texas average. About 5.9% of workers work from home. It is about 2 miles from downtown San Antonio.
