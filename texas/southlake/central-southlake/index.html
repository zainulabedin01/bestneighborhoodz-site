What it's like to live in Central Southlake

Central Southlake is an area in Southlake with about 16,500 residents, homes built around 1997, mostly owner-occupied (93%) and 52% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 5th of 6 areas in Southlake, which grades A+ overall.

Pros of living in Central Southlake

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $2,170, 38% lower than the Southlake median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Central Southlake

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Central Southlake

Is Central Southlake a good place to live?

Central Southlake covers part of Southlake, TX, home to about 16,500 people. It gets an A, in the top 12% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 5th of 6 areas we score in Southlake. It does best on schools and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Central Southlake?

Median rent in Central Southlake is $2,170 a month, 38% lower than the Southlake median. The median home is worth $1,095,000, 8% higher than the Southlake median. A median home costs 4.6x the median household income, compared with 4.1x across Southlake.

What schools serve Central Southlake?

The closest rated public schools are Walnut Grove Elementary (elementary, rated A), Don T Durham Intermediate (middle, rated A) and Carroll High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
