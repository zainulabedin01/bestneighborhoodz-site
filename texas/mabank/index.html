What it's like to live in Mabank

Mabank sits about 48 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 7,294 people lived here in 2025, up 78.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2002).

Places locals use include George Watts Memorial Park, Mabank City Park and Mabank Veterans Memorial Park.

The median age is 33.1, 41% of households have children, 44% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $54,889 a year (30% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Mabank

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $994, 29% lower than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 79% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Mabank

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 4.6 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Mabank

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Mabank is $994 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $40,000 a year. Right now 68% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $254,900, 10% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,395 before tax, or $1,665 with property tax. That is $671 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Mabank pays $3,237 a year, 24% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Schools in Mabank

Top-rated campuses include Mabank High School and Central Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 82 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Daily life in Mabank

Everyday places mapped inside Mabank, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include George Watts Memorial Park, Mabank City Park, Mabank Veterans Memorial Park.

Living in Mabank: common questions

Is Mabank, TX a good place to live?

We give Mabank a C. It ranks 402nd of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on getting around and jobs and income, and trails on cost of living and health.

How much does it cost to live in Mabank?

Median rent in Mabank is $994 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $254,900, 10% lower than the Texas median.
