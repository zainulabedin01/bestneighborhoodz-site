What it's like to live in Southwest Reno

Southwest Reno is an area in Reno with about 1,100 residents, homes built around 1994, mostly owner-occupied (88%) and 31% of households with children.

Our grade for Southwest Reno is D+. That puts it 1st of 3 areas we grade in Reno, a city that gets a C overall.

Questions about Southwest Reno

Is Southwest Reno a good place to live?

Southwest Reno covers part of Reno, TX, home to about 1,000 people. It gets a D+, in the bottom third of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 1st of 3 areas we score in Reno. It does best on jobs and income and worst on education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Southwest Reno?

Renters in Southwest Reno pay a median of $1,370 a month, 8% higher than the Reno median. The median home is worth $133,000, 63% lower than the Reno median. A median home costs 2.0x the median household income, compared with 3.8x across Reno.

The closest rated public schools are Springtown Reno Elementary (elementary, rated B), Santo Forte Junior High (middle, rated D) and Azle High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
