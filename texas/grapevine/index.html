    Living in Grapevine: common questions

    Is Grapevine, TX a good place to live?

    Grapevine gets an overall grade of A- and ranks 18th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on internet access and education levels, and trails on getting around and home ownership. It is a strong pick for young professionals.

    How much does it cost to live in Grapevine?

    Renters in Grapevine pay a median of $1,863 a month, 33% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $493,100, 74% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,305, including property tax.

    Is Grapevine safe?

    In 2025, Grapevine Police Department reported 165 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 57% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,663 per 100,000, 20% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Grapevine?

    We found 10 rated public school campuses inside Grapevine. 90% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 73 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Grapevine?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Grapevine are Northwest Grapevine, Southwest Grapevine and Central Grapevine. We score 6 areas in the city.

    Does Grapevine flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Grapevine as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Grapevine?

    Summers in Grapevine peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 104 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 39 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Grapevine?

    The average one-way commute is 22 min, 16% lower than the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home. It is about 20 miles from downtown Dallas.
