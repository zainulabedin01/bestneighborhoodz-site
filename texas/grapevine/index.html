Is Grapevine a good place to live? Common questions

Is Grapevine, TX a good place to live?

Grapevine gets an A- overall, which puts it in the top 20% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 160th of 876 nationally and 13th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Grapevine?

Renters in Grapevine pay a median of $1,863 a month, 33% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $493,100, 74% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,305, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Grapevine?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Grapevine are Northwest Grapevine, North Grapevine and Southwest Grapevine. We score 6 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Grapevine?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 16% lower than the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home.
