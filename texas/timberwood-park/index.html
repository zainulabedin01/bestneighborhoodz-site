Is Timberwood Park a good place to live? Common questions

Is Timberwood Park, TX a good place to live?

We give Timberwood Park a B+. That places it in the top 28% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 715th of 3,321 nationally and 25th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are internet access and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Timberwood Park?

Renters in Timberwood Park pay a median of $1,998 a month, 42% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $510,600, 80% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,476, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Timberwood Park?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Timberwood Park are The Bluffs, The Falls at Clementson Ranch and Lakeside. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Timberwood Park?
