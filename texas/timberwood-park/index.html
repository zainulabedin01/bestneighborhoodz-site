What it's like to live in Timberwood Park

Timberwood Park sits about 17 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. About 35,413 people live here. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2008).

Local spots people know include Bullis County Park and Bark Park.

The median age is 39.0, 45% of households have children, 86% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $157,100 a year (100% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Timberwood Park

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $157,100, 100% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Timberwood Park

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,998, 42% higher than the Texas median.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 31 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 122 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Timberwood Park

Timberwood Park renters pay a median of $1,998 a month, 42% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $80,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 44% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $510,600, 80% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,795 before tax, or $3,476 with property tax. That is $1,478 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Timberwood Park pays $8,173 a year, 93% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
