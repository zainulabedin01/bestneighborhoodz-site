Living in Archer City: common questions

Is Archer City, TX a good place to live?

Archer City gets an overall grade of A and ranks 58th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and cost of living, and trails on internet access and health. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Archer City?

Renters in Archer City pay a median of $531 a month, 62% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $138,900, 51% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $962, including property tax.

Is Archer City safe?

In 2025, Archer City Police Department reported 61 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 84% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 182 per 100,000, 91% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Archer City?

We found 2 rated public school campuses inside Archer City. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 100 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Archer City flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Archer City as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Archer City?

Summers in Archer City peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 31°F. Expect around 107 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 32 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Archer City?

The average one-way commute is 23 min, 15% lower than the Texas average. About 2.6% of workers work from home. It is about 22 miles from downtown Wichita Falls.
