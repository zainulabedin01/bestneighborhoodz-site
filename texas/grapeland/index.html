What it's like to live in Grapeland

Grapeland is a town in Houston County. 1,475 people lived here in 2025, up 1.2% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Grapeland Public Library and Grapeland City Park.

The median age is 31.9, 31% of households have children, 52% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $39,643 a year (49% lower than the Texas median).

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $872, 38% lower than the Texas median.

Cost of living in Grapeland

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Grapeland is $872 a month, 38% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $35,000 a year. Right now 33% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $829 for a one-bedroom, $1,046 for a two-bedroom and $1,302 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $121,100, 57% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $663 before tax, or $766 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Grapeland pays $1,238 a year, 71% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
