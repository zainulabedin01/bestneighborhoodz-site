What it's like to live in Gatesville

Gatesville sits about 25 miles from downtown Killeen, in the Killeen-Temple metro. 16,422 people lived here in 2025, up 1.8% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Lions Club Park, Gatesville Ball Park and Raby Park bathroom.

The median age is 39.4, 25% of households have children, 64% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $57,217 a year (27% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Gatesville

  • Low violent crime. 140 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 64% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,007, 28% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Gatesville

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, wildfires and winter storms as very high here.

Cost of living in Gatesville

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Gatesville is $1,007 a month, 28% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $40,000 a year. Right now 33% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $927 for a one-bedroom, $1,138 for a two-bedroom and $1,576 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $207,300, 27% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,135 before tax, or $1,325 with property tax. That is $318 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Gatesville pays $2,289 a year, 46% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Gatesville

Everyday places mapped inside Gatesville, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Lions Club Park, Gatesville Ball Park, Raby Park bathroom.

The average one-way commute is 23 minutes, 15% shorter than the Texas average. 4% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Killeen is about 25 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Gatesville work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $57,217, income per person is $17,236, unemployment is 4.5% and 9% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.

Living in Gatesville: common questions

Is Gatesville, TX a good place to live?

We give Gatesville a C. It ranks 105th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on cost of living and safety, and trails on health and errands and things to do. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Gatesville?

Median rent in Gatesville is $1,007 a month, 28% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $207,300, 27% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,325, including property tax.

Is Gatesville safe?

In 2025, Gatesville Police Department reported 140 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 64% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 681 per 100,000, 67% lower than the Texas rate.
