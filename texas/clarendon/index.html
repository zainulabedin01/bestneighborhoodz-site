What it's like to live in Clarendon

Clarendon is a town in Donley County. 1,860 people lived here in 2025, down 1.1% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1964).

Places locals use include Burton Memorial Library and Clarendon City Park.

The median age is 27.8, 25% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $57,250 a year (27% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Clarendon

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $798, 43% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 14 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 2 grocery stores, 14 restaurants and 2 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Clarendon

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high here.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 72% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Clarendon

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Clarendon is $798 a month, 43% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $32,000 a year. Right now 25% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $807 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,208 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $103,300, 64% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $565 before tax, or $737 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Clarendon pays $2,057 a year, 51% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
