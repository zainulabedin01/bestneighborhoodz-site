What it's like to live in Webster

Webster sits about 24 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 12,507 people lived here in 2025, up 0.1% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Texas Avenue Park, Webster Park and Walnut Park.

The median age is 33.3, 22% of households have children, 22% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $61,264 a year (22% lower than the Texas median).

Cons of living in Webster

Cost of living in Webster

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Webster is $1,393 a month, about the same as the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $56,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $281,400, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,540 before tax, or $1,977 with property tax. That is $584 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Webster pays $5,239 a year, 24% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Webster

Everyday places mapped inside Webster, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Texas Avenue Park, Webster Park, Walnut Park.

The average one-way commute is 28 minutes, 4% longer than the Texas average. 7% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Houston is about 24 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Webster work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $61,264, income per person is $41,153, unemployment is 6.5% and 38% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
