What it's like to live in Rockport

Rockport sits about 28 miles from downtown Corpus Christi, in the Corpus Christi metro. 11,348 people lived here in 2025, up 12.7% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Rockport Harbor Fairgrounds and Aransas County Public Library.

The median age is 51.2, 16% of households have children, 71% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $72,174 a year (8% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Rockport

  • Low violent crime. 221 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 43% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,212, 14% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Rockport

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of coastal flooding and hurricanes as very high here.

Cost of living in Rockport

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Rockport is $1,212 a month, 14% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $48,000 a year. Right now 55% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,073 for a one-bedroom, $1,404 for a two-bedroom and $1,944 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $320,500, 13% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,754 before tax, or $2,067 with property tax. That is $855 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Rockport pays $3,753 a year, 11% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
