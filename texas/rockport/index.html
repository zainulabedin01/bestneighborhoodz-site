Is Rockport a good place to live? Common questions

Is Rockport, TX a good place to live?

Rockport earns a C- on our index, just below the middle of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2179th of 3,321 nationally and 87th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are education levels and internet access, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Rockport?

Median rent in Rockport is $1,212 a month, 14% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $320,500, 13% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,067, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Rockport?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Rockport are Palm Harbor, Northwest Rockport and Key Allegro. We score 7 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Rockport?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 19% lower than the Texas average. About 27% of workers work from home.
