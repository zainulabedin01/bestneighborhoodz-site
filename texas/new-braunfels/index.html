Is New Braunfels a good place to live? Common questions

Is New Braunfels, TX a good place to live?

We give New Braunfels a B. That places it in the top 36% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 301st of 876 nationally and 26th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in New Braunfels?

Renters in New Braunfels pay a median of $1,654 a month, 18% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $339,400, 20% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,233, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in New Braunfels?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in New Braunfels are Vista Hills, Starlight Terrace 1 and East New Braunfels. We score 22 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in New Braunfels?

The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 16% of workers work from home.
