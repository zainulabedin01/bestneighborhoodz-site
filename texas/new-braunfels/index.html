What it's like to live in New Braunfels

New Braunfels sits about 30 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 122,492 people lived here in 2025, up 35.5% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is suburban, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2006).

Local spots people know include Panther Canyon, Fischer Park and Fisher Park, New Braunfels , Texas.

The median age is 37.1, 32% of households have children, 65% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $86,021 a year (10% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in New Braunfels

  • Low violent crime. 223 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 42% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 36% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in New Braunfels

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,654, 18% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in New Braunfels

New Braunfels renters pay a median of $1,654 a month, 18% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $66,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $339,400, 20% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,858 before tax, or $2,233 with property tax. That is $579 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in New Braunfels pays $4,500 a year, 6% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is New Braunfels safe?

In 2025, New Braunfels Police Department reported 276 violent crimes and 1,433 property crimes. That works out to 223 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 42% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,159 property crimes per 100,000, 44% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime has been fairly steady since 2021.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.

Schools in New Braunfels

New Braunfels has 22 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 59% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Comal ISD and New Braunfels ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Memorial Early College High School, Carl Schurz Elementary and Lone Star Early Childhood Center. The average rating score across all campuses is 83 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Living in New Braunfels: common questions

    Is New Braunfels, TX a good place to live?

    We give New Braunfels a B+. It ranks 24th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on jobs and income and internet access, and trails on getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in New Braunfels?

    Renters in New Braunfels pay a median of $1,654 a month, 18% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $339,400, 20% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,233, including property tax.

    Is New Braunfels safe?

    In 2025, New Braunfels Police Department reported 223 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 42% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,159 per 100,000, 44% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in New Braunfels?

    We found 22 rated public school campuses inside New Braunfels. 59% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 57 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in New Braunfels?

    Does New Braunfels flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in New Braunfels as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in New Braunfels?

    Summers in New Braunfels peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 37°F. Expect around 117 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 34 inches of rain.
