What it's like to live in Northeast New Braunfels

Northeast New Braunfels is an area in New Braunfels with about 4,800 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2012), mostly owner-occupied (69%) and 35% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 14th of 22 areas in New Braunfels, which grades B+ overall.

Pros of living in Northeast New Braunfels

  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about under 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in Northeast New Braunfels

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,110, 28% higher than the New Braunfels median.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 38 minutes, against 27 for New Braunfels as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Northeast New Braunfels

Is Northeast New Braunfels a good place to live?

Northeast New Braunfels covers part of New Braunfels, TX, home to about 4,800 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 14th of 22 areas we score in New Braunfels. It does best on education levels and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Northeast New Braunfels?

Median rent in Northeast New Braunfels is $2,110 a month, 28% higher than the New Braunfels median. The median home is worth $287,000, 15% lower than the New Braunfels median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.9x across New Braunfels.

What schools serve Northeast New Braunfels?

The closest rated public schools are Oak Creek Elementary (elementary, rated A), Canyon Middle (middle, rated C) and Canyon High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
