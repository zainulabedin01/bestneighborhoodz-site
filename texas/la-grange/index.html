What it's like to live in La Grange

La Grange is a town in Fayette County. 4,509 people lived here in 2025, up 2.9% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include White Rock Park, Kruschel Memorial Park and Lester Park.

The median age is 39.5, 31% of households have children, 55% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $53,547 a year (32% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in La Grange

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $929, 34% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 13 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 4 grocery stores, 35 restaurants and 6 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in La Grange

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 6.3 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 121 days a year at 90°F or hotter.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 78% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in La Grange

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in La Grange is $929 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $37,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $933 for a one-bedroom, $1,093 for a two-bedroom and $1,473 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $338,700, 19% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,854 before tax, or $2,124 with property tax. That is $1,195 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in La Grange pays $3,244 a year, 23% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
