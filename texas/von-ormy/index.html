What it's like to live in Von Ormy

Von Ormy sits about 15 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 1,241 people lived here in 2025, up 5.4% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 36.6, 32% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $38,869 a year (50% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Von Ormy

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,153, 18% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.1 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Von Ormy

  • Higher poverty. 23% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 34% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 134 days a year at 90°F or hotter.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 75% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Von Ormy

Von Ormy renters pay a median of $1,153 a month, 18% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $46,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 68% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $80,000, 72% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $438 before tax, or $539 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Von Ormy pays $1,217 a year, 71% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
