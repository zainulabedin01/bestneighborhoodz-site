What it's like to live in Gainesville

Gainesville is a small city in Cooke County. 18,528 people lived here in 2025, up 6.7% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Keneteso Park, Jaycee Park and Gainesville Parks and Recreation.

The median age is 36.5, 37% of households have children, 58% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $58,809 a year (25% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Gainesville

  • Low violent crime. 211 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 46% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,038, 26% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Gainesville

  • Weaker school ratings. None of the 5 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher unemployment. 8.1% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in Gainesville

Gainesville renters pay a median of $1,038 a month, 26% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $42,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 43% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,004 for a one-bedroom, $1,314 for a two-bedroom and $1,623 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $177,000, 38% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $969 before tax, or $1,167 with property tax. That is $129 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Gainesville pays $2,382 a year, 44% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
