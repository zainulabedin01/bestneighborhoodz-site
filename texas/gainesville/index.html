Is Gainesville a good place to live? Common questions

Is Gainesville, TX a good place to live?

Gainesville gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It ranks 2965th of 3,321 nationally and 152nd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Gainesville?

Renters in Gainesville pay a median of $1,038 a month, 26% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $177,000, 38% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,167, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Gainesville?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Gainesville are Southeast Gainesville, South Gainesville and Central Gainesville. We score 3 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Gainesville?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 17% lower than the Texas average. About 5.6% of workers work from home.
