Living in New Boston: common questions

Is New Boston, TX a good place to live?

New Boston gets an overall grade of C and ranks 407th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on safety and internet access. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in New Boston?

Median rent in New Boston is $850 a month, 39% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $111,500, 61% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $734, including property tax.

Is New Boston safe?

In 2025, New Boston Police Department reported 552 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 43% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 3,159 per 100,000, 51% higher than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in New Boston?

We found 2 rated public school campuses inside New Boston. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 57 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does New Boston flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in New Boston as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in New Boston?

Summers in New Boston peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 32°F. Expect around 88 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 53 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in New Boston?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 11% lower than the Texas average. About 9.0% of workers work from home. It is about 19 miles from downtown Texarkana.
