What it's like to live in Central Henderson

Central Henderson is an area in Henderson with about 5,400 residents, older homes (median year built 1963), a mix of owners and renters and 34% of households with children.

It grades C- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 2nd of 3 areas in Henderson, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in Central Henderson

  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Central Henderson

  • Higher poverty. 23% of residents live below the poverty line, against 15% across Henderson.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and strong wind as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Central Henderson

Is Central Henderson a good place to live?

Central Henderson covers part of Henderson, TX, home to about 5,400 people. It gets a C-, just below the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 2nd of 3 areas we score in Henderson. It does best on getting around and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Central Henderson?

Median rent in Central Henderson is $1,080 a month, 7% higher than the Henderson median. The median home is worth $155,000, 11% lower than the Henderson median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 2.8x across Henderson.

The closest rated public schools are Northside Elementary (elementary, rated D), Henderson Middle (middle, rated C) and Henderson High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
