What it's like to live in Crawford

Crawford sits about 15 miles from downtown Waco, in the Waco metro. 934 people lived here in 2025, up 5.4% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 44.2, 32% of households have children, 91% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $88,458 a year (13% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Crawford

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Crawford is $970 a month, 31% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $39,000 a year. Right now 32% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,029 for a one-bedroom, $1,297 for a two-bedroom and $1,693 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $238,600, 16% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,306 before tax, or $1,661 with property tax. That is $691 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Crawford pays $4,258 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Crawford

Everyday places mapped inside Crawford, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Tonkawa Falls City Park.

The average one-way commute is 26 minutes, 4% shorter than the Texas average. 11% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Waco is about 15 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Crawford work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $88,458, income per person is $42,441, unemployment is 2.8% and 28% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
