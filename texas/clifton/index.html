What it's like to live in Clifton

Clifton sits about 28 miles from downtown Waco, in the Waco metro. 3,523 people lived here in 2025, up 1.6% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Dahl Park, Cockrell-Thiele Park and Clifton City Park.

The median age is 48.8, 30% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $64,653 a year (18% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Clifton

  • Low violent crime. 28 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 93% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,055, 25% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 16 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 6 grocery stores, 22 restaurants and 5 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Clifton

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 115 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Clifton

Clifton renters pay a median of $1,055 a month, 25% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $42,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 34% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $804 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,347 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $160,000, 44% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $876 before tax, or $1,083 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Clifton pays $2,485 a year, 41% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
