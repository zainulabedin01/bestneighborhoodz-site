What it's like to live in Clear Lake Shores

Clear Lake Shores sits about 27 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 1,298 people lived here in 2025, up 1.2% from 2020. It is suburban.

Places locals use include Deep Hole Park, Sundial Park and Jarboe Bayou Park.

The median age is 54.0, 16% of households have children, 76% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $111,818 a year (42% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Clear Lake Shores

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Clear Lake Shores is $1,808 a month, 29% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $72,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $463,400, 63% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,537 before tax, or $3,012 with property tax. That is $1,204 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Clear Lake Shores pays $5,710 a year, 35% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
