    Living in Athens: common questions

    Is Athens, TX a good place to live?

    Athens gets an overall grade of C and ranks 112th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on getting around and cost of living, and trails on internet access and home ownership.

    How much does it cost to live in Athens?

    Renters in Athens pay a median of $1,112 a month, 21% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $210,800, 26% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,378, including property tax.

    Is Athens safe?

    In 2025, Athens Police Department reported 327 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 16% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,040 per 100,000, 50% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Athens?

    We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Athens. 20% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 33 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Athens?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Athens are Northeast Athens, Central Athens and Northwest Athens. We score 3 areas in the city.

    Does Athens flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Athens as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Athens?

    Summers in Athens peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 35°F. Expect around 92 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 45 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Athens?

    The average one-way commute is 21 min, 22% lower than the Texas average. About 3.1% of workers work from home.
