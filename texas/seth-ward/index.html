Cost of living in Seth Ward

Seth Ward renters pay a median of $863 a month, 38% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $35,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $931 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $62,400, 78% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $342 before tax, or $385 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Seth Ward pays $522 a year, 88% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
