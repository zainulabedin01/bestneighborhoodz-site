What it's like to live in Southwest Slaton

Southwest Slaton is an area in Slaton with about 4,300 residents, older homes (median year built 1963), mostly owner-occupied (82%) and 39% of households with children.

It grades C- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 3 areas in Slaton, which grades D+ overall.

Questions about Southwest Slaton

Is Southwest Slaton a good place to live?

Southwest Slaton covers part of Slaton, TX, home to about 4,300 people. It gets a C-, just below the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 1st of 3 areas we score in Slaton. It does best on cost of living and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Southwest Slaton?

Median rent in Southwest Slaton is $940 a month, 5% lower than the Slaton median. The median home is worth $78,000, 8% lower than the Slaton median. A median home costs 1.6x the median household income, compared with 1.5x across Slaton.
