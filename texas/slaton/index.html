How much does it cost to live in Slaton?

Renters in Slaton pay a median of $994 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $84,600, 70% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $580, including property tax.
