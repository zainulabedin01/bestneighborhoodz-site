What it's like to live in Slaton

Slaton sits about 16 miles from downtown Lubbock, in the Lubbock metro. 5,684 people lived here in 2025, down 3.0% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1963).

Local spots people know include Slaton City Park, Russell Park and Elgie Allen Park.

The median age is 35.9, 32% of households have children, 79% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $56,676 a year (28% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Slaton

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $994, 29% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Slaton

  • Weaker school ratings. Only 25% of the 4 rated campuses earn an A or B from the state.
  • Higher violent crime. 884 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 128% higher than the Texas rate.
  • Many adults uninsured. 26% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.

Cost of living in Slaton

Slaton renters pay a median of $994 a month, 29% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $40,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 41% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,050 for a one-bedroom, $1,241 for a two-bedroom and $1,718 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $84,600, 70% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $463 before tax, or $580 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Slaton pays $1,398 a year, 67% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Slaton safe?

In 2025, Slaton Police Department reported 50 violent crimes and 90 property crimes. That works out to 884 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 128% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,592 property crimes per 100,000, 24% lower than the state rate.
