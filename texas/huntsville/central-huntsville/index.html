Questions about Central Huntsville

Is Central Huntsville a good place to live?

Central Huntsville covers part of Huntsville, TX, home to about 15,700 people. It gets an F, in the bottom 8% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 5th of 5 areas we score in Huntsville. It does best on getting around and worst on jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in Central Huntsville?

Median rent in Central Huntsville is $1,070 a month, 3% higher than the Huntsville median. The median home is worth $249,000, 5% higher than the Huntsville median. A median home costs 6.1x the median household income, compared with 5.0x across Huntsville.
