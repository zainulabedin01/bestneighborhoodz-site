Is Wheeler a good place to live? Common questions

Is Wheeler, TX a good place to live?

Wheeler gets a D+ overall, which puts it in the bottom 32% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 8168th of 11,459 nationally and 355th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Wheeler?

Median rent in Wheeler is $994 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $159,000, 44% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,038, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Wheeler?

The average one-way commute is 17 min, 36% lower than the Texas average. About 1.8% of workers work from home.
