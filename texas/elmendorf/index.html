What it's like to live in Elmendorf

Elmendorf sits about 19 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 3,111 people lived here in 2025, up 67.2% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 38.9, 38% of households have children, 83% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $51,691 a year (34% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Elmendorf

  • Low violent crime. 204 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 47% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 67% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Elmendorf

  • Higher unemployment. 11% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Higher poverty. 31% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 32 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Many adults uninsured. 27% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.

Cost of living in Elmendorf

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Elmendorf is $1,327 a month, 5% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $53,000 a year. Right now 69% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $161,300, 43% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $883 before tax, or $1,024 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Elmendorf pays $1,692 a year, 60% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
