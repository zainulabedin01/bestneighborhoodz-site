    Living in Cross Plains: common questions

    Is Cross Plains, TX a good place to live?

    We give Cross Plains a B. It ranks 209th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and internet access, and trails on home ownership and jobs and income. It is a strong pick for remote workers and people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Cross Plains?

    Median rent in Cross Plains is $720 a month, 49% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $79,500, 72% lower than the Texas median.
