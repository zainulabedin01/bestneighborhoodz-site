What it's like to live in Lindale

Lindale sits about 14 miles from downtown Tyler, in the Tyler metro. 7,385 people lived here in 2025, up 23.3% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Darden Harvest Park, Pool Recreational Park and Lindale Baseball-Softball Complex.

The median age is 39.1, 42% of households have children, 65% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $83,249 a year (6% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Lindale

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 6 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 226 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 42% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Plenty close to home. 4 grocery stores, 57 restaurants and 7 parks are mapped in town.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 23% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Lindale

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves, wildfires and strong wind as very high here.

Cost of living in Lindale

Lindale renters pay a median of $1,274 a month, 9% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $51,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 45% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,041 for a one-bedroom, $1,277 for a two-bedroom and $1,718 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $272,800, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,493 before tax, or $1,835 with property tax. That is $561 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Lindale pays $4,098 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Schools in Lindale

Lindale has 6 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Lindale ISD.

Top-rated campuses include College Street Elementary, Early Childhood Center and Velma Penny Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 93 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

Daily life in Lindale

Everyday places mapped inside Lindale, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Darden Harvest Park, Pool Recreational Park, Lindale Baseball-Softball Complex.

Is Lindale, TX a good place to live?
