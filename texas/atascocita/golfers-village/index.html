What it's like to live in Golfers Village

Golfers Village is a neighborhood in Atascocita with about 900 residents, homes built around 1985, mostly owner-occupied (66%) and 12% of households with children.

Our grade for Golfers Village is B+. That puts it 9th of 28 areas we grade in Atascocita, a city that gets a B+ overall.

Pros of living in Golfers Village

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,170, 37% lower than the Atascocita median.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in Golfers Village

  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 51 minutes, against 35 for Atascocita as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Golfers Village

Is Golfers Village a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Golfers Village?
