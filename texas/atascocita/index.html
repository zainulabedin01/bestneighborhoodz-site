What it's like to live in Atascocita

Atascocita sits about 18 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. About 99,354 people live here. It is densely built, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2006).

Places locals use include Lyndsay Lyons Soccer Park, Atascocita Park and Butterfly Garden.

The median age is 35.1, 45% of households have children, 83% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $118,226 a year (51% higher than the Texas median).

Cons of living in Atascocita

  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 111 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Atascocita

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Atascocita is $1,849 a month, 32% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $74,000 a year. Right now 38% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $307,400, 8% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,683 before tax, or $2,152 with property tax. That is $303 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Atascocita pays $5,627 a year, 33% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
