What it's like to live in Garrett

Garrett sits about 30 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 880 people lived here in 2025, up 4.8% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 25.4, 78% of households have children, 70% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $79,792 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Garrett

  • Low violent crime. 226 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 42% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.6 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Garrett

  • Higher unemployment. 17% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Garrett

Garrett renters pay a median of $1,593 a month, 14% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $64,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $125,800, 56% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $689 before tax, or $807 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Garrett pays $1,417 a year, 67% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Garrett

Everyday places mapped inside Garrett, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Garrett City Park.

Downtown Dallas is about 30 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Garrett work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
