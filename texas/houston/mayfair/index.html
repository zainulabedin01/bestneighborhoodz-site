What it's like to live in Mayfair

Mayfair is a neighborhood in Houston with about 15,100 residents, homes built around 1969, a mix of owners and renters and 34% of households with children.

Our grade for Mayfair is D-. That puts it 278th of 320 areas we grade in Houston, a city that gets a D+ overall.

Pros of living in Mayfair

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Mayfair

  • Higher poverty. 35% of residents live below the poverty line, against 20% across Houston.
  • Higher unemployment. 21% of the labor force is out of work, against 7.0% across Houston.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Mayfair

Is Mayfair a good place to live?

Mayfair is a neighborhood in Houston, TX, home to about 15,100 people. It gets a D-, in the bottom 16% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 278th of 320 areas we score in Houston. It does best on getting around and worst on jobs and income.

How much does it cost to live in Mayfair?
