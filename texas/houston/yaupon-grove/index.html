What it's like to live in Yaupon Grove

Yaupon Grove is a neighborhood in Houston with about 5,200 residents, homes built around 1979, mostly rented (92% of homes) and 33% of households with children.

It grades D- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 268th of 320 areas in Houston, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in Yaupon Grove

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.7 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in Yaupon Grove

  • Higher poverty. 46% of residents live below the poverty line, against 20% across Houston.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.
