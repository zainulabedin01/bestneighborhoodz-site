About Houston

Houston sits on the coastal plain of Southeast Texas, just inland from Galveston Bay and the Gulf of Mexico. Most of the city is in Harris County, with edges reaching into Fort Bend and Montgomery counties. Land investors founded it in 1836 and named it after Sam Houston, and slow, muddy bayous like Buffalo Bayou, White Oak Bayou and Brays Bayou still wind through town.

Getting around mostly means driving. Loop 610 circles the inner city, Beltway 8 runs about 10 miles out, and the Grand Parkway (SH 99) loops farther still, with I-10, I-45 and I-69 cutting across them. Work here leans on energy companies like ExxonMobil, ConocoPhillips and Phillips 66, the Port of Houston on the Ship Channel, the Texas Medical Center and NASA's Johnson Space Center. Locals know the Museum District, the University of Houston, NRG Stadium and the trails along Buffalo Bayou.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org

What it's like to live in Houston

Houston is the anchor city of the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro area. 2,397,315 people lived here in 2025, up 4.3% from 2020.

Places locals use include Bear Creek Pioneers Park, Buffalo Bayou Park and Keith-Wiess Park.

The median age is 34.4, 29% of households have children, 42% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $64,813 a year (17% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Houston

  • Plenty close to home. 1,100 grocery stores, 14,697 restaurants and 1,173 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Houston

  • Higher poverty. 20% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.

Cost of living in Houston

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Houston is $1,361 a month, about the same as the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $54,000 a year. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $277,800, about the same as the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,521 before tax, or $1,870 with property tax. That is $509 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Houston pays $4,198 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Houston safe?

In 2025, Houston Police Department reported 21,345 violent crimes and 92,382 property crimes. That works out to 884 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 128% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 3,828 property crimes per 100,000, 84% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 28% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Houston

Houston has 539 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 62% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Spring Branch ISD, Alief ISD and Houston ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Spring Branch Academic Institute, Alief Early College High School and Carnegie Vanguard High School. The average rating score across all campuses is 82 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    How much does it cost to live in Houston?

    Median rent in Houston is $1,361 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $277,800, about the same as the Texas median. A median home costs 4.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,870, including property tax.

    Is Houston safe?

    In 2025, Houston Police Department reported 884 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 128% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 3,828 per 100,000, 84% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Houston?

    We found 539 rated public school campuses inside Houston. 62% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 49 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Houston?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Houston are Washington Avenue Coalition / Memorial Park, Boulevard Oaks and Braeswood. We score 320 areas in the city.

    Does Houston flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Houston as high, and coastal flooding as very low. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes and heat waves. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Houston?

    Summers in Houston peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 44°F. Expect around 109 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 54 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Houston?

    The average one-way commute is 27 min, about the same as the Texas average. About 13% of workers work from home.
