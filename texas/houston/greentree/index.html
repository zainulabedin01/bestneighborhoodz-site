What it's like to live in Greentree

Greentree is a neighborhood in Houston with about 5,700 residents, homes built around 1987, mostly owner-occupied (89%) and 41% of households with children.

Our grade for Greentree is A-. That puts it 75th of 320 areas we grade in Houston, a city that gets a D+ overall.

Pros of living in Greentree

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $130,800, 102% higher than the Houston median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.3 miles away.

Cons of living in Greentree

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,500, 84% higher than the Houston median.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 38 minutes, against 27 for Houston as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Greentree

Is Greentree a good place to live?

Greentree is a neighborhood in Houston, TX, home to about 5,700 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 75th of 320 areas we score in Houston. It does best on health and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Greentree?

Renters in Greentree pay a median of $2,500 a month, 84% higher than the Houston median. The median home is worth $332,000, 20% higher than the Houston median. A median home costs 2.5x the median household income, compared with 4.3x across Houston.

What schools serve Greentree?

The closest rated public schools are Hidden Hollow Elementary (elementary, rated B), Creekwood Middle (middle, rated A) and Kingwood High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
