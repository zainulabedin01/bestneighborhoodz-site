What it's like to live in Settegast

Settegast is a neighborhood in Houston with about 5,800 residents, older homes (median year built 1964), mostly owner-occupied (67%) and 35% of households with children.

It grades F against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 313th of 320 areas in Houston, which grades D+ overall.

Pros of living in Settegast

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in Settegast

  • Higher poverty. 42% of residents live below the poverty line, against 20% across Houston.
  • Higher unemployment. 34% of the labor force is out of work, against 7.0% across Houston.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 43 minutes, against 27 for Houston as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, hurricanes and tornadoes as very high in this part of town.
