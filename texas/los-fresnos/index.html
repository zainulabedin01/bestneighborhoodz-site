What it's like to live in Los Fresnos

Los Fresnos sits about 6 miles from downtown Brownsville, in the Brownsville-Harlingen metro. 8,703 people lived here in 2025, up 6.8% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Memorial Park, Los Fresnos City Park and Los Fresnos Nature Park.

The median age is 27.6, 56% of households have children, 62% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $61,938 a year (21% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Los Fresnos

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 4 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $989, 30% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Plenty close to home. 2 grocery stores, 53 restaurants and 6 parks are mapped in town.

Cons of living in Los Fresnos

  • Higher violent crime. 715 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 85% higher than the Texas rate.
  • Higher poverty. 35% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 36% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, heat waves and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 121 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Los Fresnos

Los Fresnos renters pay a median of $989 a month, 30% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $40,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 63% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $884 for a one-bedroom, $1,042 for a two-bedroom and $1,393 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $152,200, 46% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $833 before tax, or $1,068 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Los Fresnos pays $2,816 a year, 33% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Los Fresnos safe?

In 2025, Los Fresnos Police Department reported 62 violent crimes and 126 property crimes. That works out to 715 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 85% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 1,454 property crimes per 100,000, 30% lower than the state rate.

Schools in Los Fresnos

Los Fresnos has 4 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Los Fresnos CISD.

Top-rated campuses include Lopez-Riggins Elementary and Los Cuates Middle. The average rating score across all campuses is 89 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

Daily life in Los Fresnos

Everyday places mapped inside Los Fresnos, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Memorial Park, Los Fresnos City Park, Los Fresnos Nature Park.

The average one-way commute is 22 minutes, 16% shorter than the Texas average. 4% of workers work from home. 5.0% walk, bike or take transit to work.

Downtown Brownsville is about 6 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Los Fresnos work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $61,938, income per person is $24,856, unemployment is 3.6% and 24% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.
