    Living in Georgetown: common questions

    Is Georgetown, TX a good place to live?

    Georgetown gets an overall grade of C+ and ranks 43rd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on home ownership and safety, and trails on errands and things to do and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Georgetown?

    Renters in Georgetown pay a median of $1,795 a month, 28% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $429,100, 51% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,869, including property tax.

    Is Georgetown safe?

    In 2025, Georgetown Police Department reported 149 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 62% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 789 per 100,000, 62% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Georgetown?

    We found 16 rated public school campuses inside Georgetown. 31% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 13 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Georgetown?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Georgetown are Southwest Georgetown, Lakewood Estates and Chaparro Estates. We score 19 areas in the city.

    Does Georgetown flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Georgetown as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Georgetown?

    Summers in Georgetown peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 36°F. Expect around 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 37 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Georgetown?

    The average one-way commute is 28 min, 6% higher than the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home. It is about 26 miles from downtown Austin.
