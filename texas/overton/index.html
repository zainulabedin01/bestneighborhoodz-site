What it's like to live in Overton

Overton sits about 21 miles from downtown Longview, in the Longview metro. 2,315 people lived here in 2025, up 1.8% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Overton City Park and McMillan Memorial Library.

The median age is 32.5, 38% of households have children, 66% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $52,279 a year (33% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Overton

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $783, 44% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.2 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Overton

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves, wildfires and strong wind as very high here.

Cost of living in Overton

Overton renters pay a median of $783 a month, 44% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $31,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 24% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $921 for a one-bedroom, $1,205 for a two-bedroom and $1,448 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $115,300, 59% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $631 before tax, or $758 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Overton pays $1,526 a year, 64% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Overton
