How much does it cost to live in Donna?

Median rent in Donna is $806 a month, 43% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $86,100, 70% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $588, including property tax.
