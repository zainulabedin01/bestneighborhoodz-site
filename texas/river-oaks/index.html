Living in River Oaks: common questions

Is River Oaks, TX a good place to live?

River Oaks earns a grade of C on our index, 435th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on home ownership and errands and things to do, and trails on health and education levels.

How much does it cost to live in River Oaks?

Renters in River Oaks pay a median of $1,183 a month, 16% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $223,800, 21% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,455, including property tax.

Is River Oaks safe?

In 2025, River Oaks Police Department reported 161 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 58% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 953 per 100,000, 54% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in River Oaks?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside River Oaks. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 54 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does River Oaks flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in River Oaks as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in River Oaks?

Summers in River Oaks peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 37°F. Expect around 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 33 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in River Oaks?

The average one-way commute is 28 min, 6% higher than the Texas average. About 7.7% of workers work from home. It is about 37 miles from downtown Dallas.
