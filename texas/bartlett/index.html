Is Bartlett a good place to live? Common questions

Is Bartlett, TX a good place to live?

Bartlett earns a D+ on our index, in the bottom 32% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 8295th of 11,459 nationally and 367th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Bartlett?

Median rent in Bartlett is $756 a month, 46% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $218,900, 23% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,405, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Bartlett?

The average one-way commute is 34 min, 27% higher than the Texas average. About 7.4% of workers work from home.
