What it's like to live in Bartlett

Bartlett sits about 39 miles from downtown Austin, in the Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos metro. 1,678 people lived here in 2025, up 3.1% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1957).

Places locals use include Bartlett City Park and Teinert Memorial Public Library.

The median age is 39.0, 35% of households have children, 82% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $67,438 a year (14% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Bartlett

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Bartlett is $756 a month, 46% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $30,000 a year. Right now 34% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,530 for a one-bedroom, $1,817 for a two-bedroom and $2,292 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $218,900, 23% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,198 before tax, or $1,405 with property tax. That is $649 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Bartlett pays $2,483 a year, 41% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
