Is McKinney a good place to live? Common questions

Is McKinney, TX a good place to live?

McKinney earns an A- on our index, in the top 20% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 137th of 876 nationally and 10th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and education levels, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in McKinney?

Median rent in McKinney is $1,901 a month, 35% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $471,800, 66% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,232, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in McKinney?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in McKinney are Northwest McKinney, West McKinney and Southwest McKinney. We score 9 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in McKinney?

The average one-way commute is 28 min, 4% higher than the Texas average. About 24% of workers work from home.
