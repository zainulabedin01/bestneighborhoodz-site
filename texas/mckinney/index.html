What it's like to live in McKinney

McKinney sits about 29 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 236,001 people lived here in 2025, up 20.8% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2007).

Places locals use include McKinney Texas Dog Park, Wilson Creek Park and Gabe Nesbitt Community Park.

The median age is 36.9, 42% of households have children, 64% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $124,215 a year (58% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in McKinney

  • Strong public schools. 78% of the 46 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 115 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 70% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $124,215, 58% higher than the Texas median.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 21% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in McKinney

  • Higher rent. Median rent is $1,901, 35% higher than the Texas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high here.

Cost of living in McKinney

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in McKinney is $1,901 a month, 35% higher than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $76,000 a year. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $471,800, 66% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,582 before tax, or $3,232 with property tax. That is $1,331 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in McKinney pays $7,791 a year, 84% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is McKinney safe?

In 2025, McKinney Police Department reported 271 violent crimes and 1,602 property crimes. That works out to 115 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 70% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 680 property crimes per 100,000, 67% lower than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 11% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.

Schools in McKinney

McKinney has 46 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 78% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Imagine International Academy of North Texas and McKinney ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Imagine International Academy of North Texas, Roy Lee Walker Elementary and McKinney North High School. The average rating score across all campuses is 87 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
