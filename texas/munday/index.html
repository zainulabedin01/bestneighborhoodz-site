Is Munday, TX a good place to live?

We give Munday a C+. That places it around the middle of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 5105th of 11,459 nationally and 158th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Munday?

Median rent in Munday is $481 a month, 66% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $79,500, 72% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $571, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Munday?

The average one-way commute is 12 min, 54% lower than the Texas average. About 1.7% of workers work from home.
