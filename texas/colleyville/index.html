    Living in Colleyville: common questions

    Is Colleyville, TX a good place to live?

    We give Colleyville an A+. It ranks 6th of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on internet access and jobs and income, and trails on cost of living and getting around. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Colleyville?

    Median rent in Colleyville is $3,263 a month, 133% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $784,900, 177% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $5,130, including property tax.

    Is Colleyville safe?

    In 2025, Colleyville Police Department reported 35 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 91% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 485 per 100,000, 77% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Colleyville?

    We found 8 rated public school campuses inside Colleyville. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 87 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Colleyville?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Colleyville are South Colleyville, North Colleyville and Southeast Colleyville. We score 6 areas in the city.

    Does Colleyville flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Colleyville as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Colleyville?

    Summers in Colleyville peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 104 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 39 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Colleyville?

    The average one-way commute is 24 min, 9% lower than the Texas average. About 30% of workers work from home. It is about 23 miles from downtown Dallas.
