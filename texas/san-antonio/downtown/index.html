What it's like to live in Downtown

Downtown is a neighborhood in San Antonio with about 2,300 residents, homes built around 1973, mostly rented (92% of homes) and 1% of households with children.

It grades C- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 83rd of 137 areas in San Antonio, which grades D overall.

Pros of living in Downtown

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.1 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about under 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in Downtown

  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Downtown

Is Downtown a good place to live?

Downtown is a neighborhood in San Antonio, TX, home to about 2,300 people. It gets a C-, just below the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 83rd of 137 areas we score in San Antonio. It does best on getting around and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Downtown?

Median rent in Downtown is $1,460 a month, 10% higher than the San Antonio median. The median home is worth $621,000, 163% higher than the San Antonio median. A median home costs 13.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across San Antonio.
