About San Antonio

About 75 miles southwest of Austin on I-35, San Antonio is the seat of Bexar County in South Central Texas. It began in 1718 as a Spanish mission and outpost, and in 1731 it became the first chartered civil settlement in what is now Texas. The Alamo and four other missions along the San Antonio River are now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Daily driving runs on I-10, I-35 and I-37, tied together by Loop 410 on the inside and Loop 1604 farther out. The military is a big part of town through Joint Base San Antonio, which covers Fort Sam Houston, Lackland and Randolph. Big local employers also include USAA, Valero Energy, H-E-B and the South Texas Medical Center, and tourism keeps downtown busy. The River Walk winds through the center, and colleges include UTSA, Trinity University, St. Mary's University and the University of the Incarnate Word.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org

What it's like to live in San Antonio

San Antonio is the anchor city of the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro area. 1,548,422 people lived here in 2025, up 8.0% from 2020.

Local spots people know include McAllister Park, San Antonio Missions National Historical Park and South Side Lions Park.

The median age is 34.9, 31% of households have children, 52% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $65,056 a year (17% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in San Antonio

San Antonio renters pay a median of $1,324 a month, 6% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $53,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $235,700, 17% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,290 before tax, or $1,634 with property tax. That is $310 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in San Antonio pays $4,122 a year, about the same as the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is San Antonio safe?

In 2025, San Antonio Police Department reported 8,241 violent crimes and 60,693 property crimes. That works out to 532 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 37% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 3,917 property crimes per 100,000, 88% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 24% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in San Antonio

San Antonio has 381 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 42% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Basis Texas, San Antonio ISD and Southwest ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Basis San Antonio- Shavano Campus, St Philip's College Early College High School and Travis Early College High School. The average rating score across all campuses is 76 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.
