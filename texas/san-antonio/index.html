Is San Antonio a good place to live? Common questions

Is San Antonio, TX a good place to live?

We give San Antonio a D. That places it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 698th of 876 nationally and 58th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in San Antonio?

Renters in San Antonio pay a median of $1,324 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $235,700, 17% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,634, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in San Antonio?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in San Antonio are Park Forest, Hunters Creek and Whispering Oaks. We score 137 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in San Antonio?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 8% lower than the Texas average. About 14% of workers work from home.
