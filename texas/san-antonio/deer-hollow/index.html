What it's like to live in Deer Hollow

Deer Hollow is a neighborhood in San Antonio with about 3,200 residents, homes built around 1995, mostly owner-occupied (98%) and 35% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 14th of 137 areas in San Antonio, which grades D overall.

Pros of living in Deer Hollow

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $198,000, 204% higher than the San Antonio median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.5 miles away.

Cons of living in Deer Hollow

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail as very high in this part of town.
