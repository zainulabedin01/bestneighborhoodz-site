What it's like to live in James Court

James Court is a neighborhood in Edinburg with about 1,900 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2006), mostly owner-occupied (73%) and 26% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 3rd of 35 areas in Edinburg, which grades C+ overall.

Pros of living in James Court

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in James Court

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about James Court

Is James Court a good place to live?

James Court is a neighborhood in Edinburg, TX, home to about 1,900 people. It gets an A, in the top 12% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 3rd of 35 areas we score in Edinburg. It does best on schools.

How much does it cost to live in James Court?

Median rent in James Court is $1,040 a month, 4% higher than the Edinburg median. The median home is worth $299,000, 58% higher than the Edinburg median. A median home costs 3.9x the median household income, compared with 3.2x across Edinburg.
