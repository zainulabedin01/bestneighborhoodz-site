Is Edinburg a good place to live? Common questions

Is Edinburg, TX a good place to live?

Edinburg gets a D overall, which puts it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 697th of 876 nationally and 57th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Edinburg?

Median rent in Edinburg is $996 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $189,600, 33% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,338, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Edinburg?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Edinburg are Victoria Heights, Southeast Edinburg and Sprague Landing. We score 35 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Edinburg?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 18% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home.
