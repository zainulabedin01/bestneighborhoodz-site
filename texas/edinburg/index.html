    Living in Edinburg: common questions

    Is Edinburg, TX a good place to live?

    Edinburg gets an overall grade of C+ and ranks 39th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on health and jobs and income. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

    How much does it cost to live in Edinburg?

    Median rent in Edinburg is $996 a month, 29% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $189,600, 33% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,338, including property tax.

    Is Edinburg safe?

    In 2025, Edinburg Police Department reported 213 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 45% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,487 per 100,000, 29% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Edinburg?

    We found 36 rated public school campuses inside Edinburg. 83% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 78 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Edinburg?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Edinburg are Victoria Heights, The Meadows and James Court. We score 35 areas in the city.

    Does Edinburg flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Edinburg as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Edinburg?

    Summers in Edinburg peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 49°F. Expect around 167 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 24 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Edinburg?

    The average one-way commute is 22 min, 18% lower than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home. It is about 9 miles from downtown McAllen.
