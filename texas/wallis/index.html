    Living in Wallis: common questions

    Is Wallis, TX a good place to live?

    Wallis gets an overall grade of D and ranks 587th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and schools, and trails on jobs and income and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Wallis?

    Median rent in Wallis is $744 a month, 47% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $157,200, 45% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,073, including property tax.

    Is Wallis safe?

    In 2025, Wallis Police Department reported 291 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 25% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 581 per 100,000, 72% lower than the Texas rate.

    What is the weather like in Wallis?

    Summers in Wallis peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 40°F. Expect around 116 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 47 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Wallis?

    The average one-way commute is 32 min, 22% higher than the Texas average. About 4.5% of workers work from home. It is about 42 miles from downtown Houston.
