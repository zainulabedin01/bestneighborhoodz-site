Is Midland a good place to live? Common questions

Is Midland, TX a good place to live?

We give Midland a C+. That places it around the middle of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 400th of 876 nationally and 33rd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and cost of living, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Midland?

Renters in Midland pay a median of $1,434 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $319,200, 12% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,086, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Midland?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Midland are Country Sky, Grandridge Park and Grafaland. We score 76 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Midland?

The average one-way commute is 20 min, 25% lower than the Texas average. About 6.2% of workers work from home.
