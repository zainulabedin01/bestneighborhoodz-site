Is Westway, TX a good place to live?

We give Westway an F. That places it in the bottom 8% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 10672nd of 11,459 nationally and 654th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are home ownership and cost of living, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Westway?

Median rent in Westway is $1,152 a month, 18% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $106,000, 63% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $697, including property tax.
