What it's like to live in La Marque

La Marque sits about 37 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 20,324 people lived here in 2025, up 10.6% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Carbide Park, Bobby Beach Park and Jaycee Park.

The median age is 39.0, 35% of households have children, 69% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $71,102 a year (9% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in La Marque

La Marque renters pay a median of $1,443 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $58,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 61% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,261 for a one-bedroom, $1,497 for a two-bedroom and $2,017 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $206,900, 27% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,133 before tax, or $1,332 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in La Marque pays $2,394 a year, 43% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
