What it's like to live in Danbury

Danbury sits about 39 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 1,683 people lived here in 2025, up 0.8% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Skrabanek Park and Danbury Community Library.

The median age is 32.6, 45% of households have children, 78% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $92,159 a year (17% higher than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Danbury

Danbury renters pay a median of $1,425 a month, about the same as the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $57,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,212 for a one-bedroom, $1,319 for a two-bedroom and $1,790 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $233,400, 18% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,278 before tax, or $1,572 with property tax. That is $147 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Danbury pays $3,528 a year, 17% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
