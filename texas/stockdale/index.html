What it's like to live in Stockdale

Stockdale sits about 37 miles from downtown San Antonio, in the San Antonio-New Braunfels metro. 1,494 people lived here in 2025, up 6.2% from 2020. It is spread out.

Places locals use include Stockdale City Park, Bryce Field and Sarah Bain Chandler Public Library.

The median age is 42.8, 36% of households have children, 61% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $52,532 a year (33% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Stockdale

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $917, 35% lower than the Texas median.

Cons of living in Stockdale

  • Higher poverty. 25% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 36 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 121 days a year at 90°F or hotter.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 80% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Stockdale

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Stockdale is $917 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $37,000 a year. Right now 42% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,196 for a one-bedroom, $1,441 for a two-bedroom and $1,864 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $173,300, 39% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $949 before tax, or $1,142 with property tax. That is $225 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Stockdale pays $2,324 a year, 45% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Stockdale
