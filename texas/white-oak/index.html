What it's like to live in White Oak

White Oak sits about 6 miles from downtown Longview, in the Longview metro. 6,186 people lived here in 2025, down 0.5% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include White Oak City Park, Penick Park and White Oak School Community Library.

The median age is 32.5, 44% of households have children, 67% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $101,027 a year (29% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in White Oak

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 4 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 112 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 71% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,194, 15% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.3 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $101,027, 29% higher than the Texas median.

Cons of living in White Oak

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, heat waves and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 112 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in White Oak

White Oak renters pay a median of $1,194 a month, 15% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $48,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 32% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,041 for a one-bedroom, $1,232 for a two-bedroom and $1,611 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $235,000, 17% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,286 before tax, or $1,467 with property tax. That is $273 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in White Oak pays $2,163 a year, 49% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in White Oak

Everyday places mapped inside White Oak, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include White Oak City Park, Penick Park, White Oak School Community Library.

The average one-way commute is 20 minutes, 27% shorter than the Texas average. 6% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Longview is about 6 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in White Oak work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.

The median household income is $101,027, income per person is $30,841, unemployment is 5.0% and 19% of adults hold a bachelor's degree.

Is White Oak, TX a good place to live?
