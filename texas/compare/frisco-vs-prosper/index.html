Cost of living

Frisco is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Frisco: a median of $2,014 a month, 7% lower than Prosper's $2,176.

The median home is worth $642,100 in Frisco and $823,700 in Prosper. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $4,312 a month against $5,342, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.3 times income in Frisco and 4.2 times in Prosper.

Commute and location

Commutes average 29 minutes in Frisco and 29 in Prosper.

The two are about 6 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Prosper has less violent crime. Frisco reported 98 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Prosper reported 43 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 91% of Frisco's 67 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 100% of Prosper's 14.
