Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Rent is higher in Rowlett: a median of $1,990 a month, 11% higher than Sachse's $1,798.

The median home is worth $368,800 in Rowlett and $406,200 in Sachse. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,508 a month against $2,796, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.3 times income in Rowlett and 3.1 times in Sachse.

Commute and location

Commutes average 31 minutes in Rowlett and 30 in Sachse.

The two are about 4 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Sachse has less violent crime. Rowlett reported 157 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Sachse reported 69 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 21% of Rowlett's 14 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 83% of Sachse's 6.
