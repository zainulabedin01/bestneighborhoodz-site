Cost of living

Plano is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Frisco: a median of $2,014 a month, 9% higher than Plano's $1,841.

The median home is worth $642,100 in Frisco and $465,900 in Plano. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $4,312 a month against $3,109, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.3 times income in Frisco and 4.2 times in Plano.

Commute and location

Commutes average 29 minutes in Frisco and 25 in Plano.

The two are about 7 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Frisco has less violent crime. Frisco reported 98 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Plano reported 147 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 91% of Frisco's 67 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 75% of Plano's 67.
