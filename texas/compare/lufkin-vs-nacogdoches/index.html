Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Rent is higher in Lufkin: a median of $1,079 a month, 12% higher than Nacogdoches's $964.

The median home is worth $179,600 in Lufkin and $186,000 in Nacogdoches. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,196 a month against $1,159, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.1 times income in Lufkin and 4.7 times in Nacogdoches.

Commute and location

Commutes average 17 minutes in Lufkin and 16 in Nacogdoches.

The two are about 21 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Nacogdoches has less violent crime. Lufkin reported 441 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Nacogdoches reported 296 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 56% of Lufkin's 16 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 22% of Nacogdoches's 9.
