Cost of living

San Marcos is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Kyle: a median of $1,752 a month, 33% higher than San Marcos's $1,322.

The median home is worth $334,600 in Kyle and $310,400 in San Marcos. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,346 a month against $2,097, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.7 times income in Kyle and 6.1 times in San Marcos.

Commute and location

Commutes average 36 minutes in Kyle and 24 in San Marcos.

The two are about 10 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Kyle has less violent crime. Kyle reported 135 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and San Marcos reported 422 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 25% of Kyle's 12 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 50% of San Marcos's 12.
