Cost of living

Alvin is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Alvin: a median of $1,215 a month, 28% lower than Friendswood's $1,688.

The median home is worth $228,700 in Alvin and $420,100 in Friendswood. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,596 a month against $2,828, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.2 times income in Alvin and 3.3 times in Friendswood.

Commute and location

Commutes average 29 minutes in Alvin and 29 in Friendswood.

The two are about 8 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Friendswood has less violent crime. Alvin reported 128 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Friendswood reported 82 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 73% of Alvin's 11 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 100% of Friendswood's 8.
