Cost of living

Universal City is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Converse: a median of $1,693 a month, 29% higher than Universal City's $1,315.

The median home is worth $248,400 in Converse and $269,600 in Universal City. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,742 a month against $1,905, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 2.6 times income in Converse and 3.6 times in Universal City.

Commute and location

Commutes average 27 minutes in Converse and 26 in Universal City.

The two are about 3 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Universal City has less violent crime. Converse reported 470 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Universal City reported 167 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 17% of Converse's 6 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 0% of Universal City's 4.
