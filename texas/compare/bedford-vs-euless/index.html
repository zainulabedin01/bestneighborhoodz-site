Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Rent is lower in Bedford: a median of $1,502 a month, 11% lower than Euless's $1,690.

The median home is worth $350,700 in Bedford and $318,800 in Euless. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,369 a month against $2,133, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.2 times income in Bedford and 3.9 times in Euless.

Commute and location

Commutes average 24 minutes in Bedford and 25 in Euless.

The two are about 4 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Euless has less violent crime. Bedford reported 180 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Euless reported 159 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 88% of Bedford's 8 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 92% of Euless's 13.
