Cost of living

West Odessa is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Odessa: a median of $1,315 a month, 48% higher than West Odessa's $891.

The median home is worth $223,100 in Odessa and $126,300 in West Odessa. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,479 a month against $802, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.0 times income in Odessa and 1.9 times in West Odessa.

Commute and location

Commutes average 22 minutes in Odessa and 34 in West Odessa.

The two are about 8 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

We don't have a full year of police data for both cities, so we can't compare crime fairly.

For schools, 42% of Odessa's 41 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 25% of West Odessa's 4.
