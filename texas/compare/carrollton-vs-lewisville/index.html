Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Median rent is about the same: $1,735 a month in Carrollton and $1,670 in Lewisville.

The median home is worth $381,000 in Carrollton and $378,500 in Lewisville. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,549 a month against $2,555, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.8 times income in Carrollton and 4.2 times in Lewisville.

Commute and location

Commutes average 24 minutes in Carrollton and 25 in Lewisville.

The two are about 5 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Carrollton has less violent crime. Carrollton reported 98 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Lewisville reported 218 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 81% of Carrollton's 32 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 35% of Lewisville's 26.
