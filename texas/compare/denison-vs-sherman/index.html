Cost of living

Denison is the cheaper of the two.

Median rent is about the same: $1,309 a month in Denison and $1,324 in Sherman.

The median home is worth $180,900 in Denison and $243,500 in Sherman. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,190 a month against $1,612, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 2.7 times income in Denison and 4.1 times in Sherman.

Commute and location

Commutes average 19 minutes in Denison and 23 in Sherman.

The two are about 11 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Denison has less violent crime. Denison reported 226 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Sherman reported 298 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 22% of Denison's 9 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 0% of Sherman's 11.
