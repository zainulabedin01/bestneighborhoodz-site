Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Median rent is about the same: $1,841 a month in Plano and $1,857 in Richardson.

The median home is worth $465,900 in Plano and $431,400 in Richardson. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $3,109 a month against $2,902, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.2 times income in Plano and 4.4 times in Richardson.

Commute and location

Commutes average 25 minutes in Plano and 24 in Richardson.

The two are about 7 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Richardson has less violent crime. Plano reported 147 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Richardson reported 138 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 75% of Plano's 67 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 71% of Richardson's 24.
