Cost of living

Dickinson is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Dickinson: a median of $1,231 a month, 27% lower than League City's $1,684.

The median home is worth $278,000 in Dickinson and $360,000 in League City. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,868 a month against $2,459, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.0 times income in Dickinson and 3.0 times in League City.

Commute and location

Commutes average 29 minutes in Dickinson and 30 in League City.

The two are about 4 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

League City has less violent crime. Dickinson reported 141 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and League City reported 60 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 100% of Dickinson's 2 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 96% of League City's 23.
