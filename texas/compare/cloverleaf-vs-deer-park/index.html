Cost of living

Cloverleaf is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Cloverleaf: a median of $1,155 a month, 12% lower than Deer Park's $1,318.

The median home is worth $169,600 in Cloverleaf and $264,600 in Deer Park. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,131 a month against $1,778, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.1 times income in Cloverleaf and 2.6 times in Deer Park.

Commute and location

Commutes average 29 minutes in Cloverleaf and 24 in Deer Park.

The two are about 6 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

We don't have a full year of police data for both cities, so we can't compare crime fairly.

For schools, 100% of Cloverleaf's 6 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 67% of Deer Park's 9.
