Cost of living

Haltom City is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Fort Worth: a median of $1,509 a month, 7% higher than Haltom City's $1,415.

The median home is worth $303,000 in Fort Worth and $218,800 in Haltom City. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,091 a month against $1,452, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.8 times income in Fort Worth and 3.7 times in Haltom City.

Commute and location

Commutes average 27 minutes in Fort Worth and 25 in Haltom City.

The two are about 4 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Haltom City has less violent crime. Fort Worth reported 375 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Haltom City reported 359 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 42% of Fort Worth's 250 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 25% of Haltom City's 12.
