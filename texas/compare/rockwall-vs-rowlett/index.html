Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Median rent is about the same: $1,947 a month in Rockwall and $1,990 in Rowlett.

The median home is worth $411,700 in Rockwall and $368,800 in Rowlett. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,756 a month against $2,508, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.6 times income in Rockwall and 3.3 times in Rowlett.

Commute and location

Commutes average 32 minutes in Rockwall and 31 in Rowlett.

The two are about 6 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Rockwall has less violent crime. Rockwall reported 111 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Rowlett reported 157 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 77% of Rockwall's 13 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 21% of Rowlett's 14.
