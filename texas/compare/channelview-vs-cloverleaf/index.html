Cost of living

Cloverleaf is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Channelview: a median of $1,279 a month, 11% higher than Cloverleaf's $1,155.

The median home is worth $195,900 in Channelview and $169,600 in Cloverleaf. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,365 a month against $1,131, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.0 times income in Channelview and 3.1 times in Cloverleaf.

Commute and location

Commutes average 30 minutes in Channelview and 29 in Cloverleaf.

The two are about 3 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

We don't have a full year of police data for both cities, so we can't compare crime fairly.

For schools, 70% of Channelview's 10 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 100% of Cloverleaf's 6.

Common questions
