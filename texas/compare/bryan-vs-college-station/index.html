Cost of living

Bryan is the cheaper of the two.

Median rent is about the same: $1,195 a month in Bryan and $1,194 in College Station.

The median home is worth $227,800 in Bryan and $346,700 in College Station. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $1,537 a month against $2,375, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.8 times income in Bryan and 6.8 times in College Station.

Commute and location

Commutes average 19 minutes in Bryan and 17 in College Station.

The two are about 6 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

We don't have a full year of police data for both cities, so we can't compare crime fairly.

For schools, 56% of Bryan's 25 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 77% of College Station's 22.
