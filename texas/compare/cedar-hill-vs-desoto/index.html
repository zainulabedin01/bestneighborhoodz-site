Cost of living

DeSoto is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Cedar Hill: a median of $1,993 a month, 20% higher than DeSoto's $1,666.

The median home is worth $302,700 in Cedar Hill and $319,400 in DeSoto. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,102 a month against $2,191, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.0 times income in Cedar Hill and 3.9 times in DeSoto.

Commute and location

Commutes average 31 minutes in Cedar Hill and 31 in DeSoto.

The two are about 6 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Cedar Hill has less violent crime. Cedar Hill reported 155 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and DeSoto reported 283 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 31% of Cedar Hill's 13 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 55% of DeSoto's 11.
