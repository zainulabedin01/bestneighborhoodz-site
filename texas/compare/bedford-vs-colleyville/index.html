Cost of living

Bedford is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Bedford: a median of $1,502 a month, 54% lower than Colleyville's $3,263.

The median home is worth $350,700 in Bedford and $784,900 in Colleyville. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,369 a month against $5,130, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.2 times income in Bedford and 3.6 times in Colleyville.

Commute and location

Commutes average 24 minutes in Bedford and 24 in Colleyville.

The two are about 3 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Colleyville has less violent crime. Bedford reported 180 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Colleyville reported 35 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 88% of Bedford's 8 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 100% of Colleyville's 8.
