Cost of living

Round Rock is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Leander: a median of $1,939 a month, 10% higher than Round Rock's $1,763.

The median home is worth $506,200 in Leander and $418,600 in Round Rock. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $3,528 a month against $2,827, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.6 times income in Leander and 4.2 times in Round Rock.

Commute and location

Commutes average 29 minutes in Leander and 25 in Round Rock.

The two are about 10 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Leander has less violent crime. Leander reported 58 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Round Rock reported 125 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 75% of Leander's 20 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 68% of Round Rock's 28.
