Cost of living

Lancaster is the cheaper of the two.

Median rent is about the same: $1,666 a month in DeSoto and $1,704 in Lancaster.

The median home is worth $319,400 in DeSoto and $246,300 in Lancaster. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,191 a month against $1,671, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.9 times income in DeSoto and 3.6 times in Lancaster.

Commute and location

Commutes average 31 minutes in DeSoto and 29 in Lancaster.

The two are about 6 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

DeSoto has less violent crime. DeSoto reported 283 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Lancaster reported 345 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 55% of DeSoto's 11 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 42% of Lancaster's 12.
