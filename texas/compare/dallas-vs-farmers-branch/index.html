Cost of living

Dallas is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Dallas: a median of $1,472 a month, 21% lower than Farmers Branch's $1,864.

The median home is worth $320,700 in Dallas and $364,600 in Farmers Branch. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,178 a month against $2,411, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.5 times income in Dallas and 4.0 times in Farmers Branch.

Commute and location

Commutes average 26 minutes in Dallas and 22 in Farmers Branch.

The two are about 9 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Farmers Branch has less violent crime. Dallas reported 577 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Farmers Branch reported 133 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 63% of Dallas's 322 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 86% of Farmers Branch's 7.
