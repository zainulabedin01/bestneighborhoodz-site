Cost of living

Lewisville is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Lewisville: a median of $1,670 a month, 15% lower than The Colony's $1,958.

The median home is worth $378,500 in Lewisville and $383,900 in The Colony. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,555 a month against $2,647, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.2 times income in Lewisville and 3.4 times in The Colony.

Commute and location

Commutes average 25 minutes in Lewisville and 27 in The Colony.

The two are about 5 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

The Colony has less violent crime. Lewisville reported 218 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and The Colony reported 136 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 35% of Lewisville's 26 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 30% of The Colony's 10.
