Cost of living

Mesquite is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is higher in Forney: a median of $1,828 a month, 21% higher than Mesquite's $1,513.

The median home is worth $346,400 in Forney and $245,700 in Mesquite. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,421 a month against $1,666, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 3.3 times income in Forney and 3.4 times in Mesquite.

Commute and location

Commutes average 36 minutes in Forney and 32 in Mesquite.

Safety and schools

Forney has less violent crime. Forney reported 136 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Mesquite reported 453 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 50% of Forney's 16 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 49% of Mesquite's 49.
