Cost of living

Overall costs are close between the two.

Rent is lower in Hurst: a median of $1,358 a month, 31% lower than Watauga's $1,982.

The median home is worth $315,200 in Hurst and $250,500 in Watauga. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $2,058 a month against $1,727, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 4.4 times income in Hurst and 2.7 times in Watauga.

Commute and location

Commutes average 25 minutes in Hurst and 29 in Watauga.

The two are about 5 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

Watauga has less violent crime. Hurst reported 173 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, and Watauga reported 109 in 2025. The Texas rate is 387.

For schools, 100% of Hurst's 9 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 80% of Watauga's 5.
