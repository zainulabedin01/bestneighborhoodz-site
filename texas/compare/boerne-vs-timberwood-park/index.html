Cost of living

Boerne is the cheaper of the two.

Rent is lower in Boerne: a median of $1,589 a month, 20% lower than Timberwood Park's $1,998.

The median home is worth $457,900 in Boerne and $510,600 in Timberwood Park. With 20% down at today's rates, that is about $3,026 a month against $3,476, property tax included.

Compared with what locals earn, homes cost 5.4 times income in Boerne and 3.3 times in Timberwood Park.

Commute and location

Commutes average 25 minutes in Boerne and 31 in Timberwood Park.

The two are about 16 miles apart, so many people weigh them for the same job.

Safety and schools

We don't have a full year of police data for both cities, so we can't compare crime fairly.

For schools, 100% of Boerne's 8 rated campuses earn an A or B, against 100% of Timberwood Park's 6.
