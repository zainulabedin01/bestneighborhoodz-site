How much does it cost to live in Poteet?

Renters in Poteet pay a median of $734 a month, 48% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $119,200, 58% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $886, including property tax.
