What it's like to live in Marble Falls

Marble Falls is a town in Burnet County. 9,633 people lived here in 2025, up 36.3% from 2020, so it is one of the faster-growing places in Texas. It is small-town and rural in feel, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2001).

Places locals use include Westside Park, Lakeside Park and Johnson Park.

The median age is 35.5, 40% of households have children, 41% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $57,925 a year (26% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Marble Falls

  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 15 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Growing fast. The population grew 36% from 2020 to 2025, which usually means new homes, shops and jobs.

Cons of living in Marble Falls

  • Homes are pricey for local incomes. The median home costs 5.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high here.

Cost of living in Marble Falls

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Marble Falls is $1,360 a month, about the same as the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $54,000 a year. Right now 51% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,074 for a one-bedroom, $1,349 for a two-bedroom and $1,766 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $338,800, 19% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,854 before tax, or $2,273 with property tax. That is $913 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Marble Falls pays $5,020 a year, 19% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
