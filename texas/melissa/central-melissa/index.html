What it's like to live in Central Melissa

Central Melissa is an area in Melissa with about 4,000 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2014), mostly owner-occupied (84%) and 44% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 1st of 3 areas in Melissa, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in Central Melissa

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about under 0.1 miles away.

Cons of living in Central Melissa

  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 2.5 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Central Melissa

Is Central Melissa a good place to live?

Central Melissa covers part of Melissa, TX, home to about 4,000 people. It gets an A, in the top 12% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 1st of 3 areas we score in Melissa. It does best on schools and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Central Melissa?

Median rent in Central Melissa is $2,580 a month, about the same as the Melissa median. The median home is worth $382,000, 16% lower than the Melissa median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 3.3x across Melissa.

What schools serve Central Melissa?

The closest rated public schools are Harry McKillop Elementary (elementary, rated A), Melissa Middle (middle, rated A) and Melissa High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
