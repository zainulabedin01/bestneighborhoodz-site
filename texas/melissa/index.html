    Living in Melissa: common questions

    Is Melissa, TX a good place to live?

    We give Melissa an A. It ranks 22nd of 192 Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people. It does best on home ownership and internet access, and trails on getting around and cost of living. It is a strong pick for families, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Melissa?

    Median rent in Melissa is $2,532 a month, 80% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $454,000, 60% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,057, including property tax.

    Is Melissa safe?

    In 2025, Melissa Police Department reported 49 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 87% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 433 per 100,000, 79% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Melissa?

    We found 6 rated public school campuses inside Melissa. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 92 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 10,000 to 50,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Melissa?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Melissa are Central Melissa, The Quarry and The Residences at Harrison Park. We score 3 areas in the city.

    Does Melissa flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Melissa as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires and winter storms. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Melissa?

    Summers in Melissa peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 97 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 38 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Melissa?

    The average one-way commute is 34 min, 26% higher than the Texas average. About 29% of workers work from home. It is about 36 miles from downtown Dallas.
