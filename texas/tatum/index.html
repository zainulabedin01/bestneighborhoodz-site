What it's like to live in Tatum

Tatum sits about 20 miles from downtown Longview, in the Longview metro. 1,403 people lived here in 2025, up 4.2% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Local spots people know include Tatum Eagle Park, Eagles Nest Park and Alligator Run Offroad Park.

The median age is 31.5, 51% of households have children, 67% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $77,404 a year (about the same as the Texas median).

Pros of living in Tatum

  • Strong public schools. 67% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 71 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 82% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $918, 35% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 2.5 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.

Cons of living in Tatum

  • Long commutes. The average trip to work takes 32 minutes, against 27 across Texas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of wildfires and strong wind as very high here.

Cost of living in Tatum

Tatum renters pay a median of $918 a month, 35% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $37,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 46% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $921 for a one-bedroom, $1,205 for a two-bedroom and $1,448 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $195,200, 31% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,068 before tax, or $1,256 with property tax. That is $338 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Tatum pays $2,245 a year, 47% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Tatum

Everyday places mapped inside Tatum, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Tatum Eagle Park, Eagles Nest Park, Alligator Run Offroad Park.
