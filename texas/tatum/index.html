Is Tatum, TX a good place to live?

Tatum gets a D- overall, which puts it in the bottom 16% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 10262nd of 11,459 nationally and 601st of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Tatum?

Renters in Tatum pay a median of $918 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $195,200, 31% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,256, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Tatum?

The average one-way commute is 32 min, 21% higher than the Texas average. About 15% of workers work from home.
