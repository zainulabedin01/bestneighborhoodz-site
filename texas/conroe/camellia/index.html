What it's like to live in Camellia

Camellia is a neighborhood in Conroe with about 4,400 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2014), mostly owner-occupied (94%) and 22% of households with children.

It grades B against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 3rd of 13 areas in Conroe, which grades C- overall.

Pros of living in Camellia

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $113,400, 49% higher than the Conroe median.

Cons of living in Camellia

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,820, 31% higher than the Conroe median.
  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 2.1 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
