    Living in Conroe: common questions

    Is Conroe, TX a good place to live?

    Conroe gets an overall grade of C- and ranks 50th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on internet access and jobs and income, and trails on errands and things to do and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Conroe?

    Median rent in Conroe is $1,393 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $309,600, 9% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,134, including property tax.

    Is Conroe safe?

    In 2025, Conroe Police Department reported 263 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 32% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,461 per 100,000, 30% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Conroe?

    We found 21 rated public school campuses inside Conroe. 67% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 51 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Conroe?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Conroe are Windsor Lakes, Southwest Conroe and Camellia. We score 13 areas in the city.

    Does Conroe flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Conroe as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: hurricanes and tornadoes. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Conroe?

    Summers in Conroe peak at about 95°F in July, and January nights fall to about 42°F. Expect around 105 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 51 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Conroe?

    The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home. It is about 38 miles from downtown Houston.
