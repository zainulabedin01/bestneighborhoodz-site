Is Conroe a good place to live? Common questions

Is Conroe, TX a good place to live?

Conroe gets a C overall, which puts it around the middle of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 493rd of 876 nationally and 38th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and jobs and income, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Conroe?

Median rent in Conroe is $1,393 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $309,600, 9% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.1x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,134, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Conroe?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Conroe are Southwest Conroe, Camellia and Windsor Lakes. We score 13 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Conroe?

The average one-way commute is 29 min, 7% higher than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
