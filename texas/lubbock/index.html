Is Lubbock a good place to live? Common questions

Is Lubbock, TX a good place to live?

We give Lubbock a D+. That places it in the bottom 32% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 603rd of 876 nationally and 43rd of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are getting around and cost of living, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Lubbock?

Median rent in Lubbock is $1,182 a month, 16% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $212,400, 25% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,440, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Lubbock?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Lubbock are Hatton Place, Frankford Farms and Quaker Heights. We score 21 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Lubbock?

The average one-way commute is 16 min, 39% lower than the Texas average. About 7.1% of workers work from home.
