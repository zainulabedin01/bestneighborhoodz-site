What it's like to live in Lubbock

Lubbock is the anchor city of the Lubbock metro area. 273,071 people lived here in 2025, up 6.2% from 2020.

Places locals use include Mackenzie Park Lake, McAlister Park and Clapp Park.

The median age is 30.5, 28% of households have children, 51% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $60,895 a year (22% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Lubbock

  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,182, 16% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 16 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Lubbock

  • Higher violent crime. 699 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 81% higher than the Texas rate.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and winter storms as very high here.

Cost of living in Lubbock

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lubbock is $1,182 a month, 16% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $47,000 a year. Right now 56% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,050 for a one-bedroom, $1,241 for a two-bedroom and $1,718 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $212,400, 25% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,163 before tax, or $1,440 with property tax. That is $258 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Lubbock pays $3,333 a year, 21% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Lubbock safe?

In 2025, Lubbock Police Department reported 1,928 violent crimes and 6,862 property crimes. That works out to 699 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 81% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 2,489 property crimes per 100,000, 19% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 34% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.

Schools in Lubbock

Lubbock has 64 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 50% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Lubbock ISD, Frenship ISD and Lubbock-Cooper ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Talkington School for Young Women Leaders, Crestview Elementary and Roberts Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 80 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Living in Lubbock: common questions

    Is Lubbock, TX a good place to live?

    We give Lubbock a C-. It ranks 51st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on getting around and errands and things to do, and trails on safety and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Lubbock?

    Median rent in Lubbock is $1,182 a month, 16% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $212,400, 25% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,440, including property tax.

    Is Lubbock safe?

    In 2025, Lubbock Police Department reported 699 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 81% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 2,489 per 100,000, 19% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Lubbock?

    We found 64 rated public school campuses inside Lubbock. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 42 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Lubbock?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Lubbock are Hatton Place, South Lubbock and Quaker Heights. We score 21 areas in the city.

    Does Lubbock flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Lubbock as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, hail, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Lubbock?

    Summers in Lubbock peak at about 93°F in July, and January nights fall to about 30°F. Expect around 91 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 19 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Lubbock?

    The average one-way commute is 16 min, 39% lower than the Texas average. About 7.1% of workers work from home.
