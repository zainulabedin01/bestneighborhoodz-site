    Living in Hitchcock: common questions

    Is Hitchcock, TX a good place to live?

    We give Hitchcock an F. It ranks 720th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on home ownership and jobs and income, and trails on schools and getting around.

    How much does it cost to live in Hitchcock?

    Median rent in Hitchcock is $1,380 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $188,300, 34% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,231, including property tax.

    Is Hitchcock safe?

    In 2025, Hitchcock Police Department reported 331 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 15% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,297 per 100,000, 38% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Hitchcock?

    We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Hitchcock. 20% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 13 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Hitchcock?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Hitchcock are East Hitchcock, North Hitchcock and Northeast Hitchcock. We score 4 areas in the city.

    Does Hitchcock flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Hitchcock as high, and coastal flooding as very high. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, coastal flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Hitchcock?

    Summers in Hitchcock peak at about 91°F in July, and January nights fall to about 49°F. Expect around 66 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 47 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Hitchcock?

    The average one-way commute is 29 min, 9% higher than the Texas average. About 3.9% of workers work from home. It is about 40 miles from downtown Houston.
