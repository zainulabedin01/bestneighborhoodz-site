What it's like to live in Hubbard

Hubbard is a town in Hill County. 1,507 people lived here in 2025, up 8.5% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1963).

The median age is 41.5, 27% of households have children, 77% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $50,859 a year (35% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Hubbard

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Hubbard is $868 a month, 38% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $35,000 a year. Right now 81% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $858 for a one-bedroom, $1,080 for a two-bedroom and $1,495 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $134,900, 52% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $738 before tax, or $911 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Hubbard pays $2,073 a year, 51% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
