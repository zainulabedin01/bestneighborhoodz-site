Cost of living in Lacy-Lakeview

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Lacy-Lakeview is $917 a month, 35% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $37,000 a year. Right now 39% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,029 for a one-bedroom, $1,297 for a two-bedroom and $1,693 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $160,600, 43% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $879 before tax, or $1,061 with property tax. That is $144 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Lacy-Lakeview pays $2,187 a year, 48% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
