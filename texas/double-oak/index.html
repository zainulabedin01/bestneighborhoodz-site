Is Double Oak, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Double Oak?

Renters in Double Oak pay a median of $1,383 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $736,600, 160% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $4,832, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Double Oak?
