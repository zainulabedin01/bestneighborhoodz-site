Mexia is a town in Limestone County. 7,091 people lived here in 2025, up 3.4% from 2020. It is spread out.

Cost of living in Mexia

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Mexia is $887 a month, 37% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $35,000 a year. Right now 60% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $854 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,375 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $109,900, 61% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $602 before tax, or $747 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Mexia pays $1,750 a year, 59% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
