What it's like to live in Clute

Clute sits about 52 miles from downtown Houston, in the Houston-Pasadena-The Woodlands metro. 10,821 people lived here in 2025, up 1.8% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Clute City Park, Calvit-Eagle Lake Park and Woodshore Park.

The median age is 30.0, 33% of households have children, 49% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $70,208 a year (11% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Clute

  • Low violent crime. 210 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 46% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,130, 19% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 21 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Clute

  • Higher unemployment. 8.6% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes as very high here.

Cost of living in Clute

Clute renters pay a median of $1,130 a month, 19% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $45,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 54% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,212 for a one-bedroom, $1,319 for a two-bedroom and $1,790 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $271,700, 4% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,487 before tax, or $1,906 with property tax. That is $776 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Clute pays $5,024 a year, 19% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
