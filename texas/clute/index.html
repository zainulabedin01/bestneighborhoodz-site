Is Clute, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Clute?

Renters in Clute pay a median of $1,130 a month, 19% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $271,700, 4% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,906, including property tax.

How long is the commute in Clute?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 20% lower than the Texas average. About 2.9% of workers work from home.
