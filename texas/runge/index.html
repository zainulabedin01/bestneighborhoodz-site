What it's like to live in Runge

Runge is a town in Karnes County. 952 people lived here in 2025, up 6.5% from 2020. It is spread out.

The median age is 20.6, 63% of households have children and 38% of homes are owned by the people living in them.

Cost of living in Runge

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $872 for a one-bedroom, $1,143 for a two-bedroom and $1,393 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $88,500, 69% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $484 before tax, or $597 with property tax.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Runge pays $1,350 a year, 68% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
