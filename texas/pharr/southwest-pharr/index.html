What it's like to live in Southwest Pharr

Southwest Pharr is an area in Pharr with about 12,600 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2005), mostly owner-occupied (69%) and 59% of households with children.

It grades B- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 3rd of 7 areas in Pharr, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in Southwest Pharr

  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.6 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in Southwest Pharr

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southwest Pharr

Is Southwest Pharr a good place to live?

Southwest Pharr covers part of Pharr, TX, home to about 12,600 people. It gets a B-, in the top 44% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 3rd of 7 areas we score in Pharr. It does best on cost of living and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Southwest Pharr?

Median rent in Southwest Pharr is $1,000 a month, 4% lower than the Pharr median. The median home is worth $125,000, about the same as the Pharr median. A median home costs 2.4x the median household income, compared with 2.3x across Pharr.

The closest rated public schools are Carmen Anaya Elementary (elementary, rated B), Jaime Escalante Middle (middle, rated B) and Valley View High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
