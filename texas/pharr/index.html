Is Pharr, TX a good place to live?

We give Pharr a D-. That places it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 779th of 876 nationally and 70th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Pharr?

Renters in Pharr pay a median of $1,037 a month, 26% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $121,900, 57% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $879, including property tax.
