What it's like to live in Northeast Pharr

Northeast Pharr is an area in Pharr with about 6,700 residents, homes built around 1978, a mix of owners and renters and 41% of households with children.

It grades B against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 2nd of 7 areas in Pharr, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in Northeast Pharr

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $69,800, 32% higher than the Pharr median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Northeast Pharr

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,270, 22% higher than the Pharr median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Northeast Pharr

Is Northeast Pharr a good place to live?
