Questions about Central Pharr

Is Central Pharr a good place to live?

Central Pharr covers part of Pharr, TX, home to about 17,100 people. It gets a B-, in the top 44% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 4th of 7 areas we score in Pharr. It does best on cost of living and worst on health.

How much does it cost to live in Central Pharr?

Renters in Central Pharr pay a median of $1,080 a month, 4% higher than the Pharr median. The median home is worth $121,000, about the same as the Pharr median. A median home costs 2.1x the median household income, compared with 2.3x across Pharr.
