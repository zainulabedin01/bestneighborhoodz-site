What it's like to live in Meridian

Meridian sits about 37 miles from downtown Waco, in the Waco metro. 1,403 people lived here in 2025, up 0.8% from 2020. It is spread out.

Local spots people know include Meridian Park, Meridian State Park and Meridian Public Library.

The median age is 42.0, 27% of households have children, 51% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $65,625 a year (16% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Meridian

  • Low violent crime. 140 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 64% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $738, 47% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.8 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Meridian

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of wildfires as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 115 days a year at 90°F or hotter.
  • Spotty home internet. Only 77% of homes have a broadband plan.

Cost of living in Meridian

Meridian renters pay a median of $738 a month, 47% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $30,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 43% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $804 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,347 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $116,700, 59% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $639 before tax, or $819 with property tax. That is $81 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Meridian pays $2,167 a year, 49% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
