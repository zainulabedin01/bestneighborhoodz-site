What it's like to live in Robinson

Robinson sits about 8 miles from downtown Waco, in the Waco metro. 13,028 people lived here in 2025, up 4.8% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

Places locals use include Youngblood Park, Robinson City Park and Peplow Park.

The median age is 35.1, 42% of households have children, 79% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $99,289 a year (27% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Robinson

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 5 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 92 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 76% lower than the Texas rate.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $99,289, 27% higher than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 19 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Robinson

  • Errands mean a drive. Compared with similar Texas places, fewer homes here are within a mile of a grocery store, pharmacy, park or clinic.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and wildfires as very high here.

Cost of living in Robinson

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Robinson is $1,419 a month, about the same as the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $57,000 a year. Right now 48% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,029 for a one-bedroom, $1,297 for a two-bedroom and $1,693 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $302,200, 6% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,654 before tax, or $2,043 with property tax. That is $624 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Robinson pays $4,666 a year, 10% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Robinson safe?

In 2025, Robinson Police Department reported 12 violent crimes and 100 property crimes. That works out to 92 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 76% lower than the Texas rate of 387, and 762 property crimes per 100,000, 63% lower than the state rate.

Schools in Robinson

Robinson has 5 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 100% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Robinson ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Robinson High School and Robinson Intermediate. The average rating score across all campuses is 87 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
