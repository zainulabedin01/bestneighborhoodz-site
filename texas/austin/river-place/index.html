What it's like to live in River Place

River Place is a neighborhood in Austin with about 5,700 residents, homes built around 1996, mostly owner-occupied (77%) and 33% of households with children.

It grades A against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 17th of 206 areas in Austin, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in River Place

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $196,800, 110% higher than the Austin median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 2 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in River Place

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about River Place

Is River Place a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in River Place?

Median rent in River Place is $1,810 a month, 5% higher than the Austin median. The median home is worth $988,000, 78% higher than the Austin median. A median home costs 5.0x the median household income, compared with 5.9x across Austin.
