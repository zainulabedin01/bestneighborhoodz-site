What it's like to live in Lost Creek

Lost Creek is a neighborhood in Austin with about 3,000 residents, homes built around 1982, mostly owner-occupied (91%) and 49% of households with children.

Our grade for Lost Creek is A+. That puts it 13th of 206 areas we grade in Austin, a city that gets a B- overall.

Pros of living in Lost Creek

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $216,700, 131% higher than the Austin median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in Lost Creek

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,890, 67% higher than the Austin median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Lost Creek

Is Lost Creek a good place to live?

Lost Creek is a neighborhood in Austin, TX, home to about 3,000 people. It gets an A+, in the top 5% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 13th of 206 areas we score in Austin. It does best on health and worst on cost of living.

How much does it cost to live in Lost Creek?

Renters in Lost Creek pay a median of $2,890 a month, 67% higher than the Austin median. The median home is worth $1,192,000, 115% higher than the Austin median. A median home costs 5.5x the median household income, compared with 5.9x across Austin.

What schools serve Lost Creek?

The closest rated public schools are Valley View Elementary (elementary, rated B), Hill Country Middle (middle, rated A) and Westlake High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
