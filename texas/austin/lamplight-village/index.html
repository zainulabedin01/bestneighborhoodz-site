Questions about Lamplight Village

Is Lamplight Village a good place to live?

Lamplight Village is a neighborhood in Austin, TX, home to about 7,200 people. It gets a C+, around the middle of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 117th of 206 areas we score in Austin. It does best on internet access and worst on schools.

How much does it cost to live in Lamplight Village?

Renters in Lamplight Village pay a median of $1,860 a month, 8% higher than the Austin median. The median home is worth $476,000, 14% lower than the Austin median. A median home costs 5.0x the median household income, compared with 5.9x across Austin.
