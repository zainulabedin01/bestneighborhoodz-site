What it's like to live in Foster Heights

Foster Heights is a neighborhood in Austin with about 1,200 residents, homes built around 1973, a mix of owners and renters and 41% of households with children.

It grades C against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 141st of 206 areas in Austin, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in Foster Heights

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.5 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Foster Heights

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,140, 24% higher than the Austin median.
  • Higher unemployment. 8.6% of the labor force is out of work, against 4.7% across Austin.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Median rent in Foster Heights is $2,140 a month, 24% higher than the Austin median. A household would need about $86,000 a year to keep that rent at 30% of income.

The median home is worth $621,000, 12% higher than the Austin median, which means a monthly payment of about $4,128 with property tax at today's mortgage rates.
