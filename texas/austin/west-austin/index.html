What it's like to live in West Austin

West Austin is an area in Austin with about 20,600 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2001), mostly owner-occupied (67%) and 34% of households with children.

It grades A- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 43rd of 206 areas in Austin, which grades B- overall.

Pros of living in West Austin

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $186,400, 99% higher than the Austin median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in West Austin

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,140, 24% higher than the Austin median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes, hail and wildfires as very high in this part of town.

Cost of living in West Austin

Median rent in West Austin is $2,140 a month, 24% higher than the Austin median. A household would need about $86,000 a year to keep that rent at 30% of income.

The median home is worth $1,215,000, 119% higher than the Austin median, which means a monthly payment of about $7,450 with property tax at today's mortgage rates.

Questions about West Austin

Is West Austin a good place to live?

West Austin covers part of Austin, TX, home to about 20,600 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 43rd of 206 areas we score in Austin. It does best on schools and worst on errands and things to do.

How much does it cost to live in West Austin?

Median rent in West Austin is $2,140 a month, 24% higher than the Austin median. The median home is worth $1,215,000, 119% higher than the Austin median. A median home costs 6.5x the median household income, compared with 5.9x across Austin.

What schools serve West Austin?

The closest rated public schools are Bridge Point Elementary (elementary, rated A), West Ridge Middle (middle, rated A) and Westlake High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
