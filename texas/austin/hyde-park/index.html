What it's like to live in Hyde Park

Hyde Park is a neighborhood in Austin with about 3,000 residents, older homes (median year built 1964), mostly rented (72% of homes) and 13% of households with children.

Our grade for Hyde Park is A-. That puts it 45th of 206 areas we grade in Austin, a city that gets a B- overall.

Pros of living in Hyde Park

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,330, 23% lower than the Austin median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 2 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.2 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Hyde Park

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of tornadoes and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Hyde Park

Is Hyde Park a good place to live?

Hyde Park is a neighborhood in Austin, TX, home to about 3,000 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 45th of 206 areas we score in Austin. It does best on getting around and worst on home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Hyde Park?

Renters in Hyde Park pay a median of $1,330 a month, 23% lower than the Austin median. The median home is worth $834,000, 50% higher than the Austin median. A median home costs 13.0x the median household income, compared with 5.9x across Austin.

The closest rated public schools are Lee Elementary (elementary, rated A), General Marshall Middle (middle, rated D) and McCallum High School (high, rated A). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
