What it's like to live in Montopolis

Montopolis is a neighborhood in Austin with about 7,400 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2010), mostly rented (71% of homes) and 29% of households with children.

It grades D against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 175th of 206 areas in Austin, which grades B- overall.

Questions about Montopolis

Is Montopolis a good place to live?
