Is Austin a good place to live? Common questions

Is Austin, TX a good place to live?

We give Austin a B. That places it in the top 36% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 294th of 876 nationally and 25th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are education levels and getting around, and its weakest is home ownership.

How much does it cost to live in Austin?

Median rent in Austin is $1,729 a month, 23% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $555,300, 96% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 5.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,708, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Austin?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Austin are Appaloosa Run, Tarrytown and Blackland. We score 206 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Austin?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 11% lower than the Texas average. About 31% of workers work from home.
