    Living in Austin: common questions

    Is Austin, TX a good place to live?

    We give Austin a B-. It ranks 30th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on errands and things to do and education levels, and trails on safety and schools. It is a strong pick for young professionals.

    How much does it cost to live in Austin?

    Median rent in Austin is $1,729 a month, 23% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $555,300, 96% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 5.9x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,708, including property tax.

    Is Austin safe?

    In 2025, Austin Police Department reported 422 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 9% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 3,099 per 100,000, 49% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Austin?

    We found 193 rated public school campuses inside Austin. 47% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 23 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Austin?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Austin are Tarrytown, Glen Ridge and Appaloosa Run. We score 206 areas in the city.

    Does Austin flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Austin as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Austin?

    Summers in Austin peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 42°F. Expect around 123 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 36 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Austin?

    The average one-way commute is 24 min, 11% lower than the Texas average. About 31% of workers work from home.
