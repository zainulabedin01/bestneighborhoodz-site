Living in Hamlin: common questions

Is Hamlin, TX a good place to live?

We give Hamlin a B-. It ranks 310th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on cost of living and errands and things to do, and trails on internet access and health. It is a strong pick for people on a budget.

How much does it cost to live in Hamlin?

Renters in Hamlin pay a median of $858 a month, 39% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $73,200, 74% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.2x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $540, including property tax.

Is Hamlin safe?

In 2025, Hamlin Police Department reported 265 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 32% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 848 per 100,000, 59% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Hamlin?

We found 2 rated public school campuses inside Hamlin. 50% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 33 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Hamlin flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Hamlin as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Hamlin?

Summers in Hamlin peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 29°F. Expect around 111 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 25 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Hamlin?

The average one-way commute is 30 min, 11% higher than the Texas average. About 6.7% of workers work from home. It is about 38 miles from downtown Abilene.
