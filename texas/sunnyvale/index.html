Living in Sunnyvale: common questions

Is Sunnyvale, TX a good place to live?

Sunnyvale earns a grade of B+ on our index, 150th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on internet access and education levels, and trails on getting around and cost of living. It is a strong pick for remote workers.

How much does it cost to live in Sunnyvale?

Renters in Sunnyvale pay a median of $1,422 a month, about the same as the Texas median. The median home is worth $565,300, 99% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,838, including property tax.

Is Sunnyvale safe?

In 2025, Sunnyvale Police Department reported 209 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 46% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 747 per 100,000, 64% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Sunnyvale?

We found 5 rated public school campuses inside Sunnyvale. 80% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 83 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Sunnyvale flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Sunnyvale as high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, tornadoes, heat waves, hail and wildfires. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Sunnyvale?

Summers in Sunnyvale peak at about 92°F in July, and January nights fall to about 34°F. Expect around 95 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 40 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Sunnyvale?

The average one-way commute is 33 min, 23% higher than the Texas average. About 14% of workers work from home. It is about 12 miles from downtown Dallas.
