What it's like to live in Floydada

Floydada is a town in Floyd County. 2,420 people lived here in 2025, down 9.5% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1965).

Places locals use include Floydada City Park, Annie Taylor Park and Floydada Park.

The median age is 32.7, 43% of households have children, 69% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $52,826 a year (33% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Floydada

  • Strong public schools. 100% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $664, 53% lower than the Texas median.
  • Homes are affordable for local incomes. The median home costs 1.7 times the median household income, against 3.6 times across Texas.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 18 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Floydada

  • Higher poverty. 20% of people live below the poverty line, against 14% across Texas.
  • Many adults uninsured. 29% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.

Cost of living in Floydada

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Floydada is $664 a month, 53% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $27,000 a year. Right now 37% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $807 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,208 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $89,700, 68% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $491 before tax, or $581 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Floydada pays $1,084 a year, 74% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Floydada

Everyday places mapped inside Floydada, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Floydada City Park, Annie Taylor Park, Floydada Park.

The average one-way commute is 18 minutes, 33% shorter than the Texas average. 1% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown Plainview is about 26 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Floydada work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
