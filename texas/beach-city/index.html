    Living in Beach City: common questions

    Is Beach City, TX a good place to live?

    Beach City gets an overall grade of C and ranks 430th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on jobs and income and home ownership, and trails on getting around and cost of living.

    How much does it cost to live in Beach City?

    Renters in Beach City pay a median of $1,669 a month, 19% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $496,900, 75% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.3x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $3,016, including property tax.

    What is the weather like in Beach City?

    Summers in Beach City peak at about 91°F in July, and January nights fall to about 43°F. Expect around 68 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 60 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Beach City?

    The average one-way commute is 32 min, 19% higher than the Texas average. About 3.4% of workers work from home. It is about 32 miles from downtown Houston.
