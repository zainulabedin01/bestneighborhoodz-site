Is Spring a good place to live? Common questions

Is Spring, TX a good place to live?

Spring earns a B- on our index, in the top 44% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 346th of 876 nationally and 30th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Spring?

Renters in Spring pay a median of $1,711 a month, 22% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $231,500, 18% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,603, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Spring?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Spring are Spring Lakes, The Park Villages and Eastgate Village. We score 11 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Spring?

The average one-way commute is 34 min, 27% higher than the Texas average. About 11% of workers work from home.
