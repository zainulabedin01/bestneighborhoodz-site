What it's like to live in Southeast Granbury

Southeast Granbury is an area in Granbury with about 2,000 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2009), a mix of owners and renters and 25% of households with children.

Our grade for Southeast Granbury is D+. That puts it 4th of 4 areas we grade in Granbury, a city that gets a C+ overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Granbury

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in Southeast Granbury

  • Higher unemployment. 12% of the labor force is out of work, against 2.7% across Granbury.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 42 minutes, against 31 for Granbury as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of river flooding, tornadoes and heat waves as very high in this part of town.
