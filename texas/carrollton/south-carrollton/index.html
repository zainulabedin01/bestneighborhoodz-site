What it's like to live in South Carrollton

South Carrollton is an area in Carrollton with about 1,800 residents, older homes (median year built 1965), a mix of owners and renters and 59% of households with children.

It grades C+ against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 11th of 12 areas in Carrollton, which grades A overall.

Pros of living in South Carrollton

  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.4 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.

Cons of living in South Carrollton

  • Higher poverty. 16% of residents live below the poverty line, against 7.1% across Carrollton.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about South Carrollton

Is South Carrollton a good place to live?

Median rent in South Carrollton is $1,580 a month, 9% lower than the Carrollton median. The median home is worth $225,000, 41% lower than the Carrollton median. A median home costs 3.1x the median household income, compared with 3.8x across Carrollton.
