Is Carrollton a good place to live? Common questions

Is Carrollton, TX a good place to live?

Carrollton gets a B+ overall, which puts it in the top 28% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 188th of 876 nationally and 16th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are jobs and income and internet access, and its weakest is getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Carrollton?

Median rent in Carrollton is $1,735 a month, 24% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $381,000, 34% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,549, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Carrollton?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Carrollton are West Carrollton, Southwest Carrollton and Northeast Carrollton. We score 12 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Carrollton?

The average one-way commute is 24 min, 10% lower than the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home.
