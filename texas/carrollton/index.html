    Living in Carrollton: common questions

    Is Carrollton, TX a good place to live?

    Carrollton gets an overall grade of A and ranks 10th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on errands and things to do and jobs and income, and trails on getting around and health. It is a strong pick for families, young professionals, retirees and remote workers.

    How much does it cost to live in Carrollton?

    Median rent in Carrollton is $1,735 a month, 24% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $381,000, 34% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,549, including property tax.

    Is Carrollton safe?

    In 2025, Carrollton Police Department reported 98 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 75% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,004 per 100,000, 52% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Carrollton?

    We found 32 rated public school campuses inside Carrollton. 81% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 69 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Carrollton?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Carrollton are West Carrollton, Northeast Carrollton and North Carrollton. We score 12 areas in the city.

    Does Carrollton flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Carrollton as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Carrollton?

    Summers in Carrollton peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 36°F. Expect around 93 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 42 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Carrollton?

    The average one-way commute is 24 min, 10% lower than the Texas average. About 22% of workers work from home. It is about 16 miles from downtown Dallas.
