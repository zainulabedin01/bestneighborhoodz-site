What it's like to live in Anton

Anton sits about 23 miles from downtown Lubbock, in the Lubbock metro. 919 people lived here in 2025, down 0.7% from 2020. It is spread out, with an older housing stock (the median home dates to 1964).

Cost of living in Anton

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Anton is $781 a month, 44% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $31,000 a year. Right now 12% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $868 for a one-bedroom, $1,014 for a two-bedroom and $1,404 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $63,500, 78% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $348 before tax, or $408 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Anton pays $721 a year, 83% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
