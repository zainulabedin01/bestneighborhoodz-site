Is Sonterra, TX a good place to live?

How much does it cost to live in Sonterra?

Median rent in Sonterra is $1,854 a month, 32% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $273,600, 4% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,964, including property tax.
