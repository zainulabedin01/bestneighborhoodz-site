Living in Weimar: common questions

Is Weimar, TX a good place to live?

We give Weimar an A-. It ranks 113th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It does best on schools and cost of living, and trails on internet access and education levels. It is a strong pick for families.

How much does it cost to live in Weimar?

Renters in Weimar pay a median of $483 a month, 66% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $165,300, 42% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,081, including property tax.

Is Weimar safe?

In 2025, Weimar Police Department reported 90 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 77% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 853 per 100,000, 59% lower than the Texas rate.

Are the schools good in Weimar?

We found 3 rated public school campuses inside Weimar. 100% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 89 of 100 compared with other Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people.

Does Weimar flood?

FEMA rates river flooding risk in Weimar as very high, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: river flooding, hurricanes, tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

What is the weather like in Weimar?

Summers in Weimar peak at about 96°F in July, and January nights fall to about 39°F. Expect around 132 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 45 inches of rain.

How long is the commute in Weimar?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 19% lower than the Texas average. About 1.5% of workers work from home.
