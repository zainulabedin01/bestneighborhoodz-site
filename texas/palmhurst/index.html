What it's like to live in Palmhurst

Palmhurst sits about 4 miles from downtown McAllen, in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metro. 2,562 people lived here in 2025, down 1.4% from 2020. It is small-town and rural in feel.

The median age is 35.2, 44% of households have children, 80% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $98,125 a year (25% higher than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Palmhurst

  • Low violent crime. 154 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 60% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $823, 41% lower than the Texas median.
  • High incomes. The median household earns $98,125, 25% higher than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 18 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Palmhurst

  • Many adults uninsured. 28% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hurricanes, heat waves and hail as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 183 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Palmhurst

Rent is the cost most newcomers feel first. The median rent in Palmhurst is $823 a month, 41% lower than the Texas median of $1,403. To keep that rent at 30% of income, a household needs to earn about $33,000 a year. Right now 40% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $806 for a one-bedroom, $1,017 for a two-bedroom and $1,305 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $375,000, 32% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $2,053 before tax, or $2,566 with property tax. That is $1,743 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Palmhurst pays $6,164 a year, 46% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Palmhurst

Everyday places mapped inside Palmhurst, from Overture Maps.

The average one-way commute is 18 minutes, 32% shorter than the Texas average. 11% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown McAllen is about 4 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Palmhurst work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
