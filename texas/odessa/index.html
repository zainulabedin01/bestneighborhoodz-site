Is Odessa a good place to live? Common questions

Is Odessa, TX a good place to live?

We give Odessa a D-. That places it in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 740th of 876 nationally and 64th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is education levels.

How much does it cost to live in Odessa?

Renters in Odessa pay a median of $1,315 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $223,100, 21% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,479, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Odessa?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Odessa are Country Club Estates, East Pointe and Emerald Forest Gardens. We score 66 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Odessa?

The average one-way commute is 22 min, 16% lower than the Texas average. About 3.4% of workers work from home.
