    Living in Odessa: common questions

    Is Odessa, TX a good place to live?

    We give Odessa a D+. It ranks 60th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on cost of living and safety, and trails on education levels and health.

    How much does it cost to live in Odessa?

    Renters in Odessa pay a median of $1,315 a month, 6% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $223,100, 21% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,479, including property tax.

    Is Odessa safe?

    In 2025, Odessa Police Department reported 286 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 26% lower than the Texas rate. Property crime was 1,159 per 100,000, 44% lower than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Odessa?

    We found 41 rated public school campuses inside Odessa. 42% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 27 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Odessa?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Odessa are Country Club Estates, East Pointe and Idlewild. We score 66 areas in the city.

    Does Odessa flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Odessa as moderate, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves, hail, wildfires, winter storms and strong wind. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Odessa?

    Summers in Odessa peak at about 94°F in July, and January nights fall to about 32°F. Expect around 106 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 12 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Odessa?

    The average one-way commute is 22 min, 16% lower than the Texas average. About 3.4% of workers work from home.
