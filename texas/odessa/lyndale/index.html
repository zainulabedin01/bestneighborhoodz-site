What it's like to live in Lyndale

Lyndale is a neighborhood in Odessa with about 2,300 residents, older homes (median year built 1959), mostly owner-occupied (78%) and 56% of households with children.

Our grade for Lyndale is B-. That puts it 17th of 66 areas we grade in Odessa, a city that gets a D+ overall.

Questions about Lyndale

Is Lyndale a good place to live?
