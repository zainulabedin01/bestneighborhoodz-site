Is Brownsville a good place to live? Common questions

Is Brownsville, TX a good place to live?

Brownsville gets a D overall, which puts it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 695th of 876 nationally and 56th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and getting around, and its weakest is internet access.

How much does it cost to live in Brownsville?

Renters in Brownsville pay a median of $923 a month, 34% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $139,900, 51% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 2.7x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $962, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Brownsville?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Brownsville are Southwest Brownsville, Valle Hermosa Colonia and South Brownsville. We score 15 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Brownsville?

The average one-way commute is 21 min, 20% lower than the Texas average. About 8.4% of workers work from home.
