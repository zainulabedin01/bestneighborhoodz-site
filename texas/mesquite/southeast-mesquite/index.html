What it's like to live in Southeast Mesquite

Southeast Mesquite is an area in Mesquite with about 20,600 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2001), mostly owner-occupied (84%) and 55% of households with children.

It grades D- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 7th of 8 areas in Mesquite, which grades D- overall.

Pros of living in Southeast Mesquite

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $115,900, 60% higher than the Mesquite median.

Cons of living in Southeast Mesquite

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $2,480, 64% higher than the Mesquite median.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Longer commutes. The average commute is 37 minutes, against 32 for Mesquite as a whole.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Southeast Mesquite

Is Southeast Mesquite a good place to live?

Southeast Mesquite covers part of Mesquite, TX, home to about 20,600 people. It gets a D-, in the bottom 16% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 7th of 8 areas we score in Mesquite. It does best on internet access and worst on getting around.

How much does it cost to live in Southeast Mesquite?

Median rent in Southeast Mesquite is $2,480 a month, 64% higher than the Mesquite median. The median home is worth $350,000, 42% higher than the Mesquite median. A median home costs 3.0x the median household income, compared with 3.4x across Mesquite.

What schools serve Southeast Mesquite?

The closest rated public schools are Hollis T Dietz Elementary (elementary, rated F), Crandall Middle (middle, rated D) and Forney High School (high, rated B). School zones can differ from the closest school, so check with the district.
