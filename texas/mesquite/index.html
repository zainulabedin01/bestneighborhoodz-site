About Mesquite

Just east of Dallas, Mesquite sits mostly in Dallas County with a piece reaching into Kaufman County. It started on March 14, 1873, as a stop on the Texas and Pacific Railway line running from Dallas to Shreveport, and it was incorporated in 1887. Today it is known as the Rodeo Capital of Texas, a title it owes to the Mesquite Championship Rodeo, which has run since the 1940s.

Mesquite sits where four major roads meet: Interstate 30, Interstate 635, Interstate 20 and US 80. That puts downtown Dallas, Lake Ray Hubbard and DFW Airport within easy reach. Big local employers include Mesquite ISD, Town East Mall, UPS, Canadian Solar and the City of Mesquite. On summer Saturdays from June through August, the rodeo runs at Mesquite Arena near I-635 and Scyene Road. Town East Mall has been the main shopping spot since 1971, and the Heritage Trail system runs through the city park network.

Sources: en.wikipedia.org, en.wikipedia.org, visitmesquitetx.com

What it's like to live in Mesquite

Mesquite sits about 11 miles from downtown Dallas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro. 150,693 people lived here in 2025, up 0.3% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Westlake Park, Westover Park and Valley Creek Park.

The median age is 32.9, 39% of households have children, 63% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $72,537 a year (8% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Mesquite

Mesquite renters pay a median of $1,513 a month, 8% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $61,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 61% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $245,700, 13% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,345 before tax, or $1,666 with property tax. That is $153 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Mesquite pays $3,859 a year, 9% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Mesquite safe?

In 2025, Mesquite Police Department reported 680 violent crimes and 3,350 property crimes. That works out to 453 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 17% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 2,231 property crimes per 100,000, 7% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 14% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Schools in Mesquite

Mesquite has 49 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 49% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Mesquite ISD and Dallas ISD.

Top-rated campuses include Vroonland - Vanguard High School, Motley Elementary and Larry G Smith Elementary. The average rating score across all campuses is 79 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.
