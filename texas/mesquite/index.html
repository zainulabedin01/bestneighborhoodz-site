Is Mesquite a good place to live? Common questions

Is Mesquite, TX a good place to live?

Mesquite earns a D- on our index, in the bottom 16% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 762nd of 876 nationally and 67th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are internet access and home ownership, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in Mesquite?

Renters in Mesquite pay a median of $1,513 a month, 8% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $245,700, 13% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 3.4x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $1,666, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in Mesquite?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Mesquite are East Mesquite, South Mesquite and Southeast Mesquite. We score 8 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in Mesquite?

The average one-way commute is 32 min, 21% higher than the Texas average. About 10.0% of workers work from home.
