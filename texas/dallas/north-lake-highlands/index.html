What it's like to live in North Lake Highlands

North Lake Highlands is a neighborhood in Dallas with about 36,700 residents, homes built around 1982, mostly rented (82% of homes) and 30% of households with children.

It grades F against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 53rd of 53 areas in Dallas, which grades C- overall.

Pros of living in North Lake Highlands

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,280, 13% lower than the Dallas median.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.4 miles away.

Cons of living in North Lake Highlands

  • Higher poverty. 25% of residents live below the poverty line, against 17% across Dallas.
  • Higher unemployment. 8.7% of the labor force is out of work, against 4.9% across Dallas.
  • Lower-rated schools nearby. Only 1 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about North Lake Highlands

Is North Lake Highlands a good place to live?
