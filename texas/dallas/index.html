What it's like to live in Dallas

Dallas is the anchor city of the Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro area. 1,329,491 people lived here in 2025, up 1.9% from 2020.

Local spots people know include White Rock Lake Park, White Rock Lake and Kiest Park.

The median age is 33.4, 28% of households have children, 42% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $70,518 a year (10% lower than the Texas median).

Cost of living in Dallas

Dallas renters pay a median of $1,472 a month, 5% higher than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $59,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $1,536 for a one-bedroom, $1,807 for a two-bedroom and $2,275 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $320,700, 13% higher than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $1,755 before tax, or $2,178 with property tax. That is $706 a month more than the median rent, so renting is the cheaper monthly choice unless you plan to stay for years.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Dallas pays $5,075 a year, 20% higher than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Is Dallas safe?

In 2025, Dallas Police Department reported 7,674 violent crimes and 40,962 property crimes. That works out to 577 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 49% higher than the Texas rate of 387, and 3,077 property crimes per 100,000, 48% higher than the state rate.

Violent crime is down about 30% since 2021, based on the yearly rates below.

Crime rates describe the whole area a police department covers. Some streets are much quieter than the average and some are busier, so look at the neighborhood grades and visit at different times of day.

Schools in Dallas

Dallas has 322 public school campuses with a 2025 state rating inside the city, and 63% of them earn an A or B. Most students here are served by Dallas ISD.

Top-rated campuses include School for the Talented and Gifted, Dr Wright L Lassiter Jr Early College High School and Irma Rangel Young Women's Leadership School. The average rating score across all campuses is 82 out of 100, against a Texas median of 81.

Texas districts usually assign students by attendance zone rather than the closest campus, so check the zone for a specific address with the district before you sign a lease or buy.

    Living in Dallas: common questions

    Is Dallas, TX a good place to live?

    Dallas gets an overall grade of C- and ranks 54th of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. It does best on errands and things to do and cost of living, and trails on safety and home ownership.

    How much does it cost to live in Dallas?

    Renters in Dallas pay a median of $1,472 a month, 5% higher than the Texas median. The median home is worth $320,700, 13% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 4.5x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,178, including property tax.

    Is Dallas safe?

    In 2025, Dallas Police Department reported 577 violent crimes per 100,000 people, 49% higher than the Texas rate. Property crime was 3,077 per 100,000, 48% higher than the Texas rate.

    Are the schools good in Dallas?

    We found 322 rated public school campuses inside Dallas. 63% are rated A or B by the Texas Education Agency. Its schools score is 50 of 100 compared with other Texas cities of 50,000+ people.

    What are the best neighborhoods in Dallas?

    Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in Dallas are West End Historic District, Cypress Waters and Roseland Commmunity. We score 53 areas in the city.

    Does Dallas flood?

    FEMA rates river flooding risk in Dallas as low, and coastal flooding as not rated. Hazards rated high or very high here: tornadoes, heat waves and hail. Always check the flood map for a specific address before you buy.

    What is the weather like in Dallas?

    Summers in Dallas peak at about 97°F in July, and January nights fall to about 38°F. Expect around 108 days a year at 90°F or hotter, and 38 inches of rain.

    How long is the commute in Dallas?

    The average one-way commute is 26 min, 3% lower than the Texas average. About 16% of workers work from home.
