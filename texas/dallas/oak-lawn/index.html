What it's like to live in Oak Lawn

Oak Lawn is a neighborhood in Dallas with about 21,500 residents, homes built around 1996, a mix of owners and renters and 5% of households with children.

It grades A- against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 12th of 53 areas in Dallas, which grades C- overall.

Pros of living in Oak Lawn

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $108,100, 53% higher than the Dallas median.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.3 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Oak Lawn

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,820, 24% higher than the Dallas median.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Oak Lawn

Is Oak Lawn a good place to live?

Oak Lawn is a neighborhood in Dallas, TX, home to about 21,500 people. It gets an A-, in the top 20% of Texas neighborhoods, and ranks 12th of 53 areas we score in Dallas. It does best on errands and things to do and worst on home ownership.
