What it's like to live in Old East Dallas

Old East Dallas is a neighborhood in Dallas with about 8,500 residents, homes built around 1975, mostly rented (85% of homes) and 11% of households with children.

Our grade for Old East Dallas is C+. That puts it 33rd of 53 areas we grade in Dallas, a city that gets a C- overall.

Pros of living in Old East Dallas

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,290, 12% lower than the Dallas median.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.2 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Old East Dallas

  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.

Questions about Old East Dallas

Is Old East Dallas a good place to live?
