What it's like to live in Giants of the Savana

Giants of the Savana is a neighborhood in Dallas with about 3,600 residents, older homes (median year built 1960), mostly owner-occupied (70%) and 20% of households with children.

Our grade for Giants of the Savana is C. That puts it 34th of 53 areas we grade in Dallas, a city that gets a C- overall.

Pros of living in Giants of the Savana

  • Cheaper rent than the city. Median rent is $1,240, 16% lower than the Dallas median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.
  • Groceries close by. The nearest grocery store is about 0.5 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Giants of the Savana

  • Higher poverty. 35% of residents live below the poverty line, against 17% across Dallas.
  • Higher unemployment. 16% of the labor force is out of work, against 4.9% across Dallas.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of heat waves and hail as very high in this part of town.
