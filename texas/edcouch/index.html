What it's like to live in Edcouch

Edcouch sits about 18 miles from downtown McAllen, in the McAllen-Edinburg-Mission metro. 2,650 people lived here in 2025, down 4.7% from 2020. It is suburban.

Local spots people know include Edcouch Municipal Park and Bell Park.

The median age is 37.4, 27% of households have children, 50% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $21,843 a year (72% lower than the Texas median).

  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 20 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cost of living in Edcouch

Edcouch renters pay a median of $552 a month, 61% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $22,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 52% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $806 for a one-bedroom, $1,017 for a two-bedroom and $1,305 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $58,500, 79% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $320 before tax, or $403 with property tax. That is close to the median rent, so buying can make sense if you plan to stay.

Texas has no state income tax, but it leans on property taxes instead, though they are lighter here than in much of the state. The median homeowner in Edcouch pays $998 a year, 76% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.
