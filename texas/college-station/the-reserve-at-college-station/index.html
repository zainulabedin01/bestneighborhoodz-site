What it's like to live in The Reserve at College Station

The Reserve at College Station is a neighborhood in College Station with about 700 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2008), mostly owner-occupied (94%) and 21% of households with children.

Our grade for The Reserve at College Station is B-. That puts it 12th of 39 areas we grade in College Station, a city that gets a C overall.

Pros of living in The Reserve at College Station

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $82,300, 62% higher than the College Station median.
  • Well-rated schools nearby. 3 of the 3 closest public schools are rated A or B.

Cons of living in The Reserve at College Station

  • Pricier rent than the city. Median rent is $1,520, 27% higher than the College Station median.
  • Groceries are a drive. The nearest grocery store is about 2.9 miles from the middle of the neighborhood.
