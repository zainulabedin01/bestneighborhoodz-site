Is College Station a good place to live? Common questions

Is College Station, TX a good place to live?

College Station gets a D overall, which puts it in the bottom 24% of US cities of 50,000+ people. It ranks 707th of 876 nationally and 61st of 75 Texas cities of 50,000+ people. Its strongest areas are getting around and education levels, and its weakest is internet access.

How much does it cost to live in College Station?

Median rent in College Station is $1,194 a month, 15% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $346,700, 22% higher than the Texas median. A median home costs 6.8x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $2,375, including property tax.

What are the best neighborhoods in College Station?

Based on our scores, the top-rated areas in College Station are Stone Forest, Castle Rock and Castlegate II. We score 39 areas in the city.

How long is the commute in College Station?

The average one-way commute is 17 min, 35% lower than the Texas average. About 12% of workers work from home.
