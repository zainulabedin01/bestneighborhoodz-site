What it's like to live in Southern Trace

Southern Trace is a neighborhood in College Station with about 1,600 residents, mostly newer homes (median year built 2002), a mix of owners and renters and 34% of households with children.

It grades B against every neighborhood in Texas and ranks 10th of 39 areas in College Station, which grades C overall.

Pros of living in Southern Trace

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $95,000, 87% higher than the College Station median.
  • Parks within walking distance. The closest park is about 0.2 miles away.

Cons of living in Southern Trace
