What it's like to live in Stone Forest

Stone Forest is a neighborhood in College Station with about 2,500 residents, homes built around 1999, mostly owner-occupied (98%) and 40% of households with children.

Our grade for Stone Forest is A. That puts it 3rd of 39 areas we grade in College Station, a city that gets a C overall.

Pros of living in Stone Forest

  • Higher incomes. The median household earns $182,900, 259% higher than the College Station median.

Cons of living in Stone Forest

Questions about Stone Forest

Is Stone Forest a good place to live?
