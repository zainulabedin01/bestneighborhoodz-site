What it's like to live in Anthony

Anthony sits about 14 miles from downtown El Paso, in the El Paso metro. 4,539 people lived here in 2025, up 0.2% from 2020. It is spread out, with mostly newer homes (the median home was built in 2001).

Local spots people know include Ernie Rascon Memorial Park, Los Torres Altos Park and Anthony ISD Libraries.

The median age is 32.4, 55% of households have children, 74% of homes are owned by the people living in them and the typical household earns $54,588 a year (30% lower than the Texas median).

Pros of living in Anthony

  • Strong public schools. 67% of the 3 rated campuses here earn an A or B from the Texas Education Agency.
  • Low violent crime. 154 violent crimes per 100,000 people in 2025, 60% lower than the Texas rate.
  • Lower rent. Median rent is $1,066, 24% lower than the Texas median.
  • Short commutes. The average trip to work takes 22 minutes, against 27 across Texas.

Cons of living in Anthony

  • Higher unemployment. 8.1% of the labor force is out of work, against 5.3% statewide.
  • Many adults uninsured. 34% of adults 18 to 64 have no health insurance, against 20% statewide.
  • Natural hazard risk. FEMA rates the risk of hail and winter storms as very high here.
  • Long, hot summers. Expect about 114 days a year at 90°F or hotter.

Cost of living in Anthony

Anthony renters pay a median of $1,066 a month, 24% lower than the statewide median. The usual rule is to spend no more than 30% of income on rent, which means earning around $43,000 a year for a median unit. Right now 42% of renters here pay more than 30% of their income on rent.

HUD sets fair market rents for this area at $969 for a one-bedroom, $1,146 for a two-bedroom and $1,559 for a three-bedroom (fiscal year 2027). These figures include utilities and describe a modest unit, so newer apartments often ask for more.

The median home is worth $168,200, 41% lower than the Texas median. With 20% down and a 30-year loan at 7.28%, the monthly payment comes to about $921 before tax, or $1,210 with property tax. That is $144 a month more than the median rent, a gap that can be worth it if you plan to stay a while.

Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes are among the highest in the country. The median homeowner in Anthony pays $3,472 a year, 18% lower than the Texas median of $4,232. Budget for it, along with homeowners insurance, which our numbers do not include.

Daily life in Anthony

Everyday places mapped inside Anthony, from Overture Maps. Well-known spots include Ernie Rascon Memorial Park, Los Torres Altos Park, Anthony ISD Libraries.

The average one-way commute is 22 minutes, 16% shorter than the Texas average. 8% of workers work from home. Fewer than 3% walk, bike or take transit to work, so plan on driving for almost everything.

Downtown El Paso is about 14 miles away in a straight line. Many people who live in Anthony work elsewhere in the metro, so test the drive at rush hour before you commit.
