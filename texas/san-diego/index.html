Is San Diego a good place to live? Common questions

Is San Diego, TX a good place to live?

San Diego earns an F on our index, in the bottom 8% of US towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. It ranks 11265th of 11,459 nationally and 729th of 745 Texas towns of 1,000 to 10,000 people. Its strongest areas are cost of living and home ownership, and its weakest is health.

How much does it cost to live in San Diego?

Renters in San Diego pay a median of $1,096 a month, 22% lower than the Texas median. The median home is worth $85,600, 70% lower than the Texas median. A median home costs 1.6x the median household income, compared with 3.6x across Texas. With 20% down at a 7.28% rate, the monthly payment on a median home is about $574, including property tax.

How long is the commute in San Diego?

The average one-way commute is 32 min, 20% higher than the Texas average. About 10% of workers work from home.
